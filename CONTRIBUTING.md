# Contributing and review

This repository is an inspectable public working corpus. Contributions are welcome when they preserve its evidence, attribution and maturity boundaries.

## Useful contribution types

- corrections supported by a primary or authoritative source;
- architecture mappings that identify real inputs, outputs and ownership boundaries;
- comparator implementations or evidence that challenge an EA differential hypothesis;
- reproducible fixtures, traces, negative controls and burden measurements;
- failure cases, including negative or narrowing results;
- accessibility, navigation and citation improvements that do not remove provenance.

## Contribution route

1. Open an issue describing the proposed change, the affected document and the evidence supporting it, or submit a focused pull request when the change is already bounded.
2. Keep general architecture, programme-specific application material and historical/provenance records distinct.
3. Do not delete or silently rewrite frozen, historical or superseded artefacts. Add a successor, annex or explicit correction record when preservation is required.
4. State whether the contribution is conceptual, implemented, executed, independently reviewed or externally adopted. Do not collapse these statuses.
5. Preserve source attribution, licence notices, commit references and any declared residual uncertainty.

## Benchmark contributions

Benchmark submissions should identify the comparator arm, resources, authority, time horizon, evidence access, implementation burden and falsifier being tested. Conventional mechanisms that reproduce a candidate EA behaviour with equal or lower burden count against differentiation and should be reported as such.

## Review and publication boundary

A merged contribution records inclusion in this public working corpus. It does not by itself establish validation, endorsement, standards adoption, institutional approval or production readiness. Programme-specific contributions remain subject to the owner and procedural rules of the relevant programme.
