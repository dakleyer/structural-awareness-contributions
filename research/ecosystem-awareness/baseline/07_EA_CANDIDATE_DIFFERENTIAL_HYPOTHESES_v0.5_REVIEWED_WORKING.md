## 1. Reviewed conclusion — a real candidate, not an established win

Ecosystem Awareness (EA) is a **coherent candidate architectural layer for decision-scoped epistemic qualification under finite capacity and ecosystem change**. Its proposed differential is not uncertainty propagation, context retrieval, agent coordination, guardrails, attestation or human review in isolation. It is the operation-level combination of:

- qualified decision windows with explicit potentially knowable outside-window state and irreducible/structural residual;
- scope-indexed, non-fungible composition of local and external determinations, including source dependence and mismatched epistemic postures across domains;
- separate epistemic condition and operating posture, so uncertainty is neither laundered into certainty nor automatically equated with a system-wide hold; and
- risk/capacity/response-horizon requalification that can seek **more, different or less** evidence and can re-enter the supporting frame after an observed effect.

That combination is plausibly differential as an explicit architectural responsibility and interface contract. It could produce fewer unsupported systemic conclusions, more targeted requalification, preservation of warranted operation, lower low-value evidence/review burden and clearer reasons for action or abstention. These are benefit hypotheses, not observed outcomes. A peer that reproduces the same scoped behaviour at comparable burden narrows the distinctiveness claim; a favourable EA result in one case does not establish universal superiority or mathematical novelty.

## 2. Neighbouring architectures and what EA must not claim

| Neighbour | Strong function already present | EA question left to test; not an absence assertion |
|---|---|---|
| [A2A protocol](https://a2a-protocol.org/dev/specification/) | Discovery, capability/task/message/artifact interchange, transport and extensibility | Can a realistic extension preserve qualified proposition, domain/window, inherited residual and requalification semantics end to end with no EA-equivalent profile? EA should use transport, not claim to invent exchange. |
| [MCP architecture](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-06-18/architecture) | Host–client–server boundaries and controlled tool/resource/context access | Can an MCP host's source-selection/context policy support a decision-relative sufficiency and residual claim under changing mission risk and capacity? Resource access is not proof of epistemic sufficiency. |
| Agent runtimes/SDKs | State, tools, checkpoints, routing, guardrails, interrupts, HITL and traces | Can a strong orchestration policy reproduce selective frame requalification and non-fungible domain composition at comparable complexity/attention cost? These are possible EA substrates, not deficient competitors. |
| [RATS / RFC 9334](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html) and trust frameworks | Evidence, appraisal result and relying-party decision are separated; authority and trust claims have specific owners | Does EA add decision-wide qualification of evidence outside attestation without taking identity, authorization, appraisal or enforcement ownership? |
| [DebUnc](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1265/) and uncertainty-propagation work | Uncertainty can be communicated and propagated through agents | Does EA's window qualification, structural residual, source-lineage and domain non-substitution reduce false closure beyond a calibrated-UQ peer? |

## 3. The EA differential to test: four coupled claims

The v0.4 benchmark's A–G list is conserved. The claims below are renamed `EA-H1`–`EA-H4` to avoid collision with the universal candidate hypotheses and the foundational H1–H6.

| EA-specific hypothesis | Conserved v0.4 elements and EA functions | Plausible positive effect | Decisive falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EA-H1 — scoped non-fungible determination** | A/B/C/D; F3/F4/F5/F6/F8. Preserve four-position in/out-window state, source dependence and domain-specific residual; do not average unrelated claims into a global verdict. | Fewer apparently supported decisions created by duplicated evidence, cross-domain compensation or local-to-global promotion; clearer affected-domain correction. | A strong peer reaches the same decisions and explanatory trace without an equivalent composition layer or extra burden. |
| **EA-H2 — proportionate window requalification** | E and APQ consequence; F1/F2/F7/F9. Qualify `W(d,t)` to consequence, sensitivity, reversibility, residual tolerance, acquisition burden, effective capacity and remaining response time. | Fewer stale-frame commitments at high exposure and/or less low-value acquisition/review at low exposure, without losing useful response time. | A fixed broad/narrow or strong value-of-information/source-lineage peer reaches an equal or better decision/resource frontier on matched facts and budgets. |
| **EA-H3 — orthogonal assessment and posture** | F; F5/F6/F7/F8. Keep epistemic management condition and residual distinct from Normal/Containment/Migration preparation and independent actuation authority. | Preserve warranted normal operation under bounded residual; avoid both premature blanket holds and unjustified continuation after a material frame change. | A peer separates the conditions with equal correctness, timeliness and burden; or EA adds indecision without reducing either error. |
| **EA-H4 — interoperable re-entry** | G; F4/F8/F9 with F1/F2/F5/F6. Carry scope, window, inherited uncertainty, dependency, capacity and validity through transport-neutral handoff; reopen only failed assumptions. | Less downstream loss of qualification and fewer indiscriminate reruns/escalations; more traceable return-to-operation decisions. | A2A/MCP/attestation/UQ/tracing composition reproduces the handoff and targeted re-entry with no extra semantics or materially simpler implementation. |

The highest-value combined differential test is `EA-H1 + EA-H2`: avoiding false systemic closure matters only if the candidate can decide what additional evidence is worthwhile before the response window closes. `EA-H3` and `EA-H4` test whether that gain can be operated and passed across interfaces.

## 4. How the EA hypotheses are tested

First apply the shared test contract and `C-H1`–`C-H5` KPIs in [00C](./00C_FALSIFIABLE_CANDIDATE_SOLUTION_HYPOTHESES_AND_KPIS.md). Then compare EA with a strong peer on the same frozen facts, evidence, authority, deadline and resource ledger. Observe the relevant universal measures—false continuation/containment, posture correctness, deadline pass, handoff integrity, observable outcome and total burden—plus EA-specific measures such as false systemic closure, domain attribution, structural-residual preservation and targeted re-entry precision.

An EA differential is supported only where it improves the declared error–resource frontier, or preserves the same result with a simpler accountable semantic composition. It is not supported when the apparent gain comes from extra evidence, authority, reviewer time, deadline or unrecorded peer weakness. The comparator arms, case branches, industry sources and negative-result taxonomy are maintained in the separate [market and industry benchmark](./00D_MARKET_AND_INDUSTRY_BENCHMARK_RESEARCH_v0.1.md).

## 5. Claim ladder

1. **Now — architecture proposition:** EA offers an explicit way to qualify decision-relevant epistemic state, compose non-fungible scoped evidence and requalify a changing frame under finite capacity.
2. **After profile tests:** report which named branches change decisions, residual representation, delays and resource consumption relative to a peer. A pass applies only to the tested domain, scope, configuration and assumptions.
3. **After independent replication/interoperability tests:** only reproducible gains across teams and transports justify a stronger architecture-role claim.
4. **Novelty, standards and production claims:** need their own searches, formal processes and deployed evidence. None follows from a case pass or FG-TIDA discussion.

Change F1–F9 or the interface architecture only when tests expose a missing responsibility, unnecessary function, peer-equivalent simpler construction, or an unacceptable cost/latency trade-off. Until then, the frozen v0.4 baseline remains the controlled reference.
