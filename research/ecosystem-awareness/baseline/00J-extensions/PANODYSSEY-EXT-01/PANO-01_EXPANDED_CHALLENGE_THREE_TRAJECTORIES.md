# THREE BOUNDED TECHNICAL TRAJECTORIES — PROPOSED, NOT FROZEN

UC1: compare previous/current permission records → identify material scope/time/version change → specify how the affected receiver learns it → requalify only affected future reliance → specify retention of authority history and any separately evidenced later intervention history. A separate intervention-history capability is a prospective requirement, not an established Panodyssey capability. Required documentary material: attributable grant and changed state, version/time, receiver/decision scope, freshness policy, update/revalidation evidence requirements and admissible fallback. Unknown receipt or downstream action stays unknown.

UC2: observed actor identifier → source/issuer → represented organisation/principal → role and mandate scope → evidence sufficiency for the requested action. Required material: issuer/subject binding, assurance and limitations, current mandate/status, declared receiver policy, rejection/hold and valid-actor continuity conditions. A user-agent string, KYC of the publisher, or a valid signature alone does not prove agent mandate.

UC3: declaration → reception → interpretation → specified execution evidence → bounded conclusion/revalidation, as detailed in 03. Do not infer use or non-use from crawler detection or the Notice's availability.

These are the three trajectories of the single Panodyssey Challenge, one per existing specific UC. Cross-trajectory dependencies are recorded without introducing a fourth route. Technical trajectories use declared authority inputs. Legal applicability remains in the reference layer, not in new technical gates.

Development status: T-UC1 and T-UC2 are specified here to chain/material level; their detailed positive, falsifying and valid-continuity gate contracts remain PENDING REVIEW. T-UC3 has the proposed E1–E5 contract in Doc 03. All three documentary specification/conclusion decision owners remain UNASSIGNED / OPEN, as expressly allowed by Iván.
