# LOCAL DOCUMENTARY OUTCOME CONTRACT — v0.5 — 25 October 2026 — PROPOSED / NOT FROZEN — INTERNAL / NOT FOR EXTERNAL CIRCULATION

The following PASS/FAIL definitions apply at criterion level. Trajectory acceptance and package aggregation are governed by the existing “Assessment levels and aggregation” section; no criterion or trajectory result is issued before freeze and assessment.

PASS: the criterion's frozen positive requirement is met by admissible evidence and its applicable falsifier/continuity conditions are satisfied.

FAIL: a demonstrable contradiction or failure of the assessed frozen criterion.

PARTIAL: progress within specification or coverage; it is not an acceptance outcome.

NOT ESTABLISHED: insufficient evidence to decide.

NOT APPLICABLE: a criterion is demonstrably outside declared scope, with reason and reviewer approval; never substitute for missing, inaccessible or unexercised evidence.

For each local gate capture: proposed ID/version; exact proposition, subject/action/time; requirement; positive and falsifying conditions; continuity control; required source and evidence mode; provenance and confidentiality; applicability; assessor/interest; result/reason; limitations; unresolved questions; decision authority; validity window and revalidation trigger. IDs remain provisional until review/freeze.

Quantitative indicators proposed: mandatory-criteria coverage, traceability completeness, evidence sufficiency, ambiguity count, falsifier coverage, and estimated verification effort. Do not average away mandatory failures.

No product testing, live operation, legal audit, certification or guarantee.

TEST MATRIX STATUS: PENDING REVIEW; no new test identifiers frozen.


# ASSESSMENT LEVELS AND AGGREGATION

This vocabulary is a proposed local documentary reporting convention, not a new DDS-wide outcome standard. Canonical Stage A uses its selected acceptance rule and bounded differential finding.

Criterion PASS concerns one frozen requirement. For trajectory PASS, apply the sole definition under “Assessment levels and aggregation”, including reference comparison. Package-level PASS is currently undefined and blocked. PARTIAL describes progress; NOT ASSESSED is a workflow state, NOT ESTABLISHED an evidential conclusion, and NOT EXERCISED a source execution state. Do not substitute them for each other or infer equivalence to UC21 outcome vocabulary. Local trajectory PASS/FAIL report inside/outside the reviewed Stage A acceptance rule only at the declared scope; NOT ESTABLISHED remains an evidential conclusion. These labels do not automatically translate into UC21 execution statuses.

Before reviewed criteria/source freeze and actual assessment, record NOT ASSESSED / NOT FROZEN. The current new Panodyssey Challenge has that status; it has neither PASS nor FAIL.

One Challenge has three trajectories, T-UC1/T-UC2/T-UC3. Record results separately. No package/global result is issued until its aggregation rule is approved. This pending rule does not prevent drafting the generic Challenge.

Assessment-object register — ALL PENDING before a comparative evaluation: candidate specification name/version/hash and owner; strong conventional/market reference peers and rationale per trajectory; acceptance/fair-comparison rule; assessor competence/interests and decision authority. No candidate or peer is selected by this correction. These are prerequisites for a future result, not evidence that the generic design must be reorganized.

Decision owners: T-UC1 OPEN; T-UC2 OPEN; T-UC3 OPEN, expressly permitted by Iván. Operational receiver authority/mandate is a separate role and must be established for the selected candidate/context; no assignment to Iván, Olena or Alexandre is inferred.

TRAJECTORY PASS — sole definition: after the approved source/criteria freeze and actual assessment, the identified candidate/version must satisfy the preregistered acceptance rule for that trajectory, including fair comparison with strong reference peers. The mandatory applicable set must be non-empty and fully satisfied, no mandatory applicability/evidence condition may remain unresolved, and the positive, falsifying and valid-continuity controls must satisfy the reviewed rule. A tie, sufficient conventional control, no differential or inconclusive finding is recorded at its actual scope; it is not promoted to a claimed candidate advantage. Every conclusion retains source/version, scope, assessor and evidence ceiling. The candidate, peers and acceptance rule are still unselected/unfrozen.

A known mandatory criterion failure gives that trajectory FAIL, with missing evidence also reported; positive scores elsewhere cannot cancel it. If no demonstrated failure exists but a mandatory criterion cannot be decided, that trajectory is NOT ESTABLISHED. PARTIAL is a progress/coverage description and never substitutes for final acceptance. Approved N/A exclusions are counted separately and cannot erase missing mandatory evidence.

Separate four states: specification/assessment result; evidence availability; authorised routing disposition; observed execution outcome. HOLD/RESTRICT/REQUEST EVIDENCE can be legitimate decisions under uncertainty but do not prove execution or legal compliance. Preserve conflicting reviewer judgments and later adjudication separately.

Example (illustrative, not an assessment): an attributable Notice exists and the specification explicitly requires receiver-bound receipt before an execution claim. That specification may satisfy a documentary criterion; actual receipt/execution remains NOT ESTABLISHED until admissible observations exist. A spec that infers compliance from the Notice's existence fails the relevant documentary criterion.


# QUANTITATIVE INDICATORS — DEFINITIONS, NOT MEASURED RESULTS

Let M be the versioned list of mandatory applicable criteria after justified N/A exclusions. Let T be the versioned list of criteria requiring traceability; E the required mandatory evidence-slot list; and C the criteria requiring positive/falsifying/continuity conditions. Freeze these lists and their membership/counting rules before calculating ratios. Unknown applicability blocks final PASS. An empty denominator is undefined, not 100%.

Mandatory satisfaction = count(M with documented PASS) / count(M), reported as n/N with integer counts and alongside counts FAIL, NOT ESTABLISHED and NOT ASSESSED. No weighted average overrides a mandatory failure.

Traceability completeness = members of T with source edition/locator, exact supported proposition and rationale / all members of T, including unassessed records. This measures record completeness, not truth or legal conformity.

Evidence sufficiency = members of E judged sufficient under the frozen appraisal policy / all members of E. Preserve missing, inaccessible/withheld, stale, conflicting and not-collected categories separately.

Falsifier/continuity specification coverage = members of C with reviewed positive, negative and valid-continuity conditions / all members of C. Documentary coverage is not a count of tests executed.

Ambiguity backlog = unresolved material questions, deduplicated by issue ID with owner/status and affected claim; report closures and new items separately.

Review burden = recorded assessor minutes, information requests and expected verification effort in stated units; estimates are labelled estimates. No quantitative values or measured advantage are claimed in this package.

For every metric state numerator, denominator, population, version/window, counting rule, uncertainty and evidence mode. Do not convert unavailable evidence to zero; do not claim a calibrated score from an unreviewed ratio.

Always report ratio n/N and the underlying count/status lists. These are finite documentary checklist counts unless a sampling design is explicitly adopted. Missing, stale, withheld, conflicting and uncollected records are distinct uncertainty categories. Statistical intervals require a justified population, sampling model and method; no universal ban at N<30 is adopted. Counting definitions and thresholds remain PENDING REVIEW.

DISAGREEMENT RESOLUTION — OPERATIONAL PROCEDURE (PROSPECTIVE; NOT YET EXERCISED)

ADJ-01 Pre-register who may evaluate, inspect evidence, adjudicate and authorise the resulting documentary statement. Source owners may correct production facts; they do not automatically arbitrate the scientific score. A reviewer with a candidate/reference/oracle interest must declare it and cannot quietly become independent adjudicator.

ADJ-02 Independent first-pass appraisals: each eligible reviewer receives the same frozen source set/criterion/evidence ceiling and submits signed or attributable per-criterion PASS/FAIL/NOT ESTABLISHED/N/A with reason, missing evidence and conflicts. An evaluator can also mark a proposed criterion as not decidable at its evidence mode.

ADJ-03 Preserve both records verbatim. Classify divergence by source authenticity, actor/authority semantics, criterion interpretation, applicability/exclusion, evidence sufficiency, effect observation, comparator parity or acceptance threshold. Record DR-ID, affected T-UC/E/S item, owners and disputed evidence. Never overwrite dissent with a consensus summary.

ADJ-04 Clarification round: ask the source owner for only the disputed source fact; request missing receiver evidence separately; use the existing frozen criterion for all reviews. A post-hoc change of its definition, threshold or comparator is a new successor version, not a way to manufacture agreement.

ADJ-05 Adjudication: an eligible, prospectively named person/committee can choose a reasoned supported disposition, return for evidence, or preserve unresolved dissent. The final record links individual positions and exact applied rule. An unreviewed majority vote is not proof of independence or truth.

ADJ-06 Outcome rule: unresolved MATERIAL disagreement affecting a mandatory criterion means trajectory NOT ESTABLISHED unless an approved predeclared tie/adjudication rule already resolves it on admissible evidence. A demonstrated mandatory contradiction remains FAIL. Neither outcome is converted to N/A for convenience.

ADJ-07 Correction and appeal: a participant may challenge source version, arithmetic, conflict declaration or observable fact within a declared window; the challenge and response are logged. Correct factual clerical error with an erratum; a changed criterion, reference or newly supplied substantive evidence requires a prospectively frozen successor assessment. Preserve old conclusions and correction linkage.

ADJ-08 Transparency: the bounded report states participant interests, the unresolved/overruled position, evidence gaps and assessor independence level. If access restrictions prevent independent checking, say so; do not call the result independently reproduced.

NEW DEPENDENCY / MATERIAL CHANGE — REEVALUATION TRIGGER AND IMPACT RULE

DEP-01 Detect: register an unrepresented actor, provider, principal, permission history, national/use regime, resolver, training/RAG/indexing channel, downstream receiver or previously unavailable observation; give it a stable DEP-ID, time first known, source and disclosure class. A change can affect interpretation without proving a historical error.

DEP-02 Contain claims: identify precisely which frozen assumptions/receivers/gates depend on DEP-ID. Where a mandatory condition or claimed generality is affected, mark the corresponding CURRENT conclusion SUSPENDED/PENDING REQUALIFICATION; do not retroactively call the earlier bounded evidence false or delete it. Unaffected parts stay intact if the scope split is defensible.

DEP-03 Impact crosswalk: DEP-ID → originating source fact/version → S dimension → T-UC trajectory → local checkpoint (E1–E5 only for UC3) → criterion → comparator/evaluator/decision → published claim. Mark DIRECT, POSSIBLE or NO OBSERVED DEPENDENCE, supported by citations. For example, newly active RAG reception may affect E2/E3/E4/E5 and T-UC1 permission scope, not automatically UC2 identity.

DEP-04 Requalify: recheck observation sufficiency, current authority/mandate, legitimate continuity, disclosure permission, peer comparability and whether the change invalidates an existing positive, negative or N/A assumption. A receiver can HOLD/RESTRICT/REQUEST EVIDENCE only within its mandate; that disposition is not a retrospective legal verdict.

DEP-05 New version: if scoring criteria, required evidence, case scope or reference peer must change, obtain the appropriate source/assessment-owner decisions, freeze a successor dataset/rule and re-assess only the affected propositions. Archive original hashes, individual reviewer judgments, dissent and outcomes. No retroactive PASS upgrade.

DEP-06 Publish bounded delta: record what remains supported, what is withdrawn or narrowed, what is newly unassessed and why; require separate release permission if material is external. Legal-trigger unknowns remain outside this technical finding and are neither a compulsory legal-review workstream nor an automatic technical FAIL/PASS.
