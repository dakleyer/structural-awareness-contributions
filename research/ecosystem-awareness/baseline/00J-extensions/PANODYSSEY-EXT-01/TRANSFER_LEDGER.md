### Formal eligibility / stop

`Transfer(E_b,p,h,θ)` may be provisionally accepted only if: (i) the base outcome `E_b` actually exists and has auditable evidence; (ii) typed partial projection `h` preserves the *property under test* `p` and its failure/success reflection; (iii) `θ`, horizon, comparator, cost and receiver observation have the required equivalence/bound; and (iv) the added `Δ/κ` mechanisms do not alter the result, or the affected coupling checks have passed.

**Stop/mark NOT ESTABLISHED** if any of these are missing. A25 X1–X7 and A26 Y1–Y7 remain separately required for any corresponding failure/success family-admission claim. R01 criteria E1–E7 are methodological precedents, not a proof that 00J has passed them.
