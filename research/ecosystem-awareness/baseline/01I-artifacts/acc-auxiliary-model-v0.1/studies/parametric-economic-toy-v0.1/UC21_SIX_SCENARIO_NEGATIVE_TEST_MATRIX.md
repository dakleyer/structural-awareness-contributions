# UC21 S1–S6 — economic harm, positive controls and falsifiers (bridge matrix v0.1)

**Research status:** qualitative experimental mapping. Original source cases are frozen; this matrix creates **no new UC21 gate** and does not state that harm, HID or suffering has been measured. The source case's complete gate register and source-specific oracle remain authoritative.

**Common falsifier for a concrete prediction:** real pinned peer + admitted matched scenario/variant + all applicable UC21 gates and positive controls pass + verified legitimate authorized effect + deadline and burden met. An ordinary or top-notch implementation may pass **without** ACC or EA, and such success must remain a valid falsifier for that registered prediction. **R2** (spoken R3) is admitted only after R1 corrected the same registered failure and a credible material changed-context intervention was frozen prospectively. The six narratives are a **family of potential consequences**, not six empirical findings and not a scalar social-damage scale.

**Counterfactual cost/utility discipline:** for each case define relevant human work by GIC/NEO/ACW, persons materially affected, service-equivalent comparison, reversibility, source authority, permissions, resource units and horizon. Then test `Δ production`, `Δ resource cost`, same-service `G^E`, technically attainable `G^T`, legitimately executable `G^G`, `Δ H_ACW` and timing/curvature. The effects may move in opposite directions and must not be collapsed without an explicit validated valuation function. UC21 gates cannot substitute for Study-0 HID task classification and a social model cannot retrospectively rescind a UC21 PASS.
