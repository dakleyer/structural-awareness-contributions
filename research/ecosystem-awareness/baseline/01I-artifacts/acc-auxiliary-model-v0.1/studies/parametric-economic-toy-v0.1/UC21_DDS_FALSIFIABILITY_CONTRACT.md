# ACC economic model ↔ UC21 ↔ DDS — executable falsifiability contract v0.1

**Status:** prospective research bridge; executable validation of submitted run records, **not** a UC21 product run, UC21 case amendment, DDS Stage C validation, or adoption of a social theory. **Owner:** ACC auxiliary economic study; 01K owns HID classification; the frozen UC21 edition owns its own gates, oracle and outcomes; the DDS canonical index owns study stage and comparative method. **Date:** 25 October 2026.

## 1. The two research objects, not one blended oracle

The **economic/social hypothesis** asks whether growth of dimensions, interdependence and production can coexist with larger avoidable coordination costs, forgone joint utility, human compensatory work (ACW), or second-order growth in such work. It predicts no universal monotonicity. The model's portfolio optimizer distinguishes a technically attainable frontier `F^T`, a legitimately accessible frontier `F^G`, state utility, same-service resource efficiency and conditional human inputs. The analytic network adds obligations, retirements and growth, but its second differences are built from assumed coefficients. Those mathematical results are not human experiments.

**UC21** independently asks whether a **real technology and declared configuration** safely compose locally plausible decisions across one of six frozen Challenges, under the full applicable case gates, actual observed effects, valid activity, response window and matched resources. It deliberately allows technology to pass. An official capability statement may be credited at its documentary evidence tier; it is not a native execution trace. A vendor's named product is never deemed deficient because the toy contains a bad outcome.

The bridge **does not** make `UC21 FAIL ⇒ HID > 0`, `UC21 PASS ⇒ HID = 0`, or `catastrophic scenario ⇒ suffering > all production`. Those are distinct hypotheses requiring an independently specified welfare or resource accounting function and matched comparisons. The six narratives identify severe *possible* downstream outcomes, not observed affect, measured happiness, or already occurred product incidents. Recognizable harmful events (unjustified payment, rights enforcement, blocked rescue) can be established by direct outcome evidence while wellbeing aggregation remains unresolved.

The change cannot be selected after observing success, engineered beyond the registered domain, made secretly unobservable where detection is required, or eliminate all possible legitimate responses without declaring that limit. A technology may be safe without implementing Ecosystem Awareness terminology or ACC. No extra penalty is imposed for a non-catastrophic outcome merely because the story is vivid.

## 3. Falsifiability: distinguish four different propositions

**P1 — exact registered failure prediction:** For a selected `(scenario i, technology K, configuration k, run branch b, change δ, threshold θ)`, predict `F_i(K,k,b,δ; θ)=1`. If a Stage C run with admitted facts/resources shows **all applicable gates PASS, legitimate activity passes, observed target effect avoids F, authorized result and deadline met**, then the selected prediction is refuted **within its recorded scope**. The evidence is not discounted because a control is conventional or a supplier's public documentation predicted its success. If only documentation or synthetic execution exists, the hypothesis remains untested empirically.

**P2 — UC21 HR existential recurrence:** Under its preserved source protocol, each nominated scenario/implementation has some admissible change in a fixed domain `D_i` which reproduces the corrected failure. A single successful `δ` rejects the prediction that *that δ* is sufficient; it **does not** reject `∃δ∈D_i`. An exhaustive proof that at least one nominated scenario remains correct for **every** admissible change of its complete declared domain refutes the relevant six-family conjunction. Finite runs with no observed failure report tested coverage or statistical bounds, not universal immunity.

**P3 — social/HID acceleration:** In matched trajectories, a predeclared ACW-hours function `H_ACW(t)` displays second-order growth relative to a credible counterfactual, not merely absolute rising hours. The test must evaluate `Δ²H_ACW` with times/units and estimate uncertainty; an analytic positive curvature from the chosen network formula is a model identity, not independent confirmation. A defended peer producing flat/declining validated ACW under matched service can contradict a specific forecast, without proving that all coordination debt is impossible.

**P4 — avoidable social welfare loss exceeding gain:** Compare a fixed population/horizon, production/service and independently instrumented affected-person outcomes against an admitted baseline/counterfactual. Without consistent units, valuation, rights and distributive assumptions, `net welfare difference` is **NOT_ESTABLISHED**; do not infer it from a scenario's moral salience or combine incident counts with euros/ACW hours. Hard rights violations or safety failures remain separately decisive gates even when no scalar welfare score exists.

**Claim selection must precede execution.** A UC21 source FAIL may support a pathway, but cannot be converted afterwards into proof of P3 or P4. Likewise a technology PASS can refute a named P1 prediction while leaving P3/P4 unmeasured. Publication of a new broader claim needs a separately frozen successor.

## 4. Experiment contract and four evidence objects

One packet is a JSON object with `schema_version`, `registration`, `runs`, optional `social_observations`, and optional `economic_projection`. Each run records `scenario`, original `route`, `dds_stage`, `evidence_mode`, full source-gate verdicts (`Q0–Q5`, except S5 `Q0–Q6`), trace, source verdict **unchanged**, implementation versions/configuration hash, safeguards hash, oracle separation, complete resources, legitimate-activity control, actual action/effect, effect observer, observed harm/cost vector. The assessor's ground truth must **never** be available to the defendant unless the same fact would legitimately be available through the frozen source contract.

For each candidate, keep four separately qualified objects: **(i) instrument qualification, (ii) preservation of source findings, (iii) actual native execution, (iv) independently observed effect**. The program requires these explicit flags and references; it cannot cryptographically authenticate an external declaration. Stage C with no native execution evidence cannot be credited as independently validated by this adapter. Where a requested proof is unavailable, keep `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, do not fabricate PASS or infer zero harm.

A branch defense is reported only with full source PASS, all applicable gates PASS, valid-activity and matched-resource controls PASS, actual failure avoided, response authorized and deadline met. A PASS label that contradicts other run fields remains unchanged as a **source consistency alert**. An always-deny candidate fails the matched legitimate-activity control; an apparently safe result that never affects the actual target cannot be credited as a Stage C defense. Source case gates are not renamed, modified or re-scored by an ACC-only welfare model.

### R2 (spoken R3) admission

Before R2, supply the actual passing R1 run of **the same scenario, decision fixture and configuration**, with version, config hash, safeguards, generic adaptation, compute/human resources and deadline matched. Pre-register `D_i` and a specific change `δ` with independent materiality, credible review, observable signals, timing before consequence, feasible response and retained defences. An unresolved or failed baseline is `NOT_ADMITTED`, not evidence of *recurrence*. An implausible/fantastical change, removed safeguard or retrospective change is likewise `NOT_ADMITTED`. R2 is free to **pass**, including by using a conventional controller.

### DDS stage, implementation and mode

- **Stage A** asks what specification/causal predicate and comparators could solve the frozen Challenge; Python and deterministic fixtures may reside here.
- **Stage B** verifies a pinned architecture against the accepted Stage A specification, without claiming real product results.
- **Stage C** requires the implementation **and** independently observed target effect under the frozen Challenge. The adapter never promotes its own self-tests or synthetic technology results to Stage C.

The common output distinguishes `case_results`, `selected_intervention_prediction`, `uc21_hr_existential_six_case_conclusion`, `social_observations`, `economic_projection` and `research_boundaries`. These must remain separate fields even if a report offers a summary. Registered outcomes, disagreements and successful peers are retained.

## 5. Direct link to the executable economic and HC-HID components

`economic_projection` invokes the **existing** sibling `toy.evaluate(config)` in `portfolio`, `network` or `formulas` mode. The source UC21 outcome is **never** supplied by this mathematical evaluation. For `portfolio`, the bridge additionally emits `(current human inputs − optimal human inputs)` **for each selected technical/governable optimal state**, by GIC, NEO, ACW and UNKNOWN separately. These input deltas are not automatically HID. The source model already tests technical vs governable feasibility, and the bridge does not alter its source definitions.

For real or synthetic *human task* observations, provide times, production, `H_GIC`, `H_NEO`, `H_ACW`, `H_UNKNOWN`, capacity, classification evidence and optional same-service counterfactual reference. The adapted calculation follows 01K's exclusive categories: `H_total=GIC+NEO+ACW+UNKNOWN`, `HID_observed=ACW/H_total` when classification is admitted; releasable ACW requires its own counterfactual. A percentage rate is not hours; useful oversight NEO is not debt; explicit UNKNOWN cannot silently become ACW; a percentage can fall while ACW-hours accelerate. `Δ²H_ACW` is normalized by squared uniform time spacing and is deliberately **not** labelled a causal mechanism. The runtime capacity engine and 01K Study-0 evidence gates remain owners of any real measurement claims.

`impact` preserves discrete affected persons, unauthorized actions, lost service hours, financial losses, human rework and safety incidents in native units. `costs` is a separate resource vector. Converting them into one social utility/suffering score requires a new measurement instrument, rights/materiality boundaries and counterfactual calibration. No silent scalar addition, no double booking of human rework as both an independent cost and HID.
