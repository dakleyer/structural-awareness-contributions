# Independent-review-style self-audit: ACC executable economics + UC21-DDS bridge

**Review status:** code-focused self-audit; independence not claimed. **Scope:** existing `toy.py` / `run.py` / parametric scenarios and new `uc21_dds_bridge.py`; 25 October 2026. The historical pilot and source-case oracle remain unchanged.

## 1. Audit decision

The original three-engine program is an executable **analytical Stage A research model**, not a general social simulator, empirically identified causal model, calibrated HID measurement, UC21 test executor, native technology adapter or DDS Stage C result. Its 34 historical deterministic tests verify internal arithmetic and invariants, but not the empirical plausibility of coefficients or the existence of the selected frontiers beyond enumerated states. Calling it *top-notch social validation* would exceed its evidence.

The new bridge adds a runnable, fail-capable common **registration → case-trace → independent effect → economic/HID projection → scoped conclusion** pipeline. It is suitable as a research-instrument candidate **pending independent scrutiny and real-technology execution**. It does not magically upgrade synthetic cases to vendor claims. In particular no UC21 R2 recurrence or no-recurrence has been reproduced against named live products here.

## 3. Tests executed and what they establish

Execute `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py' -v` to test bridge invariants, source-route mapping, admission and non-admission of R2, mixed native/evidence modes, gate/continuity/deadline failure, independent-harm separation, conditional 01K measures, second-order calculations and negative/positive controls. Existing `test_toy.py` remains the separate 34-test computational baseline. A hypothetical packet illustrates one R1 correction and an R2 **successful defense**, to make sure the experiment is allowed to *falsify* an expected failure.

These are **unit and semantic tests**. They establish neither (a) independence of the assessor, (b) accuracy of any supplier documentation, (c) calibration of a social/human measure, (d) that one of six catastrophic narratives occurred, nor (e) the comparative superiority of Ecosystem Awareness. Every real result needs a source test record and observed target state.

## 4. Nontrivial remaining obligations before a scientific result

A real UC21/DDS campaign must be **prospectively registered**: select one source case edition and its versioned gates; qualify fixture/oracle and independent roles; define bounded specific prediction, material intervention family, strongest comparator and legitimate controls; freeze evidence access, budgets, APIs/config hashes, adaptation, deadlines and outcome measure; instrument source checks and target effects separately; execute baseline, corrected R1 and legitimate R2; preserve successes and contrary findings. For stochastic technology, predeclare repetitions, pairing, error bounds and minimum material effect. For HID/utility forecasts, collect human work classification and counterfactual evidence independently of case acceptance and do not infer distress automatically from an adverse gate.

## 5. Merge gate proposed for PR #28 (research artifact, not deployment claim)

**Eligible for a code/document research merge** when: original economic tests still pass; new semantic tests pass on clean checkout/CI; `python -B uc21_dds_bridge.py examples/uc21_s4_synthetic_defender.json` returns a synthetic *defender success* without native/HR overclaim; relative documentation paths resolve; source snapshot and 01K/UC21 gates remain untouched; a reviewer accepts the explicit Stage C / empirical evidence gap.

**Not eligible for scientific or product-validity claims** until the additional independent native experiment, source/oracle qualification and preregistration obligations are met. No change here authorizes merging, certification or declaring one of six catastrophes inevitable.
