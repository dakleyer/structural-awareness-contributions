# Mathematical lineage and bounded metaprotocol — social → agentic ecosystems

**Independent research dossier · Stage A · v0.1 · 26 October 2026**  
**Intellectual origin:** Iván Abril Palma / The Integral Management Society. Source-based reconstruction and mathematical integration prepared for independent review.  
**Status:** proposed framework; not a new mathematical theorem or validated agentic implementation.  
**Repository scope:** *research only*. No alterations to 00M, 00N, foundational canon, existing PRs or the protected EA README.

> **The question is not “does missing information stay missing?”** It is: **when can an interface adequate for one mission cease to be adequate after an admissible change of operations or context, and what is the least legitimate additional evidence and coordination needed to recover a defensible mission decision?** This claim is independently falsifiable. No agent must be conscious, universally opaque or mathematically undecidable for the question to make sense.

## What this PR contributes

1. **Exact prior-mathematical backbone:** quotient/congruence under an operation signature; finite partition refinement; decision-specific pair separation; typed partial transfers; finite second-order certificates; information/proof-acquisition cost identities. All marked as *standard mathematics or reviewer reconstruction*, with precise lineage.
2. **An integrated agentic research construction:** a bounded, rights-aware **meta-protocol** that receives a new task, detects an operational distinction lost by a previously adequate interface, requests only characterized and eligible further observations, tests a configuration and returns a scoped certificate or uncertainty. This is a candidate architecture and composite research claim, **not an all-knowing oracle**.
3. **A rigorous boundary between the social model and the agentic reduction:** internal compression and partial contracts are plausible in multi-vendor ecosystems, not necessary; “frustration” in agentic systems means a measurable **mission gap** and never subjective suffering; metamorphosis requires already authorized capabilities; solidarity is not agentic-exclusive.
4. **A source-by-source trace:** original article → exact statement in the original → subsequent mathematical repair/reconstruction → claim in this dossier → falsifier or exclusion.
5. **A proof-first scientific programme:** positive and negative controls, strong conventional baselines, independent generator and referee, permissions and costs, with criteria under which the proposed metaprotocol loses.

## Four evidence statuses

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| **S — Standard** | A known mathematical result; cite and prove in our restricted setting, no originality claim |
| **R — Reconstructed** | A new interpretation or finite model built by the research review, *not* already proved by the original historical article |
| **D — Derived conditional** | A valid restricted proposition using S/R plus declared assumptions; proof given |
| **H — Hypothesis/open** | Architecture, empirical economy, multi-vendor prevalence, superior outcomes, or scientific generality awaiting external validation |

**Hard boundaries:** Complexity alone does not imply A/B/C/D distinctions; added B does not entail decision improvement; a lower bound on resource stock does not make transitions costless; UNKNOWN must remain possible; qualitative “frustration” is never evidence of subjective experience in a synthetic agent. Sections marked H are **not** prerequisites silently smuggled into D.

**Promotion gate:** independent source-faithfulness review + theorem/proof review + strong-baseline experiments + document-control pass. No automatic elevation to 00M/00N or ITU contribution.
