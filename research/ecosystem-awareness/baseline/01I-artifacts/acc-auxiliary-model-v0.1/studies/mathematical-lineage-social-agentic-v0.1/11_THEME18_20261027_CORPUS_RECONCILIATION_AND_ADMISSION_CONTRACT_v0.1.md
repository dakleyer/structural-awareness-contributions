## 2. What we should offer #18 now: a *Decision- and Candidate-Domain Adequacy Profile* (DCDAP-18)

Proposed **informative** semantic record with **no new default execution authority**. It is *not* a newly ratified #18 message schema. Its purpose is to make choice compliance and the conditions **under which choice is meaningful** independently inspectable.

### Input domains (typed, not collapsed)

**C18-REF** — authored/versioned reference: the objectives/"voters" and their units and permissible trade-offs; lexicographic vetoes and/or choice operator; candidate class and admissible action domain; choice regime and mode; declared transition conditions; provenance, effective time, legitimate owner. An undeclared item remains explicit, not silently filled with a default. **X.dmst** design justification stays upstream; [#5](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/5) retains standing.

**RA-OBS** — *optional or required according to the declared decision dependency*: qualified observation map Ψ, history and contextual-sufficiency status, invariants/baseline/threshold/delay, directional evidence §P_RA§ or UNKNOWN, coverage and timing. **No qualified source/context ⇒ P_RA absent, not zero.** An environmental departure finding does not itself identify which operator or criticality should apply. When a decision's eligibility is conditional on validated RA evidence, that dependence must be declared; optional RA missing does not prohibit independent authorized actions.

**EA-QUAL** — a decision-specific receiving question and sources (the current qualified A, additional eligible B/Q, explicitly grounded C explorations and D barriers, or UNKNOWN where role cannot be established); valid scope/standing, current receiving interface, constraints, dependencies, residual, bounded cost/time and independent query rights. Check the [corrected source D3-A §02](./02_FORMAL_CORE_AND_PROOFS.md): select supplemental queries **conditioned on already available A**.

**CANDIDATE-PROFILE** — **not automatically part of an RA detector**: available candidate identities, local admission and rejection **predicates** (explicitly distinguish §¬\mathrm{Admit}§ from an unknown substantive truth), proof of source-independent positive utility/safety lower bound where needed, existence certificate (if any), **actual encounter** receipt, possible changed objective/candidate syntax, allowed operations, rights/consent and incremental verification/reconfiguration costs. A C frontier or common complement does not by itself give a candidate, truth, probability of arrival, or permission.

### Outputs (independent dimensions, not one misleading "drift")

1. **REFERENCE_STATUS:** attributable / stale / unversioned / no declared reference / unknown;
2. **OBSERVABLE_REGIME_STATUS:** qualified compatible / qualified departure / **UNKNOWN** (and detection coverage);
3. **DECISION_FRAME_STATUS:** qualified adequate for declared question and model / review required / underdetermined / unsupported / inconsistent frame, with residual and witness;
4. **CANDIDATE_ADMISSION_STATUS:** current admissible, characterized alternative, justified exploratory frontier, actually received candidate requiring validation, or unavailable/unknown; "not accepted locally" **never** equals "improvement";
5. **EVIDENCE_NEXT_STEP:** no inquiry, source/authorization check, context repair, a **conditioned-on-A** minimal Q request, actual candidate verification or explicit unresolved state;
6. **CONFORMANCE/AUTHORITY:** **separate** #6 act-time verification and legitimate #5 grant, #16 oversight where needed, #21 population view where relevant. Our record has **NO EXECUTION VERDICT** and never mints a policy revision.

Each reported result is pinned to decision, reference/version, subject, scope, event time/as-of, expiry, cost and source method. Keep *UNKNOWN, FALSE, not-measured, invalid-source, no-candidate-received and no-authority* distinct.

### Why this is not merely rebranding #18 drift

Drift asks whether the effective aggregation deviates from a reference. Our claim concerns whether the *reference's admissible problem and evidence* still support a **specific** decision, even if aggregation and local conformance remain unchanged. A policy can change legitimately under X.dmst/#5 without physical regime departure; a measured physical departure may require EA review without changing the operator; a candidate-domain expansion may require new source verification before choices are comparable; a Condorcet cycle is a choice-rule outcome, **not RA detection**.

## 3. A concrete multi-objective source-grounded example, with four non-identities

**Source scenario:** PR57 synthetic AG01 has **two providers and 10 physical resource units**. Four additional cartography observers are **abstract observations**, not new providers. Hold an **illustrative** declared choice operator §O§ fixed: hard safety/rights/consent veto followed by a cardinal scoring rule over **commensurable declared synthetic** mission throughput and verified cost. Source #18 does **not** specify this particular operator. The objective values, cost units and admissible thresholds must be frozen **before** scoring.

- **Stage I — classical reference:** existing legal operation delivers §Y=10§; no extra candidate received; default reference remains as declared. #18/#6 can report an action conformant while EA may legitimately hold a separate incompleteness diagnosis. No RA departure is inferred.
- **Stage II — meta-cartography available:** four source-scoped local *rejection predicates* are combined for one common candidate type; a source-qualified partial Q establishes a **conditional** near-gain (§0.8§ in AG01's blocked second-order record), but **no new qualifying candidate has arrived after the exchange**. Realized throughput remains §Y=10§. A property of a frontier may be more useful than collecting raw object states; it **does not** create an executable choice or a new vote.
- **Stage III — candidate actually received:** a new event, first captured by observer-2 at fixture time t=7, passes source/event/scope and the independently assumed **class-to-AG01 Q correspondence**. Under originally allowed transfer, verified source and consent/authority gates, the old **same** source PR57 mechanism can deliver §Y=11.5§; benchmark technical optimum is §13.5§ and remains referee-only. Same-Q competent conventional peer can deliver §11.5§ too.
- **Failure path:** False Q (AG11) may drive §Y=8.875§, so "fresh label" is not source truth. D material (AG09), absent consent (AG07), undeclared role transform (AG05), no arrival, failed scope check or high cost must **not** produce a GO. No automatic repair or self-authorization.

**Four non-identities:** (a) a four-dimensional residual class ≠ physically measured throughput Y; (b) candidate outside four local admission sets ≠ beneficial candidate; (c) source-qualified Q ≠ authenticated act-time authority; (d) change of the **choice/candidate syntax** ≠ independent evidence of a changed **observable RA regime**.
