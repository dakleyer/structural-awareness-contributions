## B. Exact novelty boundary

The following are **not novel** and must never be advertised as such: pigeonhole \(K>M\); factorization through observation; finite congruence checking; partition refinement; R03’s pair-separation→weighted cover; the monotonicity of *costless optional* information; existence of signed contradictory reports; elementary reuse-cost subtraction; incomplete prefix indistinguishability.

The potentially useful research contribution is a **carefully scoped composition** of these mechanisms into:

1. A falsifiable **changed-contract/misson adequacy** decision for an *independently generated* ecosystem.
2. A minimal, legal, source-qualified set of additional queries for this **specific receiving question**.
3. A certificate stating what the available evidence proves, refutes or leaves underdetermined, including a safe UNKNOWN.
4. A permissions-aware, budgeted, potentially metamorphic coordination proposal with no extra resources or stolen authority.
5. An **empirically contestable** comparison against conventional orchestration and the technical UC21 R1/R2/R3 cases.

Whether this composition is truly different, implementable, cheaper, better or original against modern strong prior art remains **open**, not established by internal tests.
