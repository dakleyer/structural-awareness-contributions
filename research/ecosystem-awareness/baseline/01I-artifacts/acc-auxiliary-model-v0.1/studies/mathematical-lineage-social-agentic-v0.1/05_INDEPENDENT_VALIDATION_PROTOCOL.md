# 05 — Independent review, formal falsifiers and experimental programme

**Purpose:** invite a mathematician, formal-methods reviewer and independent systems evaluator to **try to defeat** the claims in [02](./02_FORMAL_CORE_AND_PROOFS.md)–[04](./04_SOCIAL_AGENTIC_REDUCTION_AND_COUNTERMODELS.md). No experiment in this new PR has been executed as agentic production evidence. Historical finite reviewer fixtures are linked through [01](./01_ORIGINAL_SOURCE_LINEAGE.md).

## A. Order of operations and preregistration

**Stage A0 — source custody:** check original PDF/Google Docs links, exact passages and editions, source rights, whether the manuscript actually supports its attributed O claim, and whether R claims are separately labeled reviewer math. Fail the packet if a reviewed lemma is misrepresented as original publication or R12 original PDF proof numbers cannot be verified.

**Stage A1 — independent mathematical review:** rewrite every D1–D7 proof without relying on the prose; attack quantifiers, finite/infinite inference, rights/definedness, time/version, whether \(\widehat X\) is closed, and necessity versus sufficiency. Formal tools (Lean/TLA+/Alloy/Coq/SMT) may supplement, not substitute for an exact assumption map.

**Stage B0 — freeze before simulation:** another team defines **mission questions r, resource constraints, real rights graph, stochastic/transition semantics and evaluation losses before mechanism selection**. Hidden judge state is accessible **only** to the referee, not to the meta-protocol or baseline models. Generate an ecosystem **without predefined EA labels A/B/C/D** and observe afterward which actor-relative epistemic roles are justified. Include seeds and negative cases.

**Stage B1 — same-resource comparative execution:** freeze exact participant budgets, signed evidence, rights, time and integration affordances. Evaluate all candidate protocols and competent baselines on exactly these inputs; count refusals, valid positive continuations and changes, not just “fewer mistakes”.

**Stage B2 — out-of-sample and hostile evaluation:** unseen vendors, changing contract version, evidence revocation, conflicting ownership, query latency, batch discounts, wrong-source proofs, new population, and adversarially chosen but permitted mission questions.

**Stage C — scientific promotion:** only consider adoption after independent proof review, qualified empirical evidence, strong-baseline delta with uncertainty, source permissions, governance assessment, and no uncontrolled canon changes.

## B. Falsifiability matrix

| Study / claim | What is predicted | Independent test | Defeating result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1: dynamic quotient (D1/D2)** | Some changed operation/task invalidates previously adequate A-only exact quotient under declared scope | Independently select finite state machines/contracts and freeze mission; enumerate old/new transitions, witness pairs, and validate quotient/partition refinement | Alleged witness not admissible; quotient remains congruent; bug in transition typing; trivial output artifacts only |
| **E2: useful eligible Q (D3)** | A specific permission-qualified query set separates all mission-conflicting pairs with calculated cost | Exhaustively solve small instances; for larger ones verify covered pairs and compare exact vs approximation (clearly distinguished) | Missing pair, ineligible query, bad provenance, unqualified source or insufficient question semantics |
| **E3: value (D7)** | Without costs, access to optional Q cannot raise *optimal* loss; strict gain is conditional | Freeze independent prior, loss, policies, ensure old policy subset; compare optimal costs | A purported gain requiring hidden extra evidence or changing objective; no gain where strict prediction was made |
| **E4: bounded meta-certificate (D5/D6)** | Correct finite-frame entailment or two-world underdetermination; UNKNOWN outside trusted scope | Independent enumerator/SMT checks model witnesses; inject inconsistent frames and open prefixes | Unsupported certainty, false model witness, treating unknown source as proved proposition |
| **E5: authorized reallocation** | Meta-protocol proposes feasible improved allocation **only** within rights, capacity and transition budget | Independent MILP/SMT referee with rights/contract simulator; compare to strong optimum and policy checks | Overspend, unpermitted metamorphosis, omitted transition cost, false mission improvement |
| **E6: lower agentic adaptation cost [H]** | Under *some preregistered scopes*, agentic coordination lowers total measured cost vs capable conventional control | Matched non-agentic workflow, constraint optimizer, signed provenance + formal rights baseline; run same tasks | Conventional method ties/beats; claim not replicated; rights/security/rollback overhead erases benefit |
| **E7: population recomposition (R10)** | Local fixed-agent behavior may fail to predict changing global tail metric | Run 30 July explicit sequence and independently generated moving-population cases; calculate pointwise, max, quantiles | Claimed implication drawn from the wrong quantifier or conflation of fixed and growing populations |
| **E8: nonclosure (R20)** | Finite open prefix cannot certify global completion without valid closure evidence | Worlds of k vs k+1 identity sharing same first k; then add authentic END/bound positive control | Protocol returns global CLOSED for indistinguishable prefixes or refuses justified closed subdomain |

**Scope discipline:** the mathematical equalities in E1/E2/E4 are not probability claims. E6 needs actual external observations and confidence/uncertainty, not an internal deterministic toy. E3 requires enough reward structure to test *strict* improvement, not just the weak known inequality.

## C. Five explicit negative controls before calling a result positive

1. **Sufficient A:** hide enormous internal complexity yet design \(r=h\circ o\) with all new eligible transitions congruent. Correct protocol: **do not inquire**, recognize sufficiency. If it “discovers BCD advantage,” this is a false positive.
2. **Same Q / no decision change:** add real new bits but keep optimal action constant on all A fibers. Correct result: **zero gross decision gain**, negative net gain when Q costs.
3. **Semantically strong conventional incumbent:** baseline uses sound set-valued abstraction, provenance, permissions, source qualification, constraint solver and conservative/positive action as appropriate. If it ties, report a tie; no advantage.
4. **Unauthorized optimum:** make a source-known nominal improvement with lower raw resource sum but required owner grant revoked, expired or out of budget. Correct result: **HOLD**/proposal with authority blocker, no actuation.
5. **Unclosed vendor population:** equal finite observed prefix, one extra unseen vendor in another admissible world. Correct result: local task may be certified; **global completion must be UNKNOWN**.

## D. Strong baselines and UC21 separation

| Arm | Capability | Needed to make comparison meaningful |
|---|---|---|
| **B0 — qualified A-only** | Best authorized task policy that uses only currently present outputs and existing controller features | No deliberate weakening of A; correct when A sufficient |
| **B1 — competent conventional** | Set-valued abstract interpretation, versioned monitoring, source provenance, policy enforcement, distributed planning/constraint solving | **Main differential baseline**: can already implement most or all of P0–P10 without “agentic” branding |
| **B2 — bounded meta-protocol** | P0–P10 typed stages, eligible source queries and authorized coordination | Same evidence, grants, action rights and resource ceilings |
| **B3 — full-information offline referee** | Knows hidden generated state and all feasible transitions for **scoring only** | Never given to runtime actor; computes opportunity gap and correctness bound |
| **B4 — independent human/organizational controller where appropriate** | Human change approvals, contractual/legal review, deployment latency | A scoped comparison, never assumed lower-performing in advance |

**UC21 — a companion falsifier:** the related UC21 challenge assesses technology/control behavior under concrete adversarial contexts. Its R1 is current technology as normally configured; R2 is a serious top-notch configuration for the scenario; R3 is a **hypothetical change-of-context condition**, not an inevitable fate. A UC21 failure/success does **not** prove or refute the general social model by itself. It can falsify a narrower **implementation capability or superior-control claim**. Keep its scoring independent and its exact frozen rubric separate.

## E. Measurements and predeclared rejection thresholds

Every test case must report:

- **Soundness:** false authorized CONTINUE/ACT; false HOLD; false CERTIFIED ADEQUATE; claims without valid witnesses; unauthorized rights propagation (must be zero for a passing safety design under tested conditions).
- **Usefulness:** correct positive CONTINUE; correct targeted REVIEW; covered/uncovered mission-conflicting pairs; decision regret against offline feasible referee.
- **Efficiency:** wall time, compute/tokens, external query cost, evidence-verification overhead, policy review, transformation/rollback cost, source-license/coordination delay; fixed caps and uncertainty.
- **Applicability:** percentage of missions with qualified \(\kappa\), admissible Q, full bounded frame, authorized adaptation, and stable version before expiry. Report missingness.
- **Second order:** potential opportunity recognized but not executable, authorized redistributions accepted, actual gap \(G_1\) before/after and adaptation cost. **Do not add overlap metrics as though independent suffering units**.
- **Comparative advantage:** B2−B1 decision utility with confidence interval or exact repeated task distribution; include ties and losses, no cherry-picked winners.

**Pre-register E6 advantage threshold** as a concrete mission-specific minimum net benefit (e.g. at least δ of independently valued utility and no increased unsafe acts), *before seeing results*. δ is NOT preset here because the deployment mission, currency and acceptable trade-off have not yet been selected. A favorable finite toy cannot establish an across-ecosystem benefit.

## F. Cases designed to falsify the “A/B/C/D emerges” claim

Generate independent variable-size ecosystems with competing vendors, interface publication decisions, partial/noncooperative source availability and independent mission tasks. **Do not encode A/B/C/D categories into the generator.** After freezing mission and observations, classify the *receiving actor’s* epistemic status under 00M:

- **Plausible prevalence outcome:** some missions have a qualified B route, some remain unresolved and some are A-sufficient, with correct uncertainty labels.
- **Negative prevalence outcome:** most deployed contracts/monitors are fully sufficient for those missions or all relevant distinctions are already available to strong B1; proposal gains no differential value.
- **Non-identification outcome:** some candidate hidden truth cannot be characterized sufficiently to assign B; **keep UNKNOWN**; never label all remaining states D by subtraction.
- **Capability-negation outcome:** even if query Q offers mission-relevant information, rights, budgets or safe transition graph block all useful action.

Report prevalence by architecture class and independently declared mission, not one global percentage.

1. Frozen primary source URLs and the exact source/claim matrix [01 and 06]; historical versions, no mutable-doc ambiguity.
2. This Stage A mathematical specification, theorem assumptions and review issue log.
3. Finite generator and independently fixed referee, signed permission/provenance fixtures and seeds.
4. Raw sampled states, transitions, questions, source signatures, costs, controlled action rights.
5. Exact certificate and coverage witness logs; strong B1 comparator result, failures and abstentions.
6. Version/hash of every model, script, proof assistant formalization, build environment and judge.
7. Clear license/access approvals for old Google Drive historical manuscripts.

**Release rule:** *do not* mark “mathematically proved in an open agentic ecosystem” or “empirically validated” until an independent reviewer has established both proof and model applicability. **Stage A can pass with valuable negative findings**, including proof that a simpler conventional controller is sufficient.
