# 01 — Source lineage: real originals, exact reuse and forbidden overreach

**Scope:** source-auditable map for the mathematical social→agentic research dossier. All URLs below identify *existing, historical originals* and distinguish them from the subsequent mathematical review. Opening a Google Drive/Docs URL may require holder access. Neither the author nor external readers should assume public licensing from URL availability.

**Notation:** **O** = claim/proposal actually present in the historic original; **R** = formalization, counterexample or test added by the later research reviewer; **I** = integration/proof newly written in this PR. “Reviewed” does not mean peer-reviewed. ## 1. The core original articles used directly

### G — *The Given Universe* (given-universe factorization)
- **Original, PDF:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ePHBGgco-qiQ4S0BmsCNTTFdd2C1JgCd/view
- **Source locus:** original §4, *factorization* (functional compression, indistinguishable latent states), §6 (three idealized objects/limits), §7 (conditional discussion of integrity/debt).
- **O:** a public interface is a projection from a richer description; identical public reports need not imply identical hidden interiors. The proposed “debt” is conditioned on a chosen meaning of integrity.
- **I retained:** a vendor may legitimately expose a transformation contract/output and conceal internal dependencies. Under an independently specified receiving task, *distinct latent states can share a visible contract and demand different downstream decisions*. This is a possible architecture, not a universal law. It motivates the explicit observation map and its fibers in [02](./02_FORMAL_CORE_AND_PROOFS.md).
- **Not retained:** “complex system” ⇒ automatically insufficient A, “more latent states than output classes” ⇒ intrinsically better BCD, or a source §7 “debt” as a measurable agent suffering theorem. The source’s letter B for a debt quantity **is not** canonical EA A/B/C/D category B.

### R01 / S01 — *Principios de buena definición* (operational congruence; the strongest exact bridge)
- **Original:** https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GRO7MRD67_2ao_uKQl6TCxXlnV_wVAzQKxBl1SCYeK0/edit
- **Mathematical audit/development:** https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/06-lineage/mathematical-reuse/reviews/REVIEW_S01_PRINCIPIOS_BUENA_DEFINICION_v0.1.md
- **O:** identity/substitution claims require *good definition* under the operations for which substituted objects are used; observational labels alone do not establish functional interchangeability.
- **R, review §6 P1–P4:** congruence criterion for quotient operations; 4-state parity example under an expanded signature (P2); saturated domains for partial transfer (P3); finite refinement to the largest stable sub-equivalence (P4).
- **I retained:** proof that an interface adequate for an original mission/operation signature can fail for a newly permitted operation, *without the source output becoming false*. This dynamic mechanism is the central conditional mathematical assertion. The refinement is a standard finite-state partition algorithm, **not** a new mathematical discovery.
- **Audit caution:** “R01” and “S01” refer to the same lineage, **not two independent sources**. No automatic authority or semantically complete abstraction follows.

### R03 — *Primalidad y Lógica* (distinctions, prime/composite syntax)
- **Original:** https://docs.google.com/document/d/19oVzuvEbNBq0-Mj59_3QwIeuft-lkdfd8GEyewZgIHU/edit
- **Review:** https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/07-field-notes/REVIEW_R03_PRIMALIDAD_Y_LOGICA_v0.1.md
- **O:** finite syntactic indexing of primitive and composite “bunches,” and how representation may obscure initially available distinctions; source concerns proof/logic, not an API query optimizer.
- **R, review §14.2 (esp. R03.14.2–14.3):** for independently chosen finite world set X, receiving target r, and permitted queries Q_j, sufficient queries separate all r-conflicting state pairs. With additive costs, the minimal selection is weighted set cover. Review §14.2’s GF(2) XOR fixture is **an explicitly new synthetic interpretation** of a composite query; the original manuscript’s \(\oplus\) is **syntactic, not XOR**.
- **I retained:** select cheapest *eligible, provenance-qualified* observations that distinguish precisely the mission-relevant ambiguous pairs. This is an engineering composition and conditional complexity issue; ordinary known weighted set cover is not attributable as an original paper theorem.
- **Not retained:** primality forcing useful information, all “bunches” being executable, an inherent nonzero BCD benefit, or conflating syntactic operations with XOR.

### R05 — *Formalización del Meta Sistema* (second-order questions, not an omniscient oracle)

## 3. Independent prior mathematical comparators required by honest positioning

- **Equivalence relations, quotient congruences, partition refinement, finite automata minimization:** standard algebra/computer science. R01/S01 applies these familiar results in a very specific source/reviewer bridge. Do not report a new discovery of “information absence.”
- **Weighted set cover:** standard combinatorial optimization. R03 §14.2 explicitly identifies it as a familiar equivalence under restricted additive costs.
- **Sound over-approximation / abstract interpretation:** a successful conventional comparator can return sets of possibilities and still support conservative decisions where an *exact* quotient fails. Any claimed differential must beat a competent control using such methods.
- **Conditional value of information:** more costless eligible observations cannot worsen the *optimal* decision-maker who can ignore them. **Strict benefit is not guaranteed**; cost, authority, distortion and source changes must be measured.

No source cited here makes a general claim that multiagent ecosystems necessarily instantiate every category B/C/D. Those are actor-relative epistemic roles from **00M** with explicit qualification, never a finite partition of reality.

## 4. Attribution and reproducibility protocol

Each claim in [06](./06_CLAIM_AND_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md) carries source IDs G/Rxx, exact review loci, proof/file location, intended test and epistemic status. Before publishing externally, a reviewer should (a) independently open each primary article and check the cited locus, (b) verify reviewer-written derivations have **not been back-attributed to the original author manuscript**, (c) check which Drive links are accessible and whether rights permit onward distribution, and (d) separately audit 00M/00N meanings.

**The “original article” and the “source review” are distinct evidence objects.** The review may be more mathematically correct and more relevant to this PR than an older manuscript, but it must remain labeled as a *later reconstruction*.
