# 04 — Social model → agentic ecosystem: a scoped reduction, not a new ontology

## 1. Thesis and the three differential characteristics

The original ACC auxiliary social/economic model considers mission/production, feasible opportunity, coordination, distribution, transformation, bounded knowledge and human welfare. This dossier derives a **restricted technical analogue** for independent multi-vendor agents:

| Component | Social/economic source | Agentic reduction | What is *not* transferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R-1 — Interface projection** | An actor's partial view may be adequate while external dependencies remain unexposed | Each vendor may expose a contract, inputs, outputs and local guarantees but not the full internal topology; changed compositions can break mission sufficiency | No categorical assertion that **every** agentic system is opaque or has usable B/C/D |
| **R-2 — Frustration / mission gap** | Opportunity not realized and human aversion/suffering can be modeled under declared premises | Measured resource/time/performance gap relative to a declared feasible referee baseline; loss of mission opportunity, **without phenomenal experience** | Human pain, happiness, moral patienthood, dignity, or a universal monotonic “debt” law |
| **R-3 — Second-order coordination** | Information about reachable nearby configurations may exist outside an actor's role, though transfer/coordination has costs | A bounded meta-protocol can propose queries, verify permissions, coordinate already permitted reallocation or role change at testable cost | Omniscience, unbounded new A, free metamorphosis, unique “solidarity” ability, or universal advantage over conventional controls |

These properties are **more plausible as design phenomena** in some independently administered multi-vendor ecosystems; that is a **prevalence hypothesis** to be measured. Centralization, full trusted visibility, shared runtimes and matched conventional controllers remain valid negative controls.

## 2. Use of *The Given Universe*: projection by independently specified architecture

Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) represent participant-local states and \(X\subseteq\prod_i X_i\) the declared admissible global configurations. Under a contract-only receiving view \(o:X\to O\), information about the interiors is projected away. The source *Given Universe* §4 discusses the factorization idea; here the following is **our deliberately narrower conditional bridge**:

**Conditional construction AG1 (S):** If there exist **admissible** \(x,y\in X\) with \(o(x)=o(y)\), but the **independently declared** global receiving mission requires \(r(x)\ne r(y)\), then no exact A-only map \(h\) realizes \(r=h\circ o\) on this scope (F1). This is elementary and is only interesting to the extent the architecture and mission independently supply the witness.

**Critically:** the finding is *not* “a complex system must have BCD.” The witness states are generated by actual declared contracts and an independently frozen task. If the receiving question ignores the hidden differences, then r factors and **A is adequate**, irrespective of hidden complexity.

The richer **dynamic** construction AG2 [R01/S01] holds the original contract and old mission fixed, then changes the **allowed inter-vendor composition or mission**. It can produce a *new* witness \(x\sim_o y\) with \(o(f(x))\ne o(f(y))\), even though the old abstraction was accurate for old operations. That *transition-relative adequacy loss* is the actionable question.

### A two-provider example with a real negative control

- Provider V1 exposes the standard status of a work item; Provider V2 manages a downstream reserve and publishes a licensed availability/scope tag Q only to authorized recipients.
- Original task r0 is merely “is V1's handoff syntactically valid?” All hidden V2 states are irrelevant; A is sufficient. **No BCD necessity.**
- New authorized task r1 is “can the whole mission complete with the reserved capacity under the new inter-vendor SLA?” Two admissible V2 states may give same V1 A but require different r1; now **mission-relative** A may be insufficient.
- If V2's Q is available, current, same-purpose and authenticated, combining it with A may separate the required pair. For that recipient and question it **may** be qualified as a usable B-route under 00M; it is not automatically B for all recipients.
- If Q is forbidden, stale, or cannot separate the pair, there is **no invented evidence**. The proper output is HOLD or UNKNOWN.
- Strong incumbent control already querying V2 may achieve the same result at lower cost. **The metaprotocol does not win by name.**

## 3. Preserving canonical A/B/C/D without materializing an “ABCD universe”

**00M A/B/C/D are functions of participant + question + time + characterized information and method**, not four globally enumerable disjoint boxes. The letters refer to what this actor can establish or characterize in the declared scope.

| Operational phenomenon | Permitted interpretation | Unjustified interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| A local implementation contract produces output | An A-level statement if valid for the actor/question | “A includes every possible transformation or all future agent states” |
| Another actor exposes a characterized qualifying inquiry | A potential B-type pathway relative to source/receiver, subject to rights | “All latent interiors become B” |
| New vendor, changed purpose, uncharacterized possibility | May warrant uncertainty/requalification | “C is a measured missing dataset or known set of optimal states” |
| Unreachable or uncharacterized limit | Acknowledge the boundary; do not quantify D without an admissible model | “D has a finite cardinality or can be queried by metaprotocol” |
| A meta-diagnostic proves the finite frame underdetermined | Valid meta-level A about the frame; potential B relevance for a distinct actor/task | “The underlying object truth has become A” |

**Non-closed ecosystems:** membership of X and availability of transitions can change. **Do not deduce global absence** from no witness in a finite observed prefix [R11/R13/R20]. At each changed vendor, operation, mission, source version or revocation, the qualification is reopened. “Residual A–B–C–B” is a useful workflow hypothesis, not a proved fixed-point theorem.

## 4. Frustration: two meanings kept separate

The social version may model *subjective* disutility, aversion and human welfare under its own ethical/empirical assumptions. The agentic version defines a **mission gap** \(G_1=[U_{\rm ref}-U_{\rm run}]_+\) [03 §5]. No synthetic agent's feelings are asserted.

**The delicate second-order point:** after qualified observations, an actor may recognize an alternative allocation with better nominal mission value but **outside its own feasible/authorized transformation set**. Awareness of a nearer optimum may increase the measured *unrealized opportunity* or information about it while *not* changing actual performance. The observer may need a cross-owner agreement and a transformation cost. That is the economic reason to study coordination; it does not prove that BCD always reduces frustration.

The model must hold these apart:

1. Reference total optimum under a **realistic, independently constrained** resource and authority boundary.
2. Current reachable optimum for each agent under its **present** role/capabilities.
3. Proposed reachable optimum after a **separately authorized** coordinated change.
4. Information cost of identifying it, transition cost to implement it and risk of failure.
5. Human impact (if any), independently sourced and never assigned to nonhuman agents by analogy.

A diagram or numeric toy case is not evidence of monotonicity/acceleration of human debt; the social model's existing hypothesis needs independent estimation and falsifiers.

## 5. Meta-protocol: a *possible* structural differential

For candidate reconfiguration \(\tau\) and qualified query set H, compare:

\[
\Delta_{\rm agentic}
=\mathbb E[U(\tau)\mid A,Q_H]-\mathbb E[U(\mathrm{incumbent})\mid A]
-C_{\mathrm{inquiry}}-C_{\mathrm{verify}}-C_{\mathrm{coord}}-C_{\mathrm{transform}}-C_{\mathrm{risk}}.
\]

This requires a **same-budget, same-rights conventional incumbent**; otherwise it is an apples-to-oranges claim. \(\Delta_{\rm agentic}>0\) is **H (empirical)**, not derived by F1/D1/D3. \(\Delta=0\) is a valid finding; \(\Delta<0\) refutes the local economy hypothesis.

**What is genuinely agentic-dependent here?** Nothing in the core set-theoretic mathematics requires agents. The empirical candidate is that agents with explicit authorized adaptation interfaces and a composable discovery/contract protocol can, in some conditions, perform *the same bounded source-qualified coordination* at lower verified adaptation cost than a static monolith or a human institution. But modern declarative orchestration and good distributed systems may match or beat them. Test rather than assume.

## 6. Exact isomorphism/reduction limits and promotion gate

| Claimed carry-over | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Given opportunity constrained by capacity and coordination | **Restricted analogue**, if capacity/constraints are independently measured |
| “Frustration” as human suffering and agentic resource inefficiency | **Not isomorphic**: only operational mission-loss measure is transferred |
| Social solidarity as ethical freely chosen sacrifice, and programmed agent resource sharing | **Not isomorphic** ethically; functional resource-transfer policy exists without autonomous AI |
| New query distinguishes previously equivalent states | **Standard math** conditional on eligibility, no agent-specific novelty |
| Second-order frame detects what existing sources can justify | **Finite technical reconstruction**; universal oracle rejected |
| Continuous/instantaneous metamorphosis across arbitrary roles | **Rejected** without a specific finite capability, rights and transition graph |
| Local improvement implies global wellbeing | **Rejected** by task-scope and R10-style population counterexamples |
| UC21 as proof of the whole mathematical/social model | **Rejected**; it is a complementary technology falsifier, not a theorem proof |
| A synthetic good outcome as demonstrated stakeholder utility | **Rejected** without external mission data and independent adjudication |

**Deliverable of this reduction:** a *research specification* identifying when the A-only incumbent should keep acting, when to seek Q, when to attempt an authorized reallocation, and when to abstain. It is a conditional, source-traceable hypothesis that can fail under independent tests.**
