# 15\. Claim boundaries

&nbsp;

This architecture does not claim:

\- that probability and indeterminacy are synonyms;

\- that every residual is quantifiable as probability mass;

\- that every system-level property is formally undecidable;

\- that more agents automatically imply more information loss;

\- that distributed systems are inherently inferior to monolithic systems;

\- that ecosystem signaling creates knowledge that nobody possesses;

\- that complete ecosystem state sharing is desirable;

\- that Ecosystem Awareness replaces observability, uncertainty quantification, provenance, formal methods, resilience, incident exchange, trust management or human oversight; or

\- that FG-TIDA has adopted this architecture.

&nbsp;

The narrower claim is:

&nbsp;

Agentic systems need an architecture that distinguishes uncertainty inside the local context, residual indeterminacy outside that context, and information loss introduced by intermediary/compositional boundaries; manages each differently; and preserves enough decision-relevant information for ecosystem-level operating decisions without pretending to achieve omniscience.
