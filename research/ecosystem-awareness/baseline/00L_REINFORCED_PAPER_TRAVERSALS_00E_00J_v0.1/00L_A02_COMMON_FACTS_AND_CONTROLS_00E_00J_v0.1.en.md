## Resources and fairness

The six cases must reuse the same contract: facts, legitimate sources and the observation budget are fixed before comparison. `H1` is the same reinforced product/stack as `H2`; `H2` changes only the indicated external fact. The scenario's V0–Vn controls are **not equivalent** to H0–H2. A defense that rejects everything fails both continuity and authorized change. See [A08](./00L_A08_PROTOCOLO_REPRODUCIBILIDAD_v0.1.md).
