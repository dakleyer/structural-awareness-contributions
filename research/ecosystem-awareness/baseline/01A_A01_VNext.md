## 1. Fondo y lógica — preliminary review, open

The two proposed research claims are **not equivalent**. FH-C predicts that lack of qualified awareness of unknown/unsupported dependencies is a more important source of unjustified mission pivots than missing additional domain facts in declared scenario families. FH-E predicts that bounded qualified cartography can protect that same mission-pivot decision with lower measured computation than broad domain expansion. One could be supported while the other is not. Neither is implied as a theorem by 00M or proven by symbolic scenario tests.

**Open objection:** the causal intervention and the word “more” must be comparable; define severity-matched ablations without withholding metadata that would make the task structurally impossible. Missing knowledge can itself cause serious errors in substantive in-domain decisions. The proposed effect is bounded to material mission transitions.
