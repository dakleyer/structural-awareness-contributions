STATUS LEGEND

\[EXPLICIT\] — preserved explicitly in a current canonical or current operational artifact.

\[EQUIVALENT\] — wording changed, but the responsibility/constraint survives with materially equivalent meaning.

\[LINEAGE\] — intentionally retained only in a predecessor/research artifact; not currently visible in the canonical core.

\[COMPRESSED-RISK\] — a material idea survives only partially or in a weaker form; a future reader could miss it.

\[TRACE-GAP\] — source still exists, but current indexes/registries do not adequately expose it.

A. ROOT PROBLEM / WHAT ECOSYSTEM AWARENESS IS

&nbsp;

A1. System ≠ ecosystem; ecosystem is a dynamic interdependency environment, not a common-goal system.

Origin: Article 01 Naturalistic Foundations; early conceptual \#13 draft.

Current state: \[EQUIVALENT\]. Foundational Theory v0.2 preserves the open decision-relevant ecosystem and bounded local representation; Functional Architecture preserves separately governed external peers and no central brain. The explicit naturalistic contrast “ecosystem does not require cooperation/common goals/trust” is no longer prominent in the canonical foundation.

Risk: medium. This was part of the marketable/security framing and should remain traceable even if not normative.

&nbsp;

A2. Protected system vs security-relevant ecosystem.

Origin: Article 01\.

Current state: \[LINEAGE\]/\[EQUIVALENT\]. Current documents speak of mission, operating frame and surrounding dependencies, but the explicit protected-system/ecosystem distinction is no longer named.

Risk: low-to-medium. Concept remains but explanatory clarity was compressed.

&nbsp;

A3. Local security correctness does not establish ecosystem-level validity.

Origin: Article 01; public Theme \#13 conceptual work.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. General Law, F5/F6, non-superpower rules, contracts and comments all preserve this distinction.

&nbsp;

A4. Agentic lifecycle inversion: T\_ecosystem may approach or become shorter than T\_system.

Origin: Article 01; early conceptual \#13 draft.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT/EQUIVALENT\]. Foundational Theory §7 preserves T\_ecosystem \>\> T\_system versus ≈ and \< as a heuristic and relates it to churn and validity lifetime.

&nbsp;

A5. Security of assumptions as a security-relevant object.

Origin: Article 01\.

Current state: \[COMPRESSED-RISK\]. Current F6 operating-frame validity and requalification implement it, but the strong phrase “security of assumptions” is no longer a visible canonical framing.

Risk: medium because it explains why EA is different from ordinary security controls.

&nbsp;

A6. Necessity-versus-boundedness: the system increasingly needs ecosystem knowledge but can never know the complete recursive ecosystem.

Origin: Article 01; user prompts; later Foundational Theory.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. This is now a foundational contradiction in Foundational Theory v0.2 and Control Matrix v0.3.

&nbsp;

A7. EA working definition as continuous, relevant and sufficient rather than complete awareness.

Origin: Article 01 and public \#13 work.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. Foundational Theory v0.2 and Functional Architecture v0.2 preserve continuous qualification, mission relevance and minimum sufficient awareness.

&nbsp;

A8. EA is a different security layer because it qualifies whether local identity/authority/policy/attestation/oversight outputs remain meaningful under the current ecosystem conditions.

Origin: Article 01; early \#13 draft; user publication strategy.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT/EQUIVALENT\]. Strongly present in Functional Interfaces, contracts and comments, though not always under the “security of assumptions” label.

B. OBJECTIVE / RISK / RESOURCE / CONTROL-SUFFICIENCY CHAIN

&nbsp;

B1. Objective Envelope S(t) comes first; solve the architecture backward from what must be preserved.

Origin: Article 02 MCA; Article 03; Article 05; old \#13 conceptual draft; user prompts.

Current state: \[EQUIVALENT\] but \[COMPRESSED-RISK\] terminologically. F1 “Mission & Decision Context Qualification” carries objective, criticality, consequences, tolerable residual, constraints and response horizon. The explicit term “Objective Envelope” is absent from current Functional Interfaces and no longer the visible top-level causal chain.

Risk: high for lineage/clarity, low for functional completeness.

&nbsp;

B2. Response capability must be considered before deciding how much awareness is needed.

Origin: Articles 02/03/05; user prompts.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. F1, F2, F6, F7 and contracts carry reachable response capability, response horizon/window and reversibility.

&nbsp;

B3. Good Enough Early Warning (GEEW): warning quality only matters relative to the time/capability to act.

Origin: Article 03; Article 02; Article 05; old \#13 conceptual draft.

Current state: \[COMPRESSED-RISK\]. Current architecture carries response/warning horizon, actionability and response-window expiry, but the named concept and the explicit causal relation “Objective Envelope → response requirement → Good Enough Early Warning → Semantic Window” are not visible in the current canonical chain.

Risk: high. Responsibility survives; concept/derivation does not.

&nbsp;

B4. Actionable warning margin Δt \= t\_l − t\_d; detection after useful response closes is operationally insufficient.

Origin: Article 03\.

Current state: \[LINEAGE\]/\[EQUIVALENT\]. Current response-window/actionability semantics implement the idea, but Δt and the explicit test relation are absent from the current canons/testbed.

Risk: medium; useful as a testable quantitative bridge.

&nbsp;

B5. Semantic Window W(t) is a deliberate strict subset of technically observable ecosystem state O(t): W(t) ⊊ O(t).

Origin: Article 03; old \#13 draft.

Current state: \[COMPRESSED-RISK\]. F2 strongly preserves “window as allocation of attention, not universe/sensor boundary”, but the explicit O(t) versus W(t) distinction and strict-subset formulation have disappeared.

Risk: high for explaining what window selection actually means.

&nbsp;

B6. Semantic Window must be dynamic/adaptive: widen, narrow or redirect as risk/confidence/regime changes.

Origin: Article 03; user prompts.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. F2/F7/F9 and UC-EA-01/02 preserve widening, narrowing, redirecting and requalification of W(d,t).

&nbsp;

B7. Window quality has multiple dimensions beyond breadth: depth, temporal horizon, resolution, source diversity, provenance depth, update frequency, uncertainty representation and cost.

Origin: Article 03\.

Current state: \[COMPRESSED-RISK\]. Current F2/Interfaces preserve breadth, freshness, source diversity, provenance, uncertainty and cost; depth, resolution and temporal-horizon-as-window-dimensions are not consistently preserved as an explicit set.

Risk: medium-high because “window size” could otherwise collapse into breadth only.

&nbsp;

B8. Window failure surfaces: too narrow, too wide, stale, correlated, authority-blind, capacity-blind, uncertainty-erasing.

Origin: Article 03\.

Current state: \[EQUIVALENT\] but distributed. Type 1/2, F2, F4/F5, authority and human-capacity contracts collectively cover them. The named seven-surface list is no longer present as one testable unit.

Risk: medium.

&nbsp;

B9. Learn better boundaries of attention, not only better predictions: remove low-value variables and incorporate repeatedly missed precursors.

Origin: Article 03 §12.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT/EQUIVALENT\]. F9 now feeds back wasted search versus missed changes to tighten/widen/redirect W(d,t). Phraseology changed but responsibility survives well.

&nbsp;

B10. Minimum Control Architecture (MCA) is not “fewer controls”; it is the least sufficient observation/authority/intervention/feedback configuration for the Objective Envelope.

Origin: Article 02; Article 05; old \#13 draft.

Current state: \[LINEAGE\]/\[COMPRESSED-RISK\]. Current EA core incorporates minimum-sufficient awareness and posture/capacity, but no current canonical EA artifact explicitly retains MCA as a distinct configuration-space object.

Risk: high for research lineage; intentionally may remain outside FG-TIDA common interface.

&nbsp;

B11. MCA is a multi-optima / feasible-sufficiency-region problem, not one universal optimum.

Origin: Article 02/05.

Current state: \[LINEAGE\]. The current Benchmark disclaims generic risk-based optimization as novelty, but the multi-optima/Pareto/feasible-region formulation is not in the current EA canonical architecture.

Risk: high for preserving the deep research programme; probably not necessary in the public interface comments.

&nbsp;

B12. Multiple sufficient configurations can trade sensing, warning, containment, migration, signalling, redundancy, oversight and feedback.

Origin: Article 02/05.

Current state: \[LINEAGE\]/\[EQUIVALENT\]. Current functions/interfaces expose these capabilities but no longer present them as substitutable investments in one configuration landscape.

Risk: medium-high.

&nbsp;

B13. Type 1 has a resource consequence: over-protection through excessive search, observation, verification, waiting and human escalation.

Origin: user prompt 10 Sep \+ earlier MCA/Semantic Window work.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. Foundational Theory, Control Matrix, F2/F6/F7/F9 and Testbed v0.2 preserve it.

&nbsp;

B14. Type 2 has an exposure consequence: overconfidence through under-observation, stale-frame reuse and suppressed residual risk.

Origin: user prompt 10 Sep \+ earlier work.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. Foundation, Control Matrix, F2/F6/F9 and testbed preserve it.

&nbsp;

B15. Correct epistemic position is dynamic and risk/capacity indexed, never 25/25/25/25.

Origin: user prompt 10 Sep; current theory.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. Foundation and Control Matrix preserve this strongly.

&nbsp;

B16. Humans belong inside the extended determination system; escalation consumes scarce human capacity.

Origin: user prompt 10 Sep; earlier \#16 work.

Current state: \[EXPLICIT\]. Foundation, F1/F2/F6/F7/F9, \#16 contract and UC-EA-03 preserve it.
