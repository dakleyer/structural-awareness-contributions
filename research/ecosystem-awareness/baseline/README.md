# Ecosystem Awareness — current baseline corpus

This folder is the **primary public corpus of Ecosystem Awareness (EA)**. Read the documents below in order. The current foundation and benchmark are public working successors; the controlled v0.4 source files remain preserved and are not silently rewritten. [Corpus publication manifest](../CANONICAL_CORPUS_MANIFEST.md) states what is public, what is frozen and what still lacks revision-anchored SHA verification.

The complete prior v0.4 §§1–18 and working dictionary are inside this one current file. The five [v0.4 source parts](./01_FOUNDATIONAL_THEORY_v0.4.part01.md) remain intact.

The v0.4 documents 02–05 are still published as ordered parts, **not** independently complete chapters: a part may cut through a sentence. Do not mistake a part boundary for a conceptual section. Their intended single-file paths and controlled Drive revision/SHA comparison remain open.

These preserved documents explain **how the current corpus developed**; they have not been merged into, replaced by or counted as extra chapters of 01–06.

2. [Article 02 — Minimum Control Architecture and Multi-Optima](../lineage/ARTICLE_02_MINIMUM_CONTROL_ARCHITECTURE_MULTI_OPTIMA.md): sufficient control under bounded burden and plural feasible configurations; a research precursor, not an adopted architecture.
3. [Article 03 — Semantic Window and Good-Enough Early Warning](../lineage/ARTICLE_03_SEMANTIC_WINDOW_AND_GOOD_ENOUGH_EARLY_WARNING.md): an objective/response-horizon-derived window and the detector relationship.

[Article 04 — Ecosystem Signalling Without Required Cooperation](../lineage/ARTICLE_04_ECOSYSTEM_SIGNALLING_WITHOUT_REQUIRED_COOPERATION.part01.md): signals across independent actors without presuming universal cooperation ([part 2](../lineage/ARTICLE_04_ECOSYSTEM_SIGNALLING_WITHOUT_REQUIRED_COOPERATION.part02.md)).

[Article 05 — Integrated Reference Model for FG-TIDA](../lineage/ARTICLE_05_INTEGRATED_REFERENCE_MODEL_FOR_FG_TIDA.part01.md): bounded implementation-oriented reference model, not an adopted FG-TIDA standard ([part 2](../lineage/ARTICLE_05_INTEGRATED_REFERENCE_MODEL_FOR_FG_TIDA.part02.md), [part 3](../lineage/ARTICLE_05_INTEGRATED_REFERENCE_MODEL_FOR_FG_TIDA.part03.md)).
