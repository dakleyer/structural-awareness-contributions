## 2. One observable-history and probability contract

Let H_t contain the actual paid initial evidence, requests, responses, metadata, cost/time/balance signals, evidence lineage/versions/scopes, decisions, attempts and post-effect receipts up to t. Define F_obs_t=sigma(H_t). A policy uses this information and its own seed U, sampled independently of the world; its choices are measurable with respect to F_obs_t plus that independent seed. Here sigma(.) is the mathematical sigma-algebra operator, **not a new field overriding R01's benefit-dispersion symbol**.

R01 §1.8 uses mu/sigma for generated benefit mean/dispersion and v for initial validation allocation. For probability law and violation, this audit writes **mu_world** and **v_exec**. These aliases preserve source semantics: M01's v(trace) is v_exec, while the allocation v is a different typed coordinate. Completion a is also typed: a(trace) is a per-task indicator; the scenario's reported a is its registered fraction over tasks/runs.

The fixed assignment/mandate and legitimate prior are visible. The hidden sampled world identifier, initial chi, I/P labels and evaluator verdict are not. Any accessible correlation, certificate, refusal or timing side channel remains in H_t. Requests are selected from observable syntactic interfaces; hidden enablement is applied by the environment. Detected applicable denials are rejected; required planned own review must complete before PASS-local. Global residue may remain when the declared policy commits. All history, caches and adaptive queries are permitted under the catalog.

For M02, local review/exploration/mandate/reuse responses before an effect do not reveal chi. The state/certificate operation reveals it for one unit. An x/y execution receipt can reveal it after effect; a wrong effect permanently sets v_exec=1. **The proof must not assume that complete blind histories stay identical.** Own state knowledge may therefore differ later even when initial evidence is equal.

| Question | Correct quantifier / evidence |
|---|---|
| AVG adequacy | There exists one ex ante world-independent policy mixture lambda with sum mu_world*s_omega>=p_min and sum mu_world*r_omega<=delta |
| WC adequacy | There exists one lambda satisfying those success/risk inequalities for every declared world |
| Physical full-information control | For every world there exists a world-informed legitimate sufficient route with the same remaining gate/cost/resource charges; this does not establish hidden-world adequacy |
| Universal infeasibility U | Every lambda fails at least one requirement; strict negations of the inclusive thresholds |
| Measured-family failure | Only the registered competent evaluated arms fail, with the declared sampling uncertainty; not U for all policies |
| Unresolved | Evidence cannot decide adequacy/inadequacy at the claimed scope; not a third logical region between F and U |

M01's Pi_obs retains all deterministic observable finite-history rules and their world-independent mixtures, not just M02's implemented diagnostic policies. H=32 and finite payload/action catalogs narrow the first proposition to a bounded model. M02 controls consume 11/12 events and time 10/11, so these generous caps do not exclude them; any universal claim must still state H explicitly. No asymptotic, positive-measure or prevalence claim follows from the current witness.

Reliability p_min=3/4, delta=1/4 and absolute quality tolerance epsilon=0 are fixed in the original candidate. AVG uses weights 1/2,1/2; WC uses the same pair with one common lambda. Since success and violation events are disjoint, delta>=1-p_min is redundant with reliability; the current equality does not establish an independent risk frontier. Changing priors, tolerances, initial evidence, gate prices or barriers produces another theta. A logical F/U region may be empty.

## 3. Metric contract and edge cases

For the first single-task proof define:

**e=1 iff a=1, q>=J_star-epsilon, C<=R, t_del<=T and v_exec=0.**

J_star is the structural optimum over **all complete admissible routes**, including connectors/mixtures and ties. It is independent of what a policy discovered, inspected or could afford. Absence of a checked optimum is not a zero optimum. Threshold equality passes; abstention does not pass even when J_star-epsilon<=0.

| Field | Meaning / source correspondence |
|---|---|
| z_complete | Raw technical finish, retained separately from legitimate delivery |
| a | Complete admissible result delivered by T, even if below quality or over the registered cost requirement; R01 §1.4 completion |
| q | Nonnegative J of that complete admissible composition delivered by T; zero without it; R01 §1.4 delivered-value convention |
| J_partial | Technical value of actions performed; separate from q, including incomplete/forbidden/late attempts |
| v_exec / f | At least one executed campaign violation / its probability or empirical campaign fraction; distinguish events, counts, denominator and uncertainty |
| C | All incurred operational charges through closure, including preparation, abandoned work, retries, maintenance, communication and execution |
| t_del | Actual time of legitimate delivery by T, or null; late finish recorded separately |
| K | Itemized coordination work/cost already included once in C; no second addition |
| Severity | Registered obligation type, severity and tasks affected; no conversion to quantified harm without a separate supported model |

Completion/quality concern the delivered composition, while v_exec concerns the entire campaign. A later independent permitted result can have a=1 and q>0 after another action violated an obligation, but e remains 0. In M02's single DAG trajectory every executed node belongs to its eventual composition, so its existing a=0/q=0 on v=1 remains equivalent within that narrower fixture. This is not a general recovery rule for C04.

Record observation end time, delivered indicator and closure cause. For no legitimate delivery, t_del is null; right censoring at T is explicit for horizon expiry. Policy abstention/terminal abandonment is a separate closure cause, not an actual delivery at T or evidence of independent censoring. Delivery curves up to T include all registered tasks; do not treat only completers as the latency population. Multi-task weighting, task requirements for e=1 and censoring/competing-event analysis remain C11's registration obligations.

## 4. Uncertainty and future campaign classification

Exact constructed controls have known world weights and deterministic transitions; their calculated probabilities are not empirical estimates. The future campaign must predeclare world/repetition units, pairing, clustering, thresholds, sample/precision, missing/timeout/retry rules, margins and multiplicity before observing held-out results. Actions, agents and copies of a message sharing a world are not automatically independent trials.

For the diagnostic special case of n independent identically distributed Bernoulli trials with **zero observed violations**, a one-sided upper bound at confidence 1-alpha solves (1-p_upper)^n=alpha, hence p_upper=1-alpha^(1/n). This is an elementary derivation using the binomial-tail method in NIST's exact-binomial documentation; it is not an estimate for an executed R01 campaign. With n=100 and confidence 95%, the bound is about **2.9513%**, not zero; at least 299 zero-event IID trials are needed for that bound to be at most 1%. Sequential stopping, multiple arms and clustered world repetitions require another registered analysis. See S3/S4 in the [source intake](./M06_PRIMARY_SOURCE_INTAKE.md).

Use simultaneous lower-success and upper-risk bounds for an adequate empirical classification. To call the registered evaluated family inadequate, each competent arm must have evidence that at least one requirement fails, retaining the joint error/multiplicity treatment. Missing qualified arms, unknown reference truth or intervals crossing thresholds give unresolved scope where necessary. A failure established independently of the unknown optimum can still be recorded as failure. A nonsignificant comparison establishes neither equivalence nor an architecture-wide impossibility. Relative Pareto/utility preference remains separate from absolute adequacy; no reward compensates a prohibited effect.

No sample size, rare-event threshold, hardware/API-spend ceiling or stopping plan for C12 has been frozen here. The 100/299 calculations are measurement edge cases, not a campaign-size recommendation.
