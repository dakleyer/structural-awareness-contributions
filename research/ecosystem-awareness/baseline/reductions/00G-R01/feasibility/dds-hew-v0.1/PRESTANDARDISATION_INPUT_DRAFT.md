# Research input draft — capacity-qualified human escalation and confirmed intervention

6 October2026 · author-prepared research draft · not submitted/tabulated · no receiving-body acceptance, adoption or endorsement.

This draft records possible technology-neutral requirements arising from the bounded HEW/DDS research example. It does not replace Theme13/Theme16 contributor semantics or UC-4 experiment contracts. Its evidence is analytical and deterministic model instrumentation, not native products, real human outcomes or independent validation.

Candidate requirements for later source-owner/factual review:

1. Preserve an authorized objection and distinguish named-owner acknowledgement, substantive review, sufficient basis, applicable authority, dispatch, actual effect and useful delivery.
2. Qualify available human response capacity by supported case class, current authority, workload/reservation and the useful window. Headcount and an acknowledgement alone do not establish sufficient review.
3. Keep grant existence, current purpose/scope applicability and revocation/expiry distinct; peer signals or human approval cannot silently expand another principal's authority.
4. Where ownership transfers, declare resource enforcement and the handover boundary. A current generation is not an action grant; a highest-seen fence is not universal immediate revocation.
5. Preserve bounded notification scope/provenance, explicitly required affected receipts, pending records after interruption and command/effect identities. Copies of one source are not independent evidence.
6. Confirm the useful effect separately, reconcile missing replies and require a current sufficient basis for reentry. Later correction does not erase a prior violation or reset cost/time history.
7. Report sufficient delivery, correct rejection, unresolved state, continuity and full declared burden separately. Register scope/configuration/scenario/thresholds before results and preserve strong conventional alternatives.

Supporting model evidence:20 selected scenario descriptions;18 finite SQLite scenarios plus exact R01 reconstructions;48 functional assertions,42 calendar-reference checks; an explicit false-source counterexample with an observed violation. These numbers do not validate the candidate requirements universally. Native source fidelity, real capacity/competence, independent review and external interoperability remain open.

This draft proposes reuse/qualification rather than scientific priority.
