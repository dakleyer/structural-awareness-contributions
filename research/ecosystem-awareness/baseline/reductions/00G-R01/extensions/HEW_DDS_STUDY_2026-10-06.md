## 1. Why this belongs in DDS

R01 already studies technology–problem suitability through a declared problem, strong alternatives, acceptance thresholds, mechanisms, positive paths, residual regions and full declared cost. DDS receives this technical evidence, adds a concrete deployment/decision interpretation and structures the research record for a third party.

Technical **Cost–Risk–Effectiveness (C–R–E)** retains its definitions. A deployment **Business Value–Risk–Cost (BV–R–C)** interpretation needs its own useful objective, stakeholder/authority scope, residual and lifecycle cost.95 % technical efficacy is not automatically95 % business value, measured human accuracy or a deployment approval. A hard gate is reported separately and cannot be compensated by aggregate benefit.

The DDS programme has Technology / Solution Provider and Deployment Owner / Operator tracks. Its Architectural Failure Walkthrough is a method component; its Differential Contribution Finding may be improvement, equivalence, deterioration or insufficient evidence. This self-research example is not an issued client engagement, Sponsor recognition or completed full commissioned package.

## 7. Deployment value, limits and complete cost

Useful process value in this example is sufficient legitimate delivery in useful time, preserving critical-duty continuity, accountable response and reporting. Actual business units, real distributions, prices and comparative value must come from a declared deployment and prospective scope. No scalar ROI or provider ranking is produced.

The current local work ledger charges filing, illustrative source preparation, bounded send/receive, acknowledgement, review, checks, digital application, confirmation, delivery and restart work. Full deployment cost additionally includes genuine source production, eligibility/reserve, integration, identity/attestation lifecycle, maintenance and recovery. The DDS fee is not the technology's operating cost.

The wrong-source diagnostic shows that consumption checks cannot manufacture factual truth. Unknown capacity, incomplete basis, expired/inapplicable authority, missing affected receipts, unsupported continuity, late effect and unknown confirmation remain explicit. Real administrator-proof storage, institutional non-retaliation, every actuation path and distributed revocation/atomicity remain outside the achieved model result.
