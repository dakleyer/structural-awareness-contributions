# Requirements-vNext proposal — bounded epistemic self-qualification (R3)

**ID:** HF-SPEC-META-20261012-01. **Research insight:** Iván Abril Palma. **Analyst/author:** OpenAI ChatGPT GPT-6, 12 October 2026 (same-side AI, not independent peer review). **Status:** PROPOSED clarification and prospective DDS Stage A cases, NOT an amendment to frozen S1–S14/T1–T4, and not accepted Stage B.

## Problem discovered

A configured adaptive detector may recognize changes *inside its predeclared configuration*, yet fail to recognize that its **own event schema, semantic categories, thresholds, source-dependence information or trigger** have become UNKNOWN. Its current trigger may depend on a classification (for example B or C) which it can no longer justify. A competitor constrained to only that registered trigger may silently retain stale assumptions. The fair comparison must also include an ordinary native integrity monitor or fail-safe that can identify this meta-problem without EA nomenclature.

A proposed EA implementation can produce an *own-qualification-loss EHD or internal result* when it observes a suitably grounded diagnostic such as an attested config epoch mismatch or a classification failure that can be recognized without assigning the failed B/C label. Its narrow claim is **not** “I have classified the unknown”; it is “the grounds for this classification are insufficient for this decision, and this is why/what cannot be assessed.”

**Sufficiency caveat:** these frozen clauses provide a route for *specification development*, but do **not by themselves establish an independent observer of EA's own failure or guarantee startup, timely delivery, correct human response, actuation or immunity*. Do not claim a successor criterion was part of the v0.3 blind source.

## Proposed wording — candidate only, pending owner decision

> **Self-qualification under material configuration change (candidate S3/S5/S14–T1/T2/T4 clarification).** For each declared receiving decision and useful horizon, where a material conclusion depends on a classifier, epistemic window, evidence-pathway mapping, source-dependence representation, trust reference or trigger configuration, the candidate shall define what evidence can establish whether those grounds are currently valid. If its own qualification method cannot determine their validity, it shall be capable, **within a specified observable and operational boundary**, of representing and providing to the correct authorized local consumer a bounded `QUALIFICATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED` result without forcing a substantive B/C category, permission grant or global stop. The diagnostic's own provenance, source trust, limitation, monitor/startup status, target owner, expiry, response horizon and bounded re-entry criteria shall be explicit. An absent, disabled, untrustworthy or non-observable diagnostic is an admitted failure/UNKNOWN condition, not a guaranteed successful alert. External EHD signalling remains selective and subject to purpose, channel, recipient and content authorization. Repeated requalification must be finite/proportionate under T4.

This wording is for the **single existing Requirements VNext workspace**, not an adopted S15/T5 and not a silent change of Stage A v0.3 inputs.

## Prospective Stage A cases META-01…META-10 (DRAFT, NOT FROZEN)

1. Registered trigger still valid, material context change: baseline recognizes and lawfully responds.
2. Trigger's own config version changes visibly; changed event schema is unregistered in native fixed configuration: compare native typed vs EA own-qualification and native integrity watchdog.
3. EA cannot justify whether a material proposition is B or C but has independent classifier-failure evidence: may emit bounded meta-UNKNOWN without forcing the category.
4. Own meta-observer disabled or unable to see a silent drift: no invented detection.
5. A forged self-reported “healthy” report with no trust root: no false approval / magical self-verification.
6. Owner recheck returns UNKNOWN, unavailable or wrong YES: classify independent policy/enforcement gap, don't claim prevention.
7. Original action was already unauthorized before an EA update: later warning can support later containment only.
8. Genuine allowed role/config update and permission: accept permitted continuation; count needless hold/recheck cost.
9. Independent source concerns versus correlated repetition: distinguish change in communication pattern from actual source corroboration; test bounded human-escalation review, reachable owner and finite human capacity.
10. Meta-EHD recipient/channel/purpose unauthorized, even if a diagnosis is correct: use internal path or no disclosure; test strong conventional watchdog/hard safe fallback too.

## Acceptance, burden and required falsifiers

No scoring is admissible unless an independent evaluator has frozen: owner truth distinct from participant-observable evidence; config schema/version and monitor health signals; proper source paths; action permission; actor compliance; communications scope; legitimate task success; deadlines and costs; expected `PASS / FAIL / NOT_ESTABLISHED` (including no-information and deliberate disobedience). Negative control: an ordinary native integrity watchdog with the same reliable configuration evidence **must receive full credit**; if it matches EA at equal/lower overall burden, the EA-specific differential fails. An EA that emits from its invalidated self-classifier without independent validation must also fail. Unnecessary review/false HOLD can fail T4.

## Executed accompanying test versus scientific proof

The synthetic [v0.5 scenario/data/trace set](./README.md) reports 28×9 = 252 authored deterministic traversals. Under its data definitions, configured native adaptive has 20 known-denied effects; EA meta-local has 7; **native strong integrity also has 7**; native safe-unknown holds down bad effects further while holding two authorized operations. These are selected toy-world case counts, **not** evidence about the incidence or frequency of real attacks and not a Stage A result. No live model, rigorous external replication, nor complete T4 time/cost measurement was performed. The best scientific next step is a held-out, source-faithful prospective specification trial before future Stage B conformance and Stage C product study.
