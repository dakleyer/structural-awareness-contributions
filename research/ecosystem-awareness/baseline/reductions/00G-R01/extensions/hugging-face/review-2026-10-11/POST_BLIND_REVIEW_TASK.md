## Required adversarial questions

- Which executed result is reproducible with a clean checkout and original dependency versions? Which is merely a design? Keep raw traces, hashes and dissent separately.
- Does EA change an actual proposed operation or just produce a label that the following scripted policy may ignore? Distinguish signal emitted, received, consumed, decision, attempted effect and independently confirmed effect.
- Does the conventional comparator receive the same admissible current source and scope information as EA? Does its stronger freshness/lineage checker already pass?
- Which legitimate task is unnecessarily held, timed out, or made expensive by EA? Is the extra query/communication/human workload fully charged?
- Does the source schema rely on fictional leases, vendor-trust facts or hidden oracle truth unavailable to the tested receiver?
- Would a new, evaluator-created positive/negative matched example change the report's supported claim? Submit it as a prospective successor, not a rewritten older case.
- Which old auditor finding remains unresolved; which author correction verifiably improves method, not just wording?
