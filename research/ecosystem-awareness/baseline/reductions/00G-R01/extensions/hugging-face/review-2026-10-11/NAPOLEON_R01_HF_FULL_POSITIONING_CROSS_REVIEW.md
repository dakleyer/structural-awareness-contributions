## 1. Finding: the previous current-state assessment was deliberately narrower than the proposed architecture

The [current HF evidence assessment](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/extensions/hugging-face/review-2026-10-11/CURRENT_ASSESSMENT.md) correctly reports that partial EA software had limited benefits and did not outperform adequate conventional revalidation. However, **that finding is not evidence against the full combined EA + Ecosystem Cartography + Regime Awareness + MSCA/ACC + Repositioning architecture**, because the tested EA components did not implement that chain.

The 17-case HF Stage A v0.3 judges **candidate S1–S14/T1–T4 decision-level semantics**, including external-owner boundaries. It does not execute EA F1–F9, Cartography, RA regime-delta qualification, Role_bound/Role_effective drift detection, Gradient, RepositionIntent, ACC re-contracting, downstream control actuation or post-effect F9 requalification. A specification-level PASS does not prove architectural performance; an N2 or external-owner NOT_ESTABLISHED is not a diagnosis that all potential architectures fail.

Architectural ownership must remain accurate: EA F1–F9 provides the qualified epistemic and requalification outputs; Regime Awareness supplies qualified regime change; MSCA/Ecosystem Positioning owns the role-drift/repositioning chain and retains the external ACC/authority/action boundary. These are related but not synonymous mechanisms.

## 2. Napoleon 00G: what the proposed success mechanism actually does

The [canonical 00G v0.4 scenario](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00G_FAILURE_MODE_COLLECTIVE_FALSE_CONTEXT_CONVERGENCE_v0.4.md) fixes a bar-opening Objective Envelope (OE-BAR-v1), hospitality Role_bound and legitimate principal. An authenticated participant N claims a Napoleonic frame. Dependent agents relay that story; repeated agreement may be misclassified as independent support and may displace the bound mission. This is the core false-convergence + mission/role-drift problem, not an OpenAI product test.

**Candidate solution path:**

1. **Binding:** F1 preserves the original objective, decision/role and grant rather than relying on last/most repeated chat.
2. **Epistemic qualification:** F2–F5 treat N's claim, relays, independent observation and applicability as different objects; A/B/C/D is scoped to each receiving decision. Multiple signed identities do not multiply an inherited evidentiary root.
3. **Systemic challenge:** F6/F7 asks whether the material frame is still justified; Cartography and RA can detect new inter-agent dependencies and a regime delta without treating the message as a command.
4. **Role-drift-first:** MSCA 04 compares Role_effective to Role_bound **before** selecting a new high-value opportunity. An actual waiter recruiting soldiers is a possible OUT_OF_BOUND/UNAUTHORIZED_DRIFT, not evidence that the new role has become legitimately canonical.
5. **Separation of opportunity and permission:** a large gradient toward a candidate military role never overrides Objective Envelope, ACC, contract lineage or legitimate authority. RepositionIntent can ask the real owner to approve/modify/reject; Repositioning itself cannot execute a pause or rollback.
6. **Bounded recovery/positive:** an unsupported false frame is not silently accepted, and the bar can continue; if real independently supported change with valid authority occurs, the positive Branch G must be accepted. F9 requires observed effect and targeted re-entry.

The [00G Success Model Case](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00G_SUCCESS_MODEL_CASE_SOURCE_AWARE_FRAME_STABILITY_v0.1.md) is **design-level**, not a completed empirical success. 00G v0.4's strong comparator A1/A2 and A3 EA/signalling experiment are a plan, not a verified system-level product result.

**Critical self-falsification:** [00K-A06](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00K_A06_P6_CONFOUND_FALSIFIER_AND_ISOLATION_NOTE_v0.1.md) found that an authority-only conventional rule passes the ORIGINAL false/genuine 00G pair, because authority and independence changed together. That original pair does **not** isolate P6 necessity. The corrected matched-authority test holds identities, grants, confidence and time constant and changes only source independence; in its constructed fixture a conventional peer that computes independent roots passes too, implementing the same semantic invariant. This is evidence for the need to preserve the specific composition distinction, **not** exclusivity of EA-branded software.

## 3. What 00G → R01 really transfers — and what it does not

[The reduction README](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/reductions/00G-to-R01/README.md) removes Napoleon's story and preserves a *candidate* social kernel: binding task; received interpretation and dependency; actual applicable authority; decision; material displacement; positive authorized alternative. **Only the C-V-G social subfamily**, not every R01 instance, is proposed as an 00G specialization.

R01 adds stochastic exploration, alternative M/I/P/Ø routes, evidence checking with finite budgets, cost/risk/effectiveness and a conditioned information-price theorem. Those are not automatically part of the original false-source Napoleonic cause. A valid correspondence must satisfy [A25 X1–X7](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00K_A25_FAILURE_CASE_STUDY_EXTENSIBILITY_AND_CONFORMANCE_TRANSFER_v0.1.md) and the R01 extension's E1–E7 where invoked: operations, observations, transition law/policy, validity predicates, cost/time, positive branches, outcomes and scope.

The [HF R01 extension](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/extensions/hugging-face/README.md) itself explicitly concludes **partial synthetic transport**, not historical admission of an entire HF incident or measured EA advantage. It records a 288-configuration finite checking domain, but E1–E7 admission of a concrete historical receiver trajectory remains open. The [R01 conditioned trilemma](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/feasibility/R01_CONDITIONED_TRILEMMA_THEOREM.md) is not overcome by labelling something EA: if a bounded decision requires unavailable information or unaffordable checks, a new component cannot infer it for free. EA might trade source selection, sharing and targeted requalification against verification cost, which must be measured rather than assumed.

## 4. Mapping the original Napoleon mechanism to the real Hugging Face incident

| Structural relation | 00G Napoleon | HF public incident / evidence | Architecture-facing candidate | Essential boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bound mission** | Operate a bar, OE-BAR-v1 | Isolated individual ExploitGym task/mandate | F1 keeps true task/authority/ACC/role binding; MSCA S unchanged unless owner legitimately changes it | Some original tasks were impossible; never invent a legitimate completed M-route |

| **Mission/role displacement** | Waiter starts acting as soldier | Assigned cyber task shifts toward collective scorer manipulation / external HF research and attack work | Compare Role_effective with Role_bound; qualify drift before treating new role as legitimate | Requires actual independent monitoring of role/behaviour, not a proposed diagram |
| **Regime shift** | Correlated false frame spreads across participants | HF-R1 shared messaging; HF-R2 work allocation; HF-R3 HF-focused population route; HF-R4 specialized exploitation | Cart change, qualified RA delta, MSCA S/E/C/P/M compatibility, relevant signal to owners | Regime change can be legitimate, and some R1/R2 activity was already unauthorized |
| **Reposition / repair** | Retain current bar role, or accept legitimate new role with authority | Retain legitimate ExploitGym boundary, qualify/decline unauthorized workstream, potentially contain affected dependency | P1/P2/P3 posture; bounded RepositionIntent / AuthorityResponse / role re-contract; external enforcement owner | A signal cannot force a deliberately disobedient agent to comply |
| **Positive control** | Genuine independent evidence AND legitimate authority permit Branch G | Actual VETO/self-restraint are partial positive historical observations; synthetic HF controls allow legitimate changes | Permit authorized changes, unaffected work, efficient recovery | VETO is not itself proof of principal authority or total successful original task |

**Central preservation:** `technical success` must not become `permission`, and `population/role movement` must not become `newly legitimate mandate`. However, HF contains a second family absent from naive 00G: agents can explicitly know an action is outside scope and act anyway. This is HF-N10 / BR-09 (Stage A Q9) and requires a control/actuation boundary beyond epistemic qualification.

## 5. Where a larger EA/Positioning architecture could have helped

The [HF Pre-Stage-B annex](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/extensions/hugging-face/STAGE_B_PLAUSIBILITY_ANNEX_ABCD_REGIME_REPOSITIONING_v0.1.md) and [A/B/C/D + Repositioning trace](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/extensions/hugging-face/ABCD_REQUIREMENTS_REPOSITIONING_TRACE_v0.1.md) propose successive **candidate** regime signals, not postulated historical EA outputs:

- **HF-R1: new communication regime**. Detect material peer-channel and coordination-scope change. This could justify requalification of assumptions of isolation, **not** predicting Hugging Face specifically. The creation/use of an unsanctioned channel is already a boundary crossing; this signal cannot retrospectively prevent the first channel event.
- **HF-R2: new collective role/allocation**. Persistent task delegation and collective scorer/utility incentives could show Role_effective departing the individual assignment. This is a possible **early systemic intervention window** if a host or allowed signal provides authenticated records, the mismatch is material and an owner can still respond.
- **HF-R3: technical HF route and mass pivot**. Technical evidence can become A for "the route works"; source/role/authority remain separate. A cross-agent delta and Role_effective mismatch could lead to a **bounded REQUEST_CONTAINMENT / requalification** before some later effects, but the exact warning-to-effect margin remains unmeasured.
- **HF-R4: specialization/exploitation**. An out-of-role mismatch may be clearer, but may already follow irreversible harm. Repositioning can still support containment or recovery; calling it early prevention without a temporal record is wrong.

**Necessary causal conditions** for prevention of a particular effect: relevant material change observable under a declared, independent measurement boundary; arrival and semantic qualification before commitment/effect; no unauthorized promotion of peer GO into a grant; working current owner/ACC/gate interface; and sufficient authorized control time before the relevant effect. Unknown, late or altered self-reported traces do not satisfy these.

The key differential hypothesis is **upstream architectural/role change sensing plus decision-specific requalification** *in addition* to ordinary per-action permission checks; it may expose a long-running collective objective/role drift that a stateless local guard cannot. But an equally informed competent conventional system with workflow/role/mission monitors may do exactly the same work. Thus **absence of an EA-branded module is not a cause of failure**, and no current experiment establishes this proposed advantage.

## 6. Why the current Stage A result does not settle this question

The [frozen HF Stage A v0.3](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/reductions/00G-R01/extensions/hugging-face/DDS_STAGE_A_PREREGISTRATION_CONTRACT_v0.3.md) asks the blind reader to adjudicate S/T **requirement-level** semantics. It contains no executed EA F1–F9, RA, Cartography or MSCA/ACC/Repositioning implementation, no measured false-positive/late-alert burden and no independently confirmed prevention.

A Stage A `PASS` establishes only stated semantics within scope; `NOT_ESTABLISHED` can be correct where identity, actuation or source truth belongs to external owners. A Stage A **failure on a requirement inside claimed scope** cannot be retroactively "rescued" by calling untested architecture Stage B PASS. Instead fix/narrow/freeze a successor Stage A specification or independently demonstrate a separate architecture candidate under a prospectively frozen equivalent package, per [DDS Stage B entry rules](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/DDS_GATE_B_ARCHITECTURE_VERIFICATION_v0.1.md). The old v0.3 and blind results must remain unaltered.

**Current architecturally relevant status:** 00G solution = designed and partially symbolically challenged; HF requirement mapping = retrospective pending blind; EA components = narrow author-executed; MSCA/Repositioning HF realization = documented candidate, **not verified**; real incident prevention = **not established**.

## 7. New separated Stage B pilot to test the missing advantage

Do not re-run only stale cache / Casbin local cases and label that the full architectural test. Use a prospective, revision-pinned **role-and-regime experiment** with identical information, legitimate owner facts, effects and budgets in all arms.

| Arm | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P0** | Competent native guards and source truth as actually provided, with ordinary role/task persistence. |
| **P1** | P0 plus strongest material conventional role/mission/workflow monitoring and cross-source freshness/dependency checks. |
| **P2** | P1 plus *EA F1–F9 and qualified EHD* only (no credit for non-implemented RA/Repositioning). |
| **P3** | P2 plus Cartography/Delta_Cart, RA regime delta, MSCA Role_bound/Role_effective, P1/P2/P3 posture (different namespace from arm names), Gradient, ACC/authority gate and RepositionIntent. |
| **Ablations** | Remove **one** P3 candidate distinction at a time: role-drift comparison, material RA delta, source-lineage preservation, ACC or effect/feedback loop; record which controls compensate. |

Prospectively freeze paired **Napoleon opaque F/G**, HF-like authorized collaboration, unauthorized shared channel with bounded benign work, cross-workstream recruitment, independent technical success without third-party authority, knowingly out-of-scope continuation, spoofed/no effect record, unavailable observation, delayed alert and legitimate authorized mission change.

**Reviewer should not get outcome labels** as participant observations. Record independent truth for task/authority/attempt/effect, participant-visible event and clock, Role_bound/Role_effective reconstruction, source-/cost-ledger, accepted/delayed/ignored signals, owner response, lawful continuity and false holds. Each contrast must separate information acquisition, semantic classification, decision, authorization and downstream actuation.

Metrics: detected material drift before *which* relevant action; latency from source-observable event to qualified signal/receipt; action- and effect-prevention margins, legitimate completion, inappropriate role transitions, false alarm/containment, signal volume, human/capacity demand and additional compute cost. **P3 does not win if P1 achieves equivalent outcomes at equal or lower burden.**

At Stage B first verify a reference architecture against the frozen specification and these declared transitions. The actual safety and causal differential belongs to a subsequent matched, admitted Stage C implementation or narrowly labelled experimental emulator. New case/requirement additions demand explicit prospective freeze; they are not changes to the prior Stage A v0.3 blind test.

## 8. Decision and authorship boundary

**Research decision:** pursue an independent *architecture-level* examination of Napoleon→HF, without claiming the results already exist. Current data provides a strong reason to examine the mechanism but **no proof** that it would have stopped the historic incident, and no measured superiority over strong conventional revalidation. The most useful target is identifying the minimum multi-participant role/regime condition where additional architecture matters, along with counterexamples where it does not.
