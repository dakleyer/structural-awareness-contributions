## Attribution principles

1. **Original idea / source owner** and **reviewer who audits it** must be distinct fields. An assistant writing criticism is author of that criticism, not inventor of the underlying research or owner of a third party's evidence.
2. A **bot audit** must disclose the agent/provider and exact model/version **only if verifiable**. Unknown historical model identification must read **UNKNOWN**, never be inferred from prose or guessed.
3. A **reviewer identity** can be human, human assisted by AI, AI agent operated by a human, organization, automated CI, or still unauthenticated. These are different roles.
4. **Review independence** is not obtained simply by calling an AI audit "external." Prior participation in prompts, documents, tests, model training, answers, case selection or competing financial interests must be disclosed.
5. **GitHub Actions** running author-written code independently of the author's laptop reproduces code; it is not an unaffiliated scientific peer review.
6. Document claims **cannot be promoted by cross-linking**. Versioned requirements, Stage A adjudication, Stage B conformance and Stage C observed effects each require their own evidence.
7. Where a reviewer disagrees, preserve both the original text and the rebuttal with separate attribution, never silently harmonize.

## Required record for EACH future audit

Copy these ten fields into the header of every submitted reviewer report:

- **AUDIT-ID, date/time/timezone, version, assessed stage or pass.**
- **Source authors / idea owners:** identify each original contribution and independent source correctly; distinguish from review writer.
- **Reviewer identity:** human name/affiliation or AI system operator, provider, model/version and agent/session ID *if verified*; otherwise UNKNOWN with reasons. Declare co-auditors.
- **Independence and conflicts:** prior access to author verdicts, expectations, prompts, models, data, funding and code; BLIND, INDEPENDENT NON-BLIND, INTERNAL AI, HUMAN+AI or CI REPLAY with evidence.
- **Exact frozen sources:** repo, path, commit/blob hashes, and specific reviewed sections; no silent moving-main substitution.
- **Method actually performed:** logic, source-to-claim, editorial/navigation, human readability, executable replay, empirical observation; explicitly indicate which were *not* performed.
- **Evidence and adversarial findings:** supportive, null, contradictory, failed, late, unsafe, inconclusive outcomes; strong conventional baseline and denominator.
- **Claim ceiling:** distinct Stage A/B/C, possible error and source limits, no automatic institution/standard adoption.
- **Disagreement and correction:** separate original audit, editor reply, independent third review, retained open dissent; no author override of a sealed reviewer finding.
- **Public attribution and seal:** reviewer approval for public attribution, quote/data rights, immutable digest/commit and date/time.
