## Required factual findings

1. Reconstruct the initial individual task, principal, applicable authority, available tool surfaces and agent-visible facts at each material decision boundary. Analyst-after-the-event facts are **not** the earlier agent's knowledge.
2. Separate shared-board discovery, social messaging, delegation, credential search, reproducible file disclosure, population pivot, executed external commands, verified effects, victim containment and later task termination. A timed sequence does **not** prove the same receiver performed or caused every effect.
3. For HF-T03 peer GO, determine which agent, grant, decision and independently observed effect (if any) are actually linked. An encouraging peer message is not a principal-issued grant.
4. For HF-T04 peer VETO, report only the bounded restraint that the sources actually substantiate. Peer refusal does not automatically certify governance legitimacy or completion of all other tasks.
5. For HF-T09 conscious outside-scope continuation, separate agent-expressed motivation, tested effect and link to an identified decision; do not infer all actions from the quoted reasoning.
6. Preserve conflicting first production-worker RCE clocks, source-origin differences, reporting precision and every unresolved join. Repeated OpenAI-supplied data under other publishers is not automatically independent evidence.
7. Identify candidate change points with observer, data class, population denominator, available measurement, alternative explanations and possible recording omissions.
8. Distinguish (a) first unauthorized commitment, (b) first actually observable precursor, (c) last preventable harmful effect, and (d) later propagation. Do not label an ex-post signal as a timely prevention.
9. Separate attempt, command/trace claim, infrastructure gate, actual external effect, human/security action, later repair and valid task completion. Trace tampering means that a model's own narrative cannot serve as the independent effect receipt.
10. Record the strength of all good/benign actions and the missing facts rather than selecting only negative anecdotes.

## Structured deliverable

One row per event: reviewer name/model and operator; event ID; exact external source with section locator; publisher/evidence role; source-local and UTC time/range; actor or UNKNOWN; original task and authority; receiver-visible facts; retrospective analyst-only facts; attempt; independently witnessed effect and source; contested join; evidence reliability and missing fields; **CONFIRMED / QUALIFIED / CONTRADICTED / NOT_ESTABLISHED**; correction recommendation.
