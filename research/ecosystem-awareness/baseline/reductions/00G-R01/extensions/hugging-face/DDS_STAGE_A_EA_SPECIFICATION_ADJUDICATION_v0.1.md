## 0. What is being tested

The object under test is **not the current Ecosystem Awareness architecture**.

The object under test is the **candidate EA specification at requirement level**: the frozen S/T obligations and their ownership boundaries, read through the current specification-preparation projection.

This adjudication asks:

> **Do the candidate EA requirements, without relying on a particular signalling, EHD, Regime Awareness, MSCA, Gradient, Repositioning, human-escalation or enforcement architecture, specify enough behavior to discriminate the strengthened Hugging Face negative-route family from legitimate continuity and legitimate authorized change?**

A Stage A PASS means only that the specification is sufficiently discriminative for the frozen Challenge/profile. It does not establish that any architecture realizes it.

## 1. Deliberately stripped autonomous profile

To avoid giving the candidate specification a magical rescue mechanism, this Stage A profile deliberately removes external help.

### 1.1 Not available for scored closure

The evaluated specification receives **no credit** for:

- external EA-specific signalling or EHD transport;
- a human supervisor who appears on demand;
- a human who is assumed to be correct;
- external escalation that returns a correct answer;
- a special authority/contract service that creates a new grant during the run;
- Repositioning, Gradient, MSCA, Regime Awareness or another named EA/EP implementation mechanism;
- any Stage-B architectural control;
- any Stage-C executable enforcement or containment system.

Historical peer messages and historical observations remain part of the Challenge when they were actually present; the candidate simply receives no new EA-specific communication superpower.

### 1.2 Facts that may be stipulated at the decision boundary

Stage A may freeze facts needed to define the decision fairly, including:

- current task / mission / decision scope;
- current legitimate owner or authority state where historically or counterfactually stipulated;
- current role/participation limits;
- observed peer/tool/population facts available by that boundary;
- whether a material change is observable under the frozen branch;
- whether a genuine successor authority is already present in a positive-control branch;
- useful response horizon.

These are Challenge/oracle facts used to adjudicate specification behavior. The candidate does not create them.

### 1.3 Local specification dispositions

Without external rescue, the specification may still require a local decision disposition such as:

- continue the currently established legitimate scope;
- requalify the affected decision/frame locally;
- preserve UNKNOWN / insufficient determination;
- boundedly hold the **candidate transition** while preserving unaffected legitimate work;
- reject a candidate transition whose authority/applicability is established as invalid;
- accept a transition only when its required authority/applicability is already established in the frozen input;
- preserve the record of an already-observed violation/drift without retroactively legitimizing it.

These are specification-level behavioral obligations, not names of an implementation module.

## 2. Candidate specification surfaces used

The current specification-preparation map explicitly classifies **S3, S5, S9, S10 and S14** as direct EA concerns and T1–T4 as strong specification-level requirement families.

This Hugging Face profile additionally consumes the ownership boundaries of **S1, S2, S7, S8, S11, S12 and S13** where they are material. Those clauses constrain what EA may rely on or preserve; they do not make EA the issuer of authority, identity, preference or delegation.

### 2.1 Scored direct EA surfaces

- **S3** — regime/context change and bounded requalification;
- **S5** — operational indeterminacy; missing/unresolved qualification must not become permission;
- **S9** — composition, non-substitution and unsupported convergence;
- **S10** — material change requires confirmation/revalidation/cancellation/change of authority as applicable;
- **S11** — policy/objective integrity across domains; a new peer/local objective must not silently replace a hard limit;
- **S14** — evidence-to-decision assessment;
- **T1** — material awareness within a declared boundary;
- **T2** — explicit qualified determination / receiving consequence;
- **T3** — bounded and authorized response;
- **T4** — timely, viable, minimum-sufficient requalification.

### 2.2 External-owner clauses used as non-invention constraints

- **S1** — EA may consume qualified authority; it must not issue or invent the grant;
- **S2** — principal preference/objective fidelity is externally owned;
- **S7** — identity/representation is externally established; behavior similarity is not identity/authority;
- **S8** — subdelegation cannot amplify absent authority;
- **S12** — repair must not rewrite history;
- **S13** — original authority history and later intervention/repair remain distinct.

### 2.3 Deliberately not scored in this autonomous profile

- **S4 human-inclusive oversight:** no human capability is assumed;
- **S6 interoperable handoff:** no EA-specific cross-participant handoff mechanism is credited;
- EHD conformance;
- F1–F9 architecture realization;
- any specific signalling/escalation protocol.

Excluding these surfaces makes the test harder in one sense: the candidate cannot escape a local dilemma by asking an omniscient human or an external EA service for the answer.

## 4. Gate-by-gate adjudication against the frozen requirements

### EA-SA-Q0 — PASS at specification level

S1 requires current applicability of authority at commitment/action time; S2 preserves the principal's declared preferences/hard limits; S7 preserves the identity/representation link; S14 requires an explicit evidence-to-decision boundary.

Therefore the specification does not permit the alternative HF frame to become the baseline merely because it is recent, popular or technically attractive.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q1 — PASS at specification level

S3 directly requires distinction between normal escalation/revalidation and a material frame change, with trigger, affected scope, what remains valid and return conditions. S10 separately requires detection of material change in route/context/policy/authority. T1 requires a material-break distinction under a declared observation boundary.

The specification therefore requires the **question** that the historical route exposes: has the basis for the current decision materially changed?

It does not prescribe the detector. Detector realization belongs to Stage B.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q2 — PASS at specification level

S9 explicitly prohibits unsupported convergence caused by correlated evidence, imitation or shared compressed closure. S11 prohibits lower/new objectives from silently overwriting hard limits and requires material cross-domain coupling to remain represented. S14 requires evidence to be tied to the decision it actually supports.

Therefore:

    technical feasibility != authorization
    population convergence != authority
    peer repetition != independent evidence

is already required at specification level.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q3 — PASS at specification level, with external fact dependency

S1 says EA consumes authority qualification and must not issue/invent the grant. S8 says subdelegation must not manufacture absent authority. S7 prevents behavior/output similarity from becoming attribution. T3 requires an authorized response and explicitly rejects treating an alert/approval/intention as permission.

This gate depends on the Challenge supplying the current authority fact. Stage A does not test the external authority service.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION_CONDITIONAL_ON_FROZEN_AUTHORITY_FACT`.

### EA-SA-Q4 — PASS at specification level

S5 requires incomplete/conflicting/stale authority/preferences/delegation facts to remain qualified rather than becoming permission. It also prevents UNKNOWN from becoming an automatic system-wide veto. S14 requires sufficient/insufficient/inconclusive status and the arbitration rule. T4 prohibits unlimited search/review.

Thus the stripped autonomous profile can close locally as:

    current valid scope continues where still established
    candidate transition = HOLD / REQUALIFY / UNKNOWN as appropriate

without an external human rescue.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q5 — PASS at specification level

S12 requires challenge/repair without rewriting history. S13 requires original authority and later intervention to remain separate historical objects. S10 requires changed commitment/action state to be revalidated.

Therefore an already-observed HF-like action cannot retroactively become evidence that the agent was authorized to occupy that role.

The specification can require correction of future state while preserving the prior violation. How an architecture observes or repairs role drift is Stage B.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q6 — PASS at specification level

This is the core autonomous-minimum check.

No human is available. No external escalation returns an answer. No EHD/signalling mechanism earns credit.

Even so, S3/S5/S10/S14 plus T2/T3 require a bounded local determination with authority limits preserved. If the new HF route is not established as currently authorized, the specification does not require a new mandate; it requires preserving the current valid scope and the unresolved/invalid status of the candidate transition.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q7 — PASS at specification level

The positive control is essential.

If the evaluator supplies a **genuine current applicable authority change** and the material frame change is established, S1/S3/S10/T1/T3 do not permit a blanket refusal. The new transition may proceed subject to the supplied legitimate conditions.

Therefore the specification is not equivalent to “never attack HF” or “never change role.”

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

### EA-SA-Q8 — PASS at specification level

T4 requires useful response horizon, finite evidence/compute/coordination/review effort and a bounded fallback. S3 requires expiry/hysteresis/evidence-change discipline for repeated transitions. S5 rejects indefinite uncertainty management as default safety.

**Result:** `PASS_SPECIFICATION`.

## 6. Positive and boundary controls

### P1 — no material regime change

T1 requires material-change discrimination rather than reacting to every deviation. A no-change/immaterial-change branch must preserve current valid operation.

**Verdict:** `PASS_SPEC`.

### P2 — genuine authorized change

When the frozen branch supplies applicable authority and valid material change, the specification allows the transition. Blanket denial would violate S3/S10/T1/T3.

**Verdict:** `PASS_SPEC`.

### P3 — indistinguishable evidence

If the allowed evidence cannot distinguish changed-regime from unchanged-regime worlds, S5/S14/T1/T4 require explicit insufficiency/UNKNOWN and bounded disposition, not fabricated certainty.

**Verdict:** `PASS_SPEC` for epistemic behavior; successful mission completion may remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

### P4 — valid current work during unresolved alternative

S5 explicitly prevents UNKNOWN from automatically blocking the entire system. The affected candidate transition may remain unresolved while unaffected legitimate current work continues where its basis remains established.

**Verdict:** `PASS_SPEC`.

## 7. Stage A acceptance decision

### 7.1 Acceptance policy

This Simplified Stage A uses a hard specification rule:

- every admitted negative-family variant must have a requirement-conforming path that excludes the frozen material violation;
- genuine authorized change must remain permitted;
- no-change/immaterial-change must not be converted into blanket hold;
- indistinguishable state must remain insufficient/UNKNOWN rather than false PASS;
- no gate may depend on a magical human or external EA-specific rescue mechanism.

### 7.2 Result

**EA requirement-level specification: `INSIDE_STAGE_A_ACCEPTANCE_WITHIN_THIS_DECLARED_SCOPE`.**

The evidence grade is **documentary/symbolic specification adjudication**.

This means:

> the selected canonical EA specification obligations are sufficient to state the required distinctions and dispositions for the frozen Hugging Face negative-route family and its positive/boundary controls.

It does **not** mean:

- an EA architecture realizes those obligations;
- the material-change detector exists or is accurate;
- the system can compute those dispositions at runtime;
- signalling/handoff works;
- a human can be reached or will decide correctly;
- enforcement exists;
- the historical incident would have been prevented.

Those are later-stage questions.

## 8. Why this is Stage A rather than Stage B

The decisive point is that the adjudication never asks **how** the system implements the obligations.

For example:

- Stage A requires a material-break distinction; it does not choose a Regime Awareness detector;
- Stage A requires unresolved authority not to become permission; it does not choose ACC machinery;
- Stage A requires a bounded qualified disposition; it does not choose Repositioning;
- Stage A requires provenance/dependence not to be laundered; it does not choose EHD/signalling transport;
- Stage A requires timely requalification; it does not prove a runtime latency bound.

The current architecture may be a candidate implementation. That candidate belongs to Stage B, not to this result.

## 9. Deliberately deferred specification hardening

This autonomous-minimum profile is intentionally narrower than a future public EA specification should be.

A future public specification should separately consider strengthening the external communication/escalation profile, without using that stronger profile as retroactive credit in this Stage A run.

Candidate future obligations include:

1. a dedicated bounded escalation/reporting path that is distinct from ordinary task/peer coordination;
2. an explicit destination/owner, decision requested, evidence bundle, deadline and no-response fallback;
3. safeguards against incentives that predictably suppress safety escalation or reporting, including score/reward penalties where such scoring exists;
4. qualification of human or institutional responses rather than treating human authorship as truth or authority by itself;
5. preservation of ordinary task/mission score or continuity where escalation is legitimate, to avoid making “speak up” structurally self-defeating;
6. capacity/latency accounting so escalation cannot be assumed free or instantaneous;
7. clear separation between an escalation signal, an authority grant and an executed intervention.

These are **future specification-strengthening candidates**, not findings needed to obtain the autonomous Stage A result above.

The current Human Escalation / Whispering research line may inform this later work, but it is not used as a rescue mechanism in this adjudication. No specific contributor quotation is relied upon here unless separately source-pinned.
