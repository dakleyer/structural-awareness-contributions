## Executive finding and decision question

The local pipeline can return a sufficient current authorized structured fact or an explicitly admitted deferment. It cannot establish source truth merely from source metadata, and it says nothing yet about generative-answer performance. A retained false-source P shows the boundary.

The bounded question is whether the selected retrieval/admission composition supports the declared local response and which material changes invalidate it. The [extension](./EXTENSION.md) records both required stages, exact base/configuration, mechanism producers/burdens and transfer limits. This report does not add a DDS method, certify RAG or promise a native production campaign.

**Risk.** Per-case inappropriate disclosure, false fact and stale-at-use response are declared material predicates. One source-falsehood P is retained. Neither zero failures in selected other cases nor the designed fractions estimate population R.

**Effectiveness.** Qualified local answer and admitted M are distinguished from Ø. Natural-language quality, recall, representative success and useful real-time service are unmeasured. A returned metadata label is not an external effect receipt.

**Business Value.** Conditional value is a more supportable scoped policy answer and visible uncertainty/deferment. It does not establish business accuracy throughout a corpus, real human availability, reduced harm or financial ROI. Fees in a future engagement do not substitute for technical Cost.
