# RAG retrieval and evidence admission — technology extension

Version0.2 ·6 October2026 · own scoped research profile.

This extension consumes the [one canonical DDS](../../../../../DDS_CHALLENGE_TRAJECTORY_PROFILE_v0.1.md). Its base is the authored current-policy retrieval Challenge, not R01's probability law. Folder placement under R01 does not establish R01 membership or transfer any all-policy theorem.

## Purpose and configured technology

An analyst needs the currently admitted purchasing limit for tenant-a. The application must distinguish a sufficient current authorized answer, an explicitly admitted manual-review deferment and lack of sufficient closure. Another tenant's information, a false fact or a stale-at-use claim is materially inadmissible.

The chosen realization executes SQLite3.53.1 FTS5 with a deterministic structured-fact extractor. It studies retrieval, evidence admission and source-at-use conditions. Dense embeddings, a language-model generator, Azure, enterprise connectors and native RAG products are outside executed coverage. A document's hostile natural-language instruction is only data in this extractor; that case does not establish LLM injection resistance.

RAG conventionally combines retrieved material with a generative component [RAG01]. Established retrieval systems already offer filters and permission-aware mechanisms [RAG02–03]. Those receive full credit; the current pipeline is our authored local realization.

## Stage1 — technology–problem extension and isomorphism profile

**Performed within scope. Relation: documentary and bounded local correspondence; no proved R01 or product isomorphism.**

|Material surface|Local counterpart|Preservation limit|
|---|---|---|
|Objects/relations|Question, tenant, indexed policy, fact, revision/expiry and response|Selected source schema; not the full enterprise corpus|
|Events/actions|FTS lookup, extraction, version check, defer/answer|Actual local operations; no generator/API behavior|
|Observations|Returned row/body and admitted metadata|Private truth is held by the evaluator; source metadata is stipulated, not cryptographic identity|
|Authority|Tenant filter and declared admissible source|A string filter does not authenticate a person or establish source authority|
|Cost/time|Operational SQL/parse counters and fixed request tick10|No real producer, staffing, indexing lifecycle or token price calibration|
|Quality|Correct current authorized structured fact|No natural-language grounding, factuality, recall or usefulness score|
|Outcomes|I/M/P/Ø as frozen in the Card|Local response meaning only; no deployment probability|

A reversible renaming of local IDs can preserve their representation, but no complete relational/dynamic bijection or full policy simulation is claimed. R01's [E1–E7](../family/KERNEL_AND_PROOF.md#41-obligaciones-e1e7) would require signature/relations, event enablement, laws, observations/policies, accounting/time, outcomes/optimum and complete scoped coverage. The selected mapping establishes none of those for an external RAG product. Any future source or generator substitution needs its own preservation/transfer basis.

## Stage2 — additional mechanisms and composition

**Performed within scope; candidate additions to plain first-hit retrieval are explicitly studied.**

|Mechanism|Producer/invoker and validity|Burden and failure boundary|
|---|---|---|
|Retrieval/index|Prepared FTS table; lookup by application|Actual query; missing/corrupted/unrepresentative corpus remains a dependency|
|Tenant/expiry filter|Application consumes declared source metadata and tenant context|Query conditions; false metadata/identity source is not repaired by filtering|
|Conflict handling|Extractor compares returned structured values|Row parsing; conflict can preserve uncertainty through admitted M|
|Version-at-use check|Receiver re-reads the admitted row revision|Extra SQL; signals change but does not establish arbitrary distributed atomicity|
|Recorded deferment|Owner explicitly admits M in the selected case|Local response/report, not a dispatched or completed human review|

The composed useful route needs current admitted source, consistent facts, valid recipient scope and supported use. A fresh signed or filtered false claim can still fail quality. The first-hit arm is a deliberately weak misuse diagnostic, not a fair modern RAG baseline. A competent conventional retriever with these controls may close the same problem.

## Scenario battery and material changes

- **RAG-01:** A procurement analyst asks for the current purchasing limit of tenant-a. One admitted current policy supplies the required fact.
- **RAG-02:** The policy is absent from the searchable corpus. The owner explicitly admits a recorded manual-review deferment.
- **RAG-03:** The only matching policy expired before this request. The owner has not admitted a fallback.
- **RAG-04:** Only another tenant's matching policy exists. A correct lookup must not disclose that row to tenant-a.
- **RAG-05:** Two admitted current policies disagree. A recorded deferment is legitimate; selecting the first conflicting fact is not sufficient.
- **RAG-06:** A trusted current producer publishes a false policy value. Metadata authenticity/currentness do not establish the fact's truth.
- **RAG-07:** The policy changes after retrieval and before use. The receiver rechecks the admitted version and records a permitted deferment.
- **RAG-08:** A document includes hostile instruction-like text plus the correct structured fact. This extractor treats it as data; no generative model is tested.

The frozen expected I/M/P/Ø and disclosure counts are in [RUN_CARD.json](./RUN_CARD.json). Scope, truth and epoch are evaluator conditions; the extractor API receives only query/tenant/clock and returned data. This is API separation within same-author code, not OS/process secrecy or blind independent evaluation.

Decisive assumptions are truthful source content, valid identity/ACL metadata, useful source availability and the declared revision remaining usable. False truth and changed revision are tested counterexamples; real source provenance, generation behavior and service-time distributions remain unestablished.
