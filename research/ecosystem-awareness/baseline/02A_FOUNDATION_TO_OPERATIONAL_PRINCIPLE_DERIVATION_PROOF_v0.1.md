## 1. Why this bridge exists

The corpus contains three different levels that must not be collapsed:

1. **01 Foundation** explains why bounded representation, open residual, finite capacity, changing regimes and compositional uncertainty exist.
2. **02 Principles & Control Matrix** is the compact normative epistemic register: root principle, A–D poles, I0/I1/I2/O0/O1/O2, received-signal controls and the General Law of Epistemic Composition.
3. **00K P1–P6** are a deliberately smaller **ablation basis** used to remove one operational invariant at a time.

The fact that two statements sound similar is not enough to establish derivation. An accepted bridge must preserve an identifiable semantic predicate.

---

## 2. Conservation criterion

Let \(F\) be an upstream semantic statement and \(P_i\) an operational principle.

We accept an edge:

\[
F \rightsquigarrow P_i
\]

only when one of the following holds.

### D1 — direct projection

The operational predicate in \(P_i\) is an explicit restriction or executable form of an upstream invariant. Removing that predicate would permit a state forbidden by the upstream control.

### D2 — typed boundary specialization

The upstream invariant requires qualification of a received claim, and \(P_i\) specializes that rule to a particular claim type that has additional externally defined semantics.

This second class is important for **P4**: epistemic qualification alone does not invent legal/organizational authority. It requires that an authority claim remain qualified as a claim; S1/S8 supply the authority-specific source/scope/non-amplification semantics.

No edge is accepted merely because two documents use related vocabulary.

## 4. Derivation of P1–P6

### P1 — Qualified determination and explicit residual

**Operational invariant**

A receiving decision distinguishes sufficient evidence, insufficient/inconclusive evidence and residual state relative to the actual subject–proposition–decision boundary.

**Derivation**

\[
(F\text{-}A \land F\text{-}B \land F\text{-}D)\rightsquigarrow P_1
\]

- F-A forbids treating the bounded representation as exhaustive determination.
- F-B requires unresolved state to remain representable.
- F-D prevents a received record from acquiring a stronger meaning at the receiver.

Therefore P1 is a **direct projection** of the upstream model.

Primary controls: I0/O0 + E0-I/E0-O.  
Research-hypothesis support: H1/H2.

---

### P2 — Bounded unresolved effort and viable oversight

**Operational invariant**

Further determination effort has a finite decision-relevant stopping/fallback relation inside available capacity and useful response time.

**Derivation**

\[
F\text{-}C\rightsquigarrow P_2
\]

I1/O1 are already the in-window/out-of-window forms of bounded determination/window-expansion effort. P2 merely packages the common stopping/viability invariant for ablation.

Therefore P2 is a **direct projection**.

Primary controls: I1/O1 + E1-I/E1-O.  
Research-hypothesis support: H1/H6.

---

### P3 — No false closure from unresolved state

**Operational invariant**

Known unresolved, stale, conflicting or missing material state is not converted into permission or certainty-equivalent closure.

**Derivation**

\[
F\text{-}B\rightsquigarrow P_3
\]

The foundation explicitly separates operational closure from epistemic certainty; I2/E2-I are the control surfaces for false certainty.

Therefore P3 is a **direct projection**.

Primary controls: I2 + E2-I.  
Research-hypothesis support: H1.

---

### P4 — Qualification-preserving authority basis

**Operational invariant**

An action that relies on authority/representation state has a current receiver-verifiable basis sufficient for that action and its non-amplification boundary.

**Derivation class: typed boundary specialization**

\[
(F\text{-}D \land F\text{-}G)
+\text{authority semantics from S1/S8}
\rightsquigarrow P_4
\]

The epistemic foundation does **not** create authority. What it supplies is the rule that a received claim must not lose source, scope, qualification or epistemic meaning in transit/composition. When the proposition being relied on is:

> “actor A is authorized to perform action X under scope/time/conditions Y”

S1 and S8 supply the authority-specific content: origin, standing, applicability, subdelegation and non-amplification.

P4 is therefore not falsely presented as a theorem of epistemology alone. It is the **authority-typed specialization of the received-claim qualification rule**, joined to the canonical authority requirements.

This boundary is exactly why the serious P4 ablation could narrow full lineage to the weaker current decision-sufficient authority/non-amplification invariant.

---

### P5 — Material-change requalification at time of use

**Operational invariant**

A once-valid determination is not reused after a material change without requalification.

**Derivation**

\[
F\text{-}E\rightsquigarrow P_5
\]

The foundation's regime-transition and recursive-window argument makes validity time/frame-relative. P5 is the action-time operational form of that dynamic validity invariant.

Therefore P5 is a **direct projection**.

Research-hypothesis support: H5/H6.

---

### P6 — No local-to-ecosystem promotion or silent substitution

**Operational invariant**

Composition preserves material source dependence, compatibility and scope; repeated/local claims do not become independent ecosystem truth merely through aggregation.

**Derivation**

\[
(F\text{-}F \land F\text{-}G)\rightsquigarrow P_6
\]

O2/E2-O prohibit window/source-local conclusions from becoming ecosystem truth. The General Law of Epistemic Composition prohibits cross-domain cancellation and unqualified substitution.

Therefore P6 is a **direct projection**.

Research-hypothesis support: H2/H3.

## 6. Non-circularity

The bridge is intentionally one-way.

- 01/02 provide the semantic invariants.
- P1–P6 compact them for ablation.
- The existence of P1–P6 is **not** used to prove the truth of 01/02.
- Later tests do not retroactively define the foundation.
- A test failure may force refinement of the operational projection, as occurred with P4, without rewriting the upstream foundational claim.

That separation avoids a circular “the principles are correct because the tests built from the principles pass” argument.

## 8. Claim boundary

This document establishes a **derivation/conservation argument**, not empirical validation.

It does not establish:

- that the Foundation is complete;
- that P1–P6 are the only possible operational basis;
- that an authority relationship is created by epistemic qualification;
- that requirements S1–S14 are satisfied by any implementation; or
- that the six-principle basis is globally minimal under every possible re-formulation.

Those questions are intentionally delegated to later layers of the corpus.
