> **Success claim.** Independently governed local actors may keep different valid local postures, but their shared-resource consequences are composed before action. Material incompatibility triggers scoped requalification, owner-preserving coordination or bounded containment; no local result is promoted into a universal system posture.

## 1. Minimum successful traversal

Using the Central Bridge fixture:

1. each actor keeps its own scope, evidence and posture;
2. a material frame/resource change is detected;
3. the system exposes that the local postures jointly compete for the same non-fungible resource;
4. conflicts and legitimate veto/authority scopes remain explicit;
5. the affected shared decision is requalified;
6. a compatible allocation, bounded hold or targeted escalation is produced before the useful response window closes;
7. unaffected scopes continue rather than being globally frozen.

## 4. Upward extension

- multiple corridors/cities/jurisdictions;
- regional logistics + emergency + public/private mobility;
- multi-site industrial systems;
- federated cloud/edge capacity domains.

Adding scale is admissible while local ownership and shared-resource coupling remain explicit.

## 5. Downward extension

Minimum forms include:

- one production cell with safety + maintenance + production controllers;
- one warehouse intersection with forklifts/humans;
- one GPU cluster with deployment + incident + capacity controllers;
- two independently justified postures competing for one machine/time slot.

Only two local scopes and one shared non-fungible resource are required.

## 6. Horizontal extension

- logistics/loading windows;
- GPU/compute allocation;
- factory-machine scheduling;
- room/bed/staff capacity;
- energy/charging capacity;
- shared maintenance windows.

## 7. Extension boundary

A single controller making one bad decision is not 00F. Ordinary congestion without heterogeneous decision frames is not 00F.

## 8. Transfer result

\[
Conf(R_F)\Rightarrow G_F\Rightarrow \neg F_F.
\]

The success pattern is **local validity + explicit composition + bounded requalification**, not centralized omniscience.
