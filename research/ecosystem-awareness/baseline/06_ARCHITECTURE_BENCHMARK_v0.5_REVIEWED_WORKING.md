The earlier top-level v0.5 publication remains a source/publication alias; this baseline copy keeps the same comparative analysis with only relative links adjusted for its corpus location. **Benchmark review:** v0.5 working successor, 15 September 2026  
**Source baseline:** [v0.4 public reference freeze](../ARCHITECTURE_BENCHMARK_v0.4_PUBLIC_FREEZE.md) and the six controlled EA v0.4 documents listed in the [corpus manifest](../CANONICAL_CORPUS_MANIFEST.md).  
**Status:** research benchmark and test specification; no completed comparative validation, novelty proof, standards adoption, implementation superiority or quantified benefit. This document **does not modify** the frozen baseline or the preserved v0.4 benchmark.

## 1. Reviewed conclusion — a real candidate, not an established win

Ecosystem Awareness (EA) is a **coherent candidate architectural layer for decision-scoped epistemic qualification under finite capacity and ecosystem change**. Its proposed differential is **not** uncertainty propagation, context retrieval, agent coordination, guardrails, attestation or human review in isolation. It is the operation-level combination of:

- qualified decision windows with explicit potentially knowable outside-window state and irreducible/structural residual;
- scope-indexed, non-fungible composition of local and external determinations, including source dependence and mismatched epistemic postures across domains;
- separate epistemic condition and operating posture, so uncertainty is neither laundered into certainty nor automatically equated with a system-wide hold;
- risk/capacity/response-horizon requalification that can seek **more, different or less** evidence and can re-enter the supporting frame after an observed effect.

That combination is plausibly **differential as an explicit architectural responsibility and interface contract**. It could produce operational value: fewer unsupported systemic conclusions, more targeted requalification, preservation of warranted operation, lower low-value evidence/review burden and clearer reasons for action or abstention. These are **benefit hypotheses**, not observed outcomes. The decisive comparison is against a **strongly composed peer**, not against a weak platform that merely transmits a confidence score.

A peer that reproduces the same scoped behaviour with its existing mechanisms and comparable burden narrows the EA distinctiveness claim. Even then, an interoperable profile may remain useful; it would not justify claiming unique functionality. Conversely, an EA improvement on one case does not establish universal superiority or mathematical novelty. The v0.4 conclusion “provisionally complete and materially differentiated” is retained as the historical freeze's judgment, **not upgraded here into a validated result**.

## 2. Neighbouring architectures and what EA must not claim

| Neighbour | Strong function already present | EA question left to test; not an absence assertion |
|---|---|---|
| [A2A protocol](https://a2a-protocol.org/dev/specification/) | Discovery, capability/task/message/artifact interchange, transport and extensibility | Can a realistic A2A extension preserve qualified proposition, domain/window, inherited residual and requalification semantics end to end with no EA-equivalent profile? EA should use its transport, not claim to invent exchange. |
| [MCP architecture](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-06-18/architecture) | Host–client–server boundaries and controlled tool/resource/context access | Can an MCP host's existing source selection/context policy support a decision-relative sufficiency and residual claim under changing mission risk and capacity? Resource access is not itself a proof of epistemic sufficiency. |
| Agent runtimes/SDKs | State, tools, checkpoints, routing, guardrails, interrupts, HITL and traces | Can a strong orchestration policy reproduce selective frame requalification and non-fungible domain composition at comparable complexity/attention cost? These are EA's possible substrates, not deficient “competitors.” |
| [RATS / RFC 9334](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html) and trust frameworks | Evidence, appraisal result and relying-party decision are separated; authority and trust claims have specific owners | Does EA add decision-wide qualification of evidence **outside attestation** without taking identity, authorization, appraisal or enforcement ownership? An attestation result cannot be treated as epistemic truth for unrelated domains. |
| [DebUnc](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1265/) and uncertainty-propagation work | Uncertainty can be communicated and propagated through agents | Does EA's window qualification, structural residual, source-lineage and domain non-substitution alter a supported decision or reduce false closure beyond a calibrated-UQ peer? “Agents communicate uncertainty” is prior art, not EA's claim. |

This table reports **architectural task boundaries**, not a comprehensive negative search over every extension or implementation. Public standards/framework specifications and research papers are moving targets. A comparative claim must cite the exact peer version, configured behaviour and date, rather than infer absence from an overview page.

The nearby [EA/MSCA annex](./01B_EA_MSCA_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md), [EA/Regime Awareness annex](./01C_EA_REGIME_AWARENESS_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md) and [joint operation profile](./01D_EA_MSCA_RA_OPERATION_COMPOSITION_PROFILE_v0.1.md) clarify adjacent responsibilities. They are **candidate integrations**, not extra validated EA differentiators.

## 3. The differential to test: four coupled claims, not seven isolated inventions

The v0.4 benchmark's A–G list is conserved. For comparative testing, its seven elements are grouped into four *coupled* claims; no new “H” is introduced.

| Candidate claim | Conserved v0.4 elements and EA functions | Mechanism proposed | Plausible positive effect | Decisive falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 — scoped non-fungible determination | A/B/C/D; F3/F4/F5/F6/F8 | Preserve four-position in/out-window state, Type 0/1/2 controls, source dependence and domain-specific residual; do not average unrelated claims into a global verdict. | Fewer apparently supported decisions created by duplicated evidence, cross-domain compensation or local-to-global promotion; clearer affected-domain correction. | A strong peer with scope/lineage/UQ and ordinary policy achieves the same decisions and explanatory trace without an equivalent composition layer or extra burden. |
| H2 — proportionate window requalification | E and APQ consequence; F1/F2/F7/F9 | Qualify W(d,t) to consequence, sensitivity, reversibility, residual tolerance, acquisition burden, effective capacity and remaining response time; widen, redirect, stop or narrow on material change. | Fewer stale-frame commitments at high exposure **and/or** less low-value acquisition/review at low exposure, without losing useful response time. | Fixed broad/narrow or strong value-of-information/source-lineage baselines achieve an equal or better decision/resource frontier under matched facts and budgets. |
| H3 — orthogonal assessment and posture | F; F5/F6/F7/F8 | Keep epistemic management condition and residual distinct from Normal/Containment/Migration preparation and from independent actuation authority. | Preserve warranted normal operation under bounded residual; avoid both premature blanket holds and unjustified continuation after a material frame change. | Peer policy separates these conditions with equal correctness, timeliness and operational burden; or EA creates extra indecision without reducing either error. |
| H4 — interoperable re-entry | G; F4/F8/F9 with F1/F2/F5/F6 | Carry scope, window, inherited uncertainty, dependency, capacity and validity through a transport-neutral handoff; an observed effect reopens only failed assumptions. | Less downstream loss of qualification and fewer indiscriminate reruns/escalations; more traceable return-to-operation decisions. | A2A/MCP/attestation/UQ/tracing composition reproduces the handoff and targeted re-entry with no extra semantics or materially simpler implementation. |

**Highest-value differential test:** H1 + H2 together. Preventing false systemic closure is most useful when the system can also decide what additional evidence is worthwhile *before* the response window closes. H3/H4 determine whether that gain can be operated and passed across interfaces. This prioritization is a test strategy, not a claim that A–G are independently novel or that one benefit has been measured.

## 4. Comparator design: a strong baseline and fair resource accounting

Every UC-EA-01…04 record should run at least three arms on the **same frozen case facts, objective, actors, available evidence, authority and action horizon**:

| Arm | Minimum capability | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| B0 — ordinary mechanisms | Realistic orchestration, retrieval, provenance, UQ/confidence, guardrails, HITL and logging | Reveal the failure mode, but **not** support a novelty claim against a strawman. |
| B1 — best plausible peer composition | B0 plus scoped propositions, explicit source lineage/correlation, uncertainty propagation, risk/criticality-aware evidence acquisition, capacity limits, exceptions and permitted requalification rules | The main comparator. Implement these capabilities conscientiously using existing frameworks or peer research. |
| EA — candidate profile | B1's operational/security mechanisms **plus** the EA F1–F9 decision-window, structural-residual, non-fungible composition and selective frame-re-entry semantics | Test incremental behaviour and incremental burden attributable to EA, not to extra data, authority or better implementation quality. |

If B1 must add a semantic layer materially equivalent to EA to match outcomes, the result can support **architecture-role distinctiveness**, but the new layer's code/effort must be counted. If B1 matches without it, the architecture-role claim weakens. If EA uses more evidence, compute, reviewer time or later action, this cost must be visible. Peer strengths should be reused rather than removed to make EA look better.

The benchmark must preregister case branches, decision rule, supported/unsupported outcomes, false-closure criterion, response deadline and resource accounting *before* inspecting comparative results. Blinded adjudication where practical, sensitivity analysis across assumptions and failure-case disclosure prevent a “same story, different thresholds” comparison.

## 5. UC-EA-01…04: outcome measures and potential benefits

| Profile / adverse branch | Primary differential | Observe both error and burden | Positive but bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| UC-EA-01 — material context change after an earlier qualified commitment | H2/H3/H4 | Reuse of stale Q/W, time to identify the invalidated assumption, deadline miss, unnecessary containment, measured outcome vs mere command receipt | More timely **targeted** requalification, with fewer unjustified continuations or needless system-wide holds than B1 at comparable capacity. |
| UC-EA-02 — incomplete/conflicting/partially scoped evidence | H1/H2 | Unsupported certainty, duplicate-source counting, structural-residual preservation, decision change per acquired evidence, acquisition/compute/human cost, remaining response margin | Better distinction between evidence worth obtaining and residual that must stay open; no advantage if B1 does the same at lower cost. |
| UC-EA-03 — nominal human approval under finite effective capacity | H2/H3 | Approval treated as epistemic cure, reviewer minutes, queue/attention depletion, useful response time, false escalation loops | More honest authority/evidence separation and less low-value review load **without** transferring oversight ownership to EA. |
| UC-EA-04 — locally valid but coupled determinations | H1/H4 | False systemic closure, missing domain attribution, copied confidence, scope/dependency trace loss across handoff, corrective-action targeting | Fewer cross-domain substitutions and clearer reasons why a local determination cannot settle the whole decision. |

**APQ stress branch:** compare a high-rated fast source that repeats an upstream record with a lower-rated genuinely independent route that addresses the unresolved domain. B1 must already know source lineage/correlation when available. EA earns value only if its decision-relative selection changes a supported decision or preserves viable response **beyond** a strong cost/latency/QoS/trust selector with that information. Merely preferring “diversity” is not a positive result.

No universal direction is pre-asserted for every metric: wider observation can help one high-consequence case and harm another by latency, regime mixing or capacity consumption. A supported benefit needs an explicit **joint error–resource frontier**, not a single accuracy, confidence or throughput score.

## 6. Claim ladder and decision after the tests

1. **Now — architecture proposition:** EA supplies an explicit way to qualify decision-relevant epistemic state, compose non-fungible scoped evidence and requalify a changing frame under finite capacity. This is a clear potential differential and the source of the benefit hypotheses above.
2. **After component/profile tests — comparative evidence:** report which UC branches changed decisions, residual representation, delays and resource consumption relative to B1. A pass supports only the tested domain, scope, peer configuration and assumptions.
3. **After independent replication/interoperability tests — stronger architecture claim:** only if alternative teams and transports reproduce the gain may the interface/profile be described as a robust comparative contribution.
4. **Novelty/standards/production claims:** require their own searches, formal processes, deployed evidence and institutionally controlled attribution. None follows automatically from a case pass or a public FG-TIDA discussion.

Change F1–F9 or the interface architecture only if tests expose a missing responsibility, unnecessary function, a peer-equivalent simpler construction, or a cost/latency penalty that defeats the claimed value. Until then, the frozen six-document v0.4 baseline remains the controlled reference. The present successor is deliberately more precise about **what EA could add** while keeping the evidentiary burden where it belongs.
