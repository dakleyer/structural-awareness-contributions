## 1. What this register contributes

DDS can benefit from the two article-review initiatives in several concrete ways: a better problem contract, a justified minimum of information for one action, safe reuse of qualified evidence, selective revalidation after a source changes, honest coverage of unknowns, and a more complete account of human and information-acquisition burdens. The purpose here is to turn each possible contribution into a question that can be proved or tested against a competent alternative.

The historical mathematical initiative contributes definitions, conditional lemmas, countermodels and proposed constructions. The other article initiative contributes organizational research questions, measurement proposals, field reconstructions and conjectures. Their scientific objects remain different. A mathematical theorem is not measured human behavior; a simulation is not a company outcome; a common case does not make their evidence independent.

The register currently contains **20 candidate contribution families** with a crosswalk to the **41 available review identities**. It is a first deep synthesis of those reviewed sources and current DDS interfaces, not a claim that every original article has been completely proved or that no other contribution remains to be found. R22–R29 and unexamined parts of compound sources remain future source work. New candidates are added here under stable identifiers rather than by making a second competing contribution register.

### What counts as a contribution

A contribution may be a sharper specification, an independently useful proof obligation, a discriminating Challenge, a measurement contract, an architecture-verification obligation, an implementation test or a result showing that an existing method already suffices. None requires our proposed mechanism to win. A useful source idea may survive correction of its notation or narrowing of a universal claim.

The source of the idea, the reviewer-developed construction and the proposed DDS transfer are recorded separately. Problems already found in a formulation are distinguished from missing proof or measurement. In particular, absence of an additional proof belongs in **development**, not in a list of article refutations.

The method already contains many of these distinctions. Each card therefore identifies an **existing surface** before proposing an addition. Linking an article is not a new mechanism. For each future claimed benefit ask which unresolved case, executable obligation, measured burden or stronger proof the contribution actually adds.

DDS evidence mode is independent of stage. A mathematical or documentary specification can support Stage A within its declared scope. A formal model can support Stage B if it actually verifies architecture against the frozen specification. Scientific studies of incentives, workers, long-term competence, organizational forecasting or physical heat retain independent methods and permissions; they are not automatically DDS Stage C.

## 3. Readiness, acceptance and priorities

**All cards initially have status PROPOSED.** Their source lemmas or existing fixtures may have narrower evidence, identified in individual records; the proposed transfer itself has not been admitted or executed by publishing this register.

- **P1:** first bounded formalization or technical comparison worth preparing; not a launch authorization or completed test-ready specification.
- **P2:** useful follow-on research after source/contract conditions and strong comparator details are fixed.
- **P3:** longer observational, temporal or human research; avoid converting the hypothesis into an immediate technical claim.

Future status changes use: PROPOSED → SPECIFICATION_READY → TESTED_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE, or OPEN, NULL/NO_INCREMENTAL_GAIN, SUPERSEDED or WITHDRAWN with a reason. Each change cites the dated evidence and scope. Historical entries remain recoverable. These are register maintenance labels, not a new DDS stage taxonomy.

A comparison must predeclare required correctness/risk limits and resource/latency envelope. Distinguish a **correctness gate** from a **differential finding**. Passing a condition says whether the declared object satisfies it; superiority requires a matched comparison. Equal or cheaper strong incumbents, missing admissible data and no useful difference are legitimate outcomes. Do not select thresholds after seeing results.

### DDS-PC-001 — Minimal decision-sufficient evidence

**Source reviews:** [R01](#source-r01), [R03](#source-r03), [R14](#source-r14), [D19](#source-d19). **Status:** PROPOSED. **Priority:** P1.

**Existing surface and source idea.** 00M §4 already defines task-relative sufficiency. R01/R03 add qualifier selection; the proposed contribution is an implementable, costed minimal-certificate problem.

**Proposed contribution.** For a independently fixed receiving question q, identify a lawful evidence set that separates source states requiring different actions; preserve scope/version/rights in the certificate.

**DDS object and stage.** A; later B checker and C receiving integration. Existing receiving surface: [current corpus source](./baseline/00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md#41-when-a-summary-is-sufficient-for-an-exact-answer).

**First discriminating study.** Use a finite controlled state family, enumerate equal-summary/different-action pairs, and compare full-state evidence, a minimal eligible witness set and a direct trusted verdict. Add irrelevant evidence and impossible-by-budget cases.

**Strong comparator.** A competent sufficient-statistic/certificate or policy implementation with the same source access; record verification, acquisition and decode costs.

**Evidence and acceptance.** False release, false hold, certificate size, queries, verification time and total eligible cost. A candidate must preserve required decisions and show a declared benefit or an honest tie. Freeze the substantive rule, any comparison margin and the resource envelope before execution.

**Development needed.** Prove factorization on stated domain and minimality under declared cost; implement distinct comparators. Pair-cover finite checks are bounded evidence, not open-world enumeration.

**Limit or error to avoid.** Source-relative sufficiency is not new mathematics; revealing evaluator-only answers is not a cheap candidate certificate.

### DDS-PC-002 — Receiving-context requalification

**Source reviews:** [R08](#source-r08), [R09](#source-r09), [R21](#source-r21), [D14](#source-d14), [D19](#source-d19). **Status:** PROPOSED. **Priority:** P1.

**Existing surface and source idea.** 00M and HC-HID already qualify information relative to the receiving process. Projection studies motivate discriminating tests for an actual source-to-recipient contract.

**Proposed contribution.** Make a source PASS reusable only when its question, scope, semantic meaning, version, purpose, authority and validity window support the new receiving action.

**DDS object and stage.** A; B adapter conformance; C pinned handoff effects. Existing receiving surface: [current corpus source](./baseline/01K_A01_HUMAN_CAPACITY_HID_COMPONENT_SPEC_v0.1.md).

**First discriminating study.** Pair identical source verdicts with different receiving questions/purposes; include lawful reuse, changed version, revoked grant, independent support and genuinely unknown qualification.

**Strong comparator.** A mature provenance and policy gate with verified inputs; do not compare against a gate deliberately stripped of ownership or clock checks.

**Evidence and acceptance.** Wrong release/hold, legitimate reuse, review/query burden and post-change correctness. Freeze the substantive rule, any comparison margin and the resource envelope before execution.

**Development needed.** Specify what each field proves, what remains unknown and which authority grants action; implement receiver independently from oracle.

**Limit or error to avoid.** Neither valid source output nor absence of rejection grants permission; an access restriction may be proper rather than system failure.

**Existing surface and source idea.** R02 studies local conflict separately from authorization; R18 motivates refined partition atoms. Four-valued evidence and truth maintenance are established neighbours.

## 6. Three worked proposal kernels

These are reviewer-developed illustrative constructions for preparing the cards, not original-author proofs or newly executed DDS results.

### 6.1 Action-relative sufficiency rather than full knowledge

Let a bounded declared world have three bits: producer status a, rightful external grant b and freshness t. The producer summary is a; the receiving question is q=b AND t. Two states can share a and require different receiving decisions, so that summary is insufficient. Adding both b and t is sufficient on this declared family. A direct trusted verdict may also suffice. If illustrative costs are b=2, t=1 and trusted verdict=5, the combined certificate costs 3; this is a constructed cost example, not measured savings.

What DDS should test is whether a legitimate candidate can obtain and verify those distinctions at lower total burden than a competent incumbent, including acquisition, authority, cache and latency costs. Keeping all state data is a correct comparator. A producer PASS does not itself answer q. The future Stage B question is whether an adapter realizes this contract; Stage C observes its actual effects under the original Challenge. This kernel supports DDS-PC-001/002/014.

### 6.2 Witness reuse with permissions and real costs

Suppose two receiving judgments require witness sets W1 and W2. Under a frozen meaning/version/rights contract, only witnesses in the **eligible** intersection can be shared. A proposed cost comparison is:

```text
cost_without_reuse = cost_of_first_acquisition_and_check
                  + cost_of_second_acquisition_and_check

cost_with_reuse = cost_of_union_acquisition
                + required_checking_for_each_recipient
                + reuse_lookup/storage/coordination
                + invalidation_and_requalification
```

A positive result requires the second quantity to be lower while all required judgments remain correct. It may be equal or higher, especially with incompatible permissions or rapidly changing sources. Reuse never transfers authority by itself. This is a cost ledger to implement, not an assumption that recombination is free or that thermodynamic entropy is conserved. It supports DDS-PC-007/008 and should be contrasted with real provenance/cache mechanisms.

### 6.3 Capacity freed is not yet an effective decision

A configuration saves two operator-hours but still requires a competent authorized reviewer within a one-hour action window. If the saved operator time belongs to a person without the knowledge or mandate, the authorized response path remains unavailable. A legitimate standing delegation plus an independently verified review schedule may change that outcome. The experiment must distinguish task-time saved, gross arrivals/service at the reviewer, expert contribution, binding authority and effective action before deadline.

A conventional workflow with proper staffing/delegation is a strong positive control. A successful escalation receipt without timely effect is insufficient. The architecture can first be tested as a bounded specification/reference model; empirical worker benefit and retention require separate instruments. This kernel supports DDS-PC-011/012/020.

### Three immediate candidate refinements

1. **Representation sufficiency:** state the receiving property P and representation E. A downstream function exists exactly when P is constant on E's fibres. Pair witnesses can expose a missing distinction; acquisition or a narrower task can resolve it. Compare against a mature provenance-aware baseline; the existence proof does not predict acquisition cost or deployment benefit.
2. **Action-utility hygiene:** certify non-inferiority against a declared null action, admissible states, stakeholders and horizon. Do not infer dominance over another design from that property. Safe=(0,1), null=(0,0), competitor=(-1,100) is a finite counterexample: the robust guarantee is stronger while expected utility need not be greater. This changes a comparative proposition, not the legitimacy of scoped safety design.
3. **Time and shared dependencies:** validity can return after a regime departure; one rule can cover many states; a shared historical anchor does not establish a correlation coefficient; a transient load burst is not infinite backlog. Record actual contracts, rule semantics, dependence evidence and time window before accepting those deductions.
