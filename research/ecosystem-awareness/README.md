# Ecosystem Awareness — public entry point

The **complete Ecosystem Awareness corpus** is in one [canonical reading folder](./baseline/README.md). Its index explains the problem, the present six-document argument, the candidate extension annexes, validation profiles, five research-lineage articles, controls and provenance. Start there; this page is only the route from the wider repository.

EA asks whether the evidence, residual uncertainty, assumptions and response capacity supporting **a particular decision** remain sufficient when its ecosystem is partly known and changing. It does not assume that local correctness, more agents, or a human approval necessarily settles the whole decision. Its candidate contribution is scoped epistemic qualification and proportionate requalification, to be tested against strong neighbouring architectures.

The [DAOS case](../../submissions/itu-fg-tida/2026-theme-contributions/delegated-authority-os-under-context-change/README.md), [MSCA corpus](../../standards/minimum-sufficient-control/README.md) and [EWS corpus](../regime-awareness/minimalistic-early-warning-systems/README.md) are independent sources with their own indexes. EA's annexes [01B](./baseline/01B_EA_MSCA_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md), [01C](./baseline/01C_EA_REGIME_AWARENESS_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md), [01D](./baseline/01D_EA_MSCA_RA_OPERATION_COMPOSITION_PROFILE_v0.1.md) and [01E](./baseline/01E_EA_DAOS_MODEL_CASE_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md) document proposed architectural relationships without merging those programmes.

For version, freeze, publication and open verification questions, use the [corpus manifest](./baseline/CANONICAL_CORPUS_MANIFEST.md). Earlier gateway prose is preserved [here](./baseline/EA_PARENT_INDEX_SOURCE_2026-09-15.md); this short page is not another research document.
