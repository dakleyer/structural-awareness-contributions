# Ecosystem Awareness — public reading and integration map

## A human route through the corpus

For the actual ordered main documents and annexes in the corpus folder, start with the [primary baseline index](./baseline/README.md). This map is a secondary reading aid across programmes and evidence layers.

Ecosystem Awareness (EA) begins with a practical question: **for this mission and this decision, what do we need to know and control well enough to proceed, what remains unresolved, and what would make us reconsider?** An ecosystem cannot be fully known, and it changes while we operate. EA therefore seeks a minimally sufficient, explicitly qualified control architecture under uncertainty—not maximum information collection or a claim to have mapped the entire world.

Read the main argument in this order:

1. **Why is EA needed?** Start with the [integrated Foundational Theory 01 v0.5](./baseline/01_FOUNDATIONAL_THEORY_v0.5_INTEGRATED.md): it carries the complete earlier v0.4 derivation and the three developed origins/relations — residual indeterminacy, ecosystem change, and integration with neighbouring approaches — in one current working document, with the dictionary. The separate [01A source note](./baseline/01A_FOUNDATIONAL_DUAL_ORIGIN_NOTE_v0.1.md) and [v0.4 public snapshot](./FOUNDATIONAL_THEORY_v0.4_PUBLIC_FREEZE.md) remain preserved for provenance, not as two competing foundations.
2. **What is our honest position toward the unknown?** The controlled [Epistemic Safety Principles & Control Matrix](./baseline/02_EPISTEMIC_SAFETY_PRINCIPLES_CONTROL_MATRIX_v0.4.part01.md) distinguishes the known, identified unknowns, reasonably obtainable knowledge and structural residual beyond the active window. Its six internal and six received-signal controls prevent locally plausible evidence from becoming an unjustified global conclusion. Continue through [part 2](./baseline/02_EPISTEMIC_SAFETY_PRINCIPLES_CONTROL_MATRIX_v0.4.part02.md) and [part 3](./baseline/02_EPISTEMIC_SAFETY_PRINCIPLES_CONTROL_MATRIX_v0.4.part03.md).
3. **How does EA actually work?** The [Functional Architecture](./baseline/03_FUNCTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_v0.4.part01.md) moves from mission/decision qualification and finite observation to evidence qualification, scoped composition, operating posture, smallest useful requalification and outcome learning: F1–F9. Continue through [part 2](./baseline/03_FUNCTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_v0.4.part02.md) and [part 3](./baseline/03_FUNCTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_v0.4.part03.md). The [general interface architecture](./baseline/04_FUNCTIONAL_INTERFACES_AGENTIC_SECURITY_v0.4.part01.md) describes how those functions exchange qualified information with agents and security components ([parts 2](./baseline/04_FUNCTIONAL_INTERFACES_AGENTIC_SECURITY_v0.4.part02.md) and [3](./baseline/04_FUNCTIONAL_INTERFACES_AGENTIC_SECURITY_v0.4.part03.md)). The [cross-theme contracts](./baseline/05_CROSS_THEME_INTERFACE_CONTRACTS_v0.4.part01.md) are a **provisional FG-TIDA-oriented example**, not a restriction of that general architecture ([parts 2](./baseline/05_CROSS_THEME_INTERFACE_CONTRACTS_v0.4.part02.md) and [3](./baseline/05_CROSS_THEME_INTERFACE_CONTRACTS_v0.4.part03.md)).
4. **What concrete situation challenges the architecture?** Read the separately preserved [Delegated Authority OS under Context Change parent case](../../submissions/itu-fg-tida/2026-theme-contributions/delegated-authority-os-under-context-change/): mobility is its smallest instantiation, not the case's name or its limit. Its extensibility and CH-S1…14 challenges lead first to the [EA model-case architectural interface annex](./baseline/01E_EA_DAOS_MODEL_CASE_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md), then to the detailed [DAO/DAOS → EA case and test guide](./validation/DAOS_EA_USE_CASES_MASTERCLASS_v0.1.md). One T0–T2 situation produces four different EA validation lenses, collected in the [validation family](./validation/VALIDATION_PROFILE_FAMILY_v0.5_FROZEN.md) and [UC-EA-01…04](./validation/README.md); these are not four submitted FG-TIDA Use Cases.
5. **How does EA connect to control and early warning?** [EA ↔ MSCA](./baseline/01B_EA_MSCA_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md) asks what bounded observation, coordination and intervention may support the mission. [EA ↔ Regime Awareness](./baseline/01C_EA_REGIME_AWARENESS_INTERFACE_ANNEX_v0.1.md) asks whether evidence of changing conditions requires the frame to be requalified, without treating a detector as the authority to act. The [joint operation profile](./baseline/01D_EA_MSCA_RA_OPERATION_COMPOSITION_PROFILE_v0.1.md) tests whether evidence, control envelope, authority, uncertainty, cost and response horizon belong to the **same** action and remain compatible.
6. **What would count as progress—or failure?** The [reviewed benchmark v0.5](./baseline/06_ARCHITECTURE_BENCHMARK_v0.5_REVIEWED_WORKING.md) states the possible differential and benefits **as propositions to test** against a strong peer composition. The [validation profiles](./validation/README.md) and interoperability test expose failures, costs and negative cases. The earlier [v0.4 benchmark](./ARCHITECTURE_BENCHMARK_v0.4_PUBLIC_FREEZE.md) remains preserved for comparison.

That is the main reading line. [Lineage and Articles 01–05](./lineage/) show how the architecture was derived; [governance](./governance/) and the [canonical manifest](./CANONICAL_CORPUS_MANIFEST.md) show what is frozen, what is a working addition and what still needs publication verification. The current 01 working successor is a **single integrated file**. Controlled v0.4 documents 02–05 remain in ordered parts; those source versions still require continuous, revision-checked canonical reader files.

## Core architecture versus one standards-discussion example

The v0.4 **Functional Architecture** fixes F1–F9. The v0.4 **Functional Interfaces & Agentic Security Integration** describes a technology-neutral landscape (O1–O6 and S1–S13) and external handoffs. The v0.4 **Cross-Theme Interface Contracts** explore producer/consumer exchanges in an FG-TIDA discussion setting. They are a worked application of interface reasoning, **not** the definition of every possible architecture or an obligation for neighbouring themes to adopt EA.

The EA design question is decision-scoped: what must be justified for this mission, what can be qualified in a finite observation window, what remains structurally unresolved, whether differently scoped results compose, and when the frame or posture must be requalified. EA does not claim a complete ecosystem model, a super-controller, or action authority merely because an epistemic handoff exists.

## Validation and falsification route

The [validation family](./validation/VALIDATION_PROFILE_FAMILY_v0.5_FROZEN.md) binds four profiles to the same parent-case facts: [UC-EA-01](./validation/UC-EA-01_v0.3_FROZEN.md) tests operating-frame requalification; [UC-EA-02](./validation/UC-EA-02_v0.6_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests bounded determination and acquisition-pathway qualification; [UC-EA-03](./validation/UC-EA-03_v0.4_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests effective human oversight; [UC-EA-04](./validation/UC-EA-04_v0.5_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests scoped composition. [EA-ITP-01](./validation/EA-ITP-01_v0.1_FROZEN.md) is the interoperability test profile. The [reviewed benchmark](./baseline/06_ARCHITECTURE_BENCHMARK_v0.5_REVIEWED_WORKING.md) requires fair comparison with a strong peer composition and records costs, errors and negative results rather than treating plausible diagrams as validation.
