## Core architecture versus one standards-discussion example

The v0.4 **Functional Architecture** fixes F1–F9. The v0.4 **Functional Interfaces & Agentic Security Integration** describes a technology-neutral landscape (O1–O6 and S1–S13) and external handoffs. The v0.4 **Cross-Theme Interface Contracts** explore producer/consumer exchanges in an FG-TIDA discussion setting. They are a worked application of interface reasoning, **not** the definition of every possible architecture or an obligation for neighbouring themes to adopt EA.

The EA design question is decision-scoped: what must be justified for this mission, what can be qualified in a finite observation window, what remains structurally unresolved, whether differently scoped results compose, and when the frame or posture must be requalified. EA does not claim a complete ecosystem model, a super-controller, or action authority merely because an epistemic handoff exists.

|---|---|---|---|
| [Cost of Clarity / RUP](../../applied-research/cost-of-clarity-rup/) | Whether information, distinctions and authority are adequate **before commitment**. | A sealed commitment basis, unresolved conditions, authority/evidence provenance and later changes could inform EA's decision frame; EA may request requalification when they become relevant. | This is OÜ JUBAP applied research under its own EIS assessment route. It is not an EA validation set or an EA-controlled artifact. |
| [Regime Awareness](../regime-awareness/) | Whether supporting conditions remain valid **during operation**. | Scoped context/regime evidence can help EA qualify or requalify a frame; EA can supply decision scope and targeted qualification requests. | [TIDA — Delegated Authority OS under Context Change](../../submissions/itu-fg-tida/2026-theme-contributions/delegated-authority-os-under-context-change/) | Extensible parent case with bounded mobility instantiation, challenges and ToR traceability. | Its T0–T2 facts and CH-S1…14 expose four EA validation lenses. Read the [masterclass](./validation/DAOS_EA_USE_CASES_MASTERCLASS_v0.1.md). | The mobility scene is one minimum instantiation; the four UC-EA files are architecture-validation profiles, not four FG-TIDA filings. |
| [FG-TIDA theme contributions](../../submissions/itu-fg-tida/2026-theme-contributions/) | Public contributor discussion on oversight and ecosystem defence. | A place to test provisional cross-theme contracts and provenance. | Contributor comments and case material are not an adopted FG-TIDA architecture. |

These arrows are **candidate information dependencies**, not evidence that the programmes are already integrated end to end. Cost of Clarity does not cause every regime change; external shocks and endogenous change can defeat an initially sound frame. EA, Regime Awareness and MSCA have distinct scientific objects and testing burdens.

## Validation and falsification route

The [validation family](./validation/VALIDATION_PROFILE_FAMILY_v0.5_FROZEN.md) binds four profiles to the same parent-case facts: [UC-EA-01](./validation/UC-EA-01_v0.3_FROZEN.md) tests operating-frame requalification; [UC-EA-02](./validation/UC-EA-02_v0.6_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests bounded determination and acquisition-pathway qualification; [UC-EA-03](./validation/UC-EA-03_v0.4_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests effective human oversight; [UC-EA-04](./validation/UC-EA-04_v0.5_MAINTENANCE_FREEZE.md) tests scoped composition. [EA-ITP-01](./validation/EA-ITP-01_v0.1_FROZEN.md) is the interoperability test profile. The [reviewed benchmark](./ARCHITECTURE_BENCHMARK_v0.5_REVIEWED_WORKING.md) requires fair comparison with a strong peer composition and records costs, errors and negative results rather than treating plausible diagrams as validation.
