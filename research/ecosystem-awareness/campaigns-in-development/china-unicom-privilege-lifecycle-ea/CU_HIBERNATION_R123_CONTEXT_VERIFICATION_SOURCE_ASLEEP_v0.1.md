# CU-HIB-01 — Hibernation, Context Verification and a Second Sleeping Source

**Focused R1 → R2 → R3 Challenge for discussion of FG-TIDA Theme #23**  
**Status:** source-grounded *working scenario*, 14 October 2026 · **NOT EXECUTED / NOT ADOPTED / NOT PREREGISTERED**. Independently prepared as a potential contribution for discussion, not an agreed China Unicom collaboration or an implemented source system.

## 1. Begin with Ying's actual question — not a general EA argument

[FG-TIDA Theme #23](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23), proposed by **Ying Wang (@Snoopywy) and Ye Tao (China Unicom)**, concerns AI-agent authorization and privilege lifecycle management. The particularly important transition is **hibernation → activation**: suspended privileges cannot be restored merely because an agent has resumed operation.

In response to the [initial three questions and the original hypothetical case](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-5915019756), [Ying said on 14 October](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6164646309) that restoration should reassess original intent/scope, resource dependency graph, current service criticality, network ACL/policy, credential rotations and runtime telemetry. If evidence is inadequate, unconditional high-impact restoration is inappropriate; a restricted grant, reauthorization or human review may be indicated **if permitted by the actual source policy**. She recognizes *partially verifiable* cross-domain conditions and envisages **optional** ecosystem context signals.

[Zhao's 9 October input](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6072991126) suggests recording the factual *restore condition* when the privilege is suspended. [Ying's 09:49 clarification](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6165603429) accepts that anchor and says that, at activation, the system **first validates the predeclared condition, then derives intent under current context**. The ordering comes from her discussion, not from an adopted normative implementation. Zhao suggested an anchor *rather than* rederiving intent; Ying's proposal adds the second step. Both contributions are credited; neither is being challenged here.

**The next question is not whether these controls are sensible. They are. The question is what happens when they need current context that another legitimately governed participant cannot yet verify.** This is consistent with earlier [UCON usage-control research (Park and Sandhu, 2004)](https://doi.org/10.1145/984334.984339) on ongoing authorizations, mutable attributes, obligations and conditions. UCON provides **prior art for R2-type continuous checking**, not a specification for recursively suspended evidence producers.

## 2. Freeze the problem (R1), repair it (R2), then change a condition of the repair (R3)

**Same requested action Q throughout:** agent A has a previously legitimate, suspended authority to \`isolate(R)\` for incident X. It asks to restore/use that authority at activation. Grant identity and stated purpose need not change. A separate current source-owner policy must determine whether isolation is allowed, forbidden, restricted or subject to reapproval under the actual dependency status.

| Traversal | Concrete situation | Correct methodological treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 — original hibernation gap / problem freeze** | A previously authorized isolation agent sleeps. While it is suspended, another critical service S may become dependent on R. On waking, historical identity/credential/policy continuity by itself cannot show the current action remains applicable. | Fix the request, original scope, authorization owner, environmental facts and required legitimate outcomes. **This is the problem**, not evidence of an actually observed China Unicom failure. A purely historical check can provide a constructed negative R1. |
| **R2 — strong conventional repair / solution freeze** | Use the source-discussed suspension-time restore anchor, **validate it**, then **rederive intent in current context**. Permit every legitimate native graph/freshness/telemetry/NACL/credential check, generic discovery, source query, human escalation and restricted-grant check that the defender justifies. Demonstrate correct restoration when context is established and correct restriction/reapproval when a material change is established. | **A fully competent R2 can solve the known hibernation example. Credit that success.** Once the solution has actually been demonstrated, freeze its entire configuration, including generic controls, legitimate observation/source access, adaptation, deadlines and costs. A written design is **not** an executed freeze. |
| **R3 — only if B is itself a #23-governed evidence-producing agent (conditional candidate)** | On A's subsequent activation, the owner-approved rule requires current proposition P. **If and only if** B is an AI agent whose authority to issue/refresh P is governed by #23, B's own declared lifecycle state may suspend that authority. Specify whether B is **normally hibernating** or **security-suspended/revoked**; whether its historical snapshot remains admissible is a separate policy/freshness/issuer-standing question. A CMDB, PIP, independent service or read-only data plane outside that lifecycle makes this the **ordinary source/attribute availability case**, not a new R3. | Freeze full R2 and all lawful source methods. Determine if independently authorized reads, CAEP/change notices, prior still-valid evidence or B's ordered reactivation can satisfy P. A missing reply by itself **never proves B's state**. If B's governed lifecycle prevents every required source-authorized path, report the exact unverifiable premise and available review, not an automatic native failure. |

**R3 scope gate — to decide before any experiment:** B must be established as a #23-governed AI agent with a suspendible *evidence-production* privilege, not just a policy-information provider or CMDB. Its lifecycle state must be established from an authorized lifecycle source. Its suspension class (routine hibernation versus security quarantine/revocation) and its impact on prior assertions must be defined by the issuer/source policy. **If any of these elements is unestablished, downgrade this R3 candidate to a known R2 data-availability case or mark its admissibility NOT_ESTABLISHED.**

The R3 candidate is **about the lifecycle-dependent authority to produce the particular evidence required by an R2 check**—not about inventing a new action permission, suppressing ordinary source checks or changing policy to force a particular outcome. It must be established prospectively that R2's true defended coverage did not already settle it. The original direct S→R case remains **R2 calibration** after repair.

**Freeze discipline:** (i) freeze the source-owned **problem/desired action**, (ii) repair and demonstrate the **R2 solution**, (iii) freeze *all* R2 facilities/rights, then (iv) introduce the distinct R3 source/availability change. Any later specialized fix becomes another R2 generation. This is the [DDS Stage A Challenge/trajectory method](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/DDS_CHALLENGE_TRAJECTORY_PROFILE_v0.1.md), with parallel [UC-21: when controls work but the system fails](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/use-cases/issues/21); the original UC-21's separate local route names and its results are **not** transferred as proof of this Theme #23 case.

## 3. At R3: what could A actually know?

**Source roles are separate.** A is the requesting agent; its issuer/authorization PDP owns `isolate(R)` applicability and its PEP enforces the authorized decision. **PIP/CMDB/infrastructure may supply P without becoming a #23-governed agent.** For the special R3 variant alone, B is explicitly stipulated *and independently admitted* as an agent whose lifecycle-issued authority to produce or refresh the minimum proposition P can be suspended. The factual data may remain stored and independently queryable. Who may read it, whether B must reactivate, and whether an older statement remains authoritative depend on the respective policy and evidence owners; do not silently merge source owner, B, PIP, PDP, and PEP.

**Do not treat evidence labels as six mutually exclusive states.** The evaluator independently records (a) **world truth** about P; (b) **B's lifecycle state and certified issuer status** (normal hibernation, security suspension/revocation, active, or unknown); (c) **source availability** (reachable/unreachable/unknown); (d) **evidence freshness** (current/stale/unknown), **coverage** (sufficient/partial/unknown), and **agreement** (consistent/contested/unknown); and (e) **whether the minimum owner-required P is ESTABLISHED, NOT_ESTABLISHED or INCONSISTENT**. The same case can be simultaneously STALE, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE and PARTIAL. `INDETERMINATE` is retained separately as a possible **native PDP evaluation outcome**, dependent on its actual policy, not a magic global label for every missing fact. A's valid grant alone does not establish P. The [OASIS XACML 3.0 Standard with approved errata](https://docs.oasis-open.org/xacml/3.0/errata01/os/xacml-3.0-core-spec-errata01-os-complete.html) already defines a PDP **Indeterminate** outcome when evaluation cannot complete, including missing attributes **when the policy requires their presence** (`MustBePresent=true`); this is a valid native R2 precedent. Our richer evidence-state labels are **analytical qualifiers**, not six invented normative XACML verdicts.

| R3 evidence profile | What A may justifiably establish | Decision question |
|---|---|---|
| **B is infrastructure, not a #23 agent** | A CMDB/PIP or independent data-plane source has no presumed agent hibernation privilege. | Classify as ordinary R2 evidence availability, not recursive R3. |
| **B is independently registered as normally hibernating** | Its issuer/lifecycle source confirms that B's evidence-production privilege is dormant. A prior P statement remains usable only under independently approved validity/freshness and issuer-standing rules; an independent authorized data-plane query may still be available. | Validate exactly which route lawfully establishes P and whether B must reactivate first. |
| **B is independently security-suspended or revoked** | The issuer reports suspected compromise or withdrawn source authority, which may also undermine historical P. Timestamp freshness alone is not enough to restore trust. | Ask whether owner policy invalidates earlier assertions and requires independent evidence, new approval or a clean reissue; never assume the snapshot remains admissible. |
| **B does not respond; its state is unknown** | Non-response is a channel observation, not a verified lifecycle event. [Chandra–Toueg (1996)](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/info/people/sam/FDpapers.html) concerns fallible suspicion in asynchronous crash-prone systems. | Treat as ordinary R2 availability uncertainty unless a source-authorized lifecycle record separately establishes B's actual suspension. |
| **B awake / current and sufficient source checks** | The owner-defined dependency question is currently verified within the accepted scope; if there is a relevant new edge, the check may reveal it. | Does the R2 source-owned policy permit full isolation, require restriction, or authorize continuation under an explicit exception? |
| **Known R2 control — STALE / ordinary SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE** | P's existing snapshot cannot meet the source-approved freshness/coverage rule, or an ordinary source query times out. No extra lifecycle dependency is established. | A competent R2 should already preserve insufficient evidence and apply its legitimate fallback. **Do not count this as an uncovered R3.** |
| **R3 candidate — B's privilege to answer SUSPENDED** | The minimum required P is not currently certified because B is not authorized to refresh/respond until B's lifecycle permits it. Last snapshot may be fresh enough, a separately authorized data-plane query may exist, or B may need to reactivate first; all three possibilities depend on a real policy. | Which actor may lawfully restore evidential access, in which order, with what bounded delay and minimum current proposition? Does frozen R2 already handle it? |
| **B asleep / other live participants have qualified indications** | C reports a new version/partial relation; D has a related service-criticality signal; one report may be a replay of another. **Some evidence of contextual movement may be available without a complete dependency answer.** | Can a recipient recognize what is genuinely new, what is correlated and **which original assumption is no longer demonstrably current**, in time for authorized review? |
| **B asleep / no lawful fresh source or trustworthy signal** | A and EA cannot distinguish whether a material dependency exists. Silence cannot certify absence of change. | State **NOT_ESTABLISHED / UNKNOWN** and preserve the legitimate bounded owner decision. **Neither native policy nor EA gets evaluator-private truth.** |
| **Unchanged context / legitimate positive** | An independently defined source-policy coverage rule establishes current sufficiency for the specific isolation decision. **No signal by itself is not such a rule.** | May the legitimate system proceed within its original rights without needless global suspension? |

An expired receipt, a sleeping producer whose privilege is suspended, an ordinary network timeout and a contested source assertion are **not interchangeable** uncertainties. **Silence does not establish B's lifecycle state.** The failure-detector analogy above applies only when inferring state from missing replies, **not** when an authorized lifecycle issuer has supplied a valid suspension record. If A has incomplete data about S→R and B is asleep, that does not itself prove S→R changed. Nor does A's valid credential prove that the dependent policy condition is still satisfied. [IETF RFC 9334](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html) provides a useful **separation of evidence, verifier appraisal, freshness and relying-party decision**; it does **not** attest to the completeness of a remote service-dependency graph. [NISTIR 8179](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8179/final) supports a disciplined classification of service/system criticality, but does not determine the live S→R edge or confer authorization on A.

### A simple, traceable observation example (candidate only)

- At suspension \`T1\`: original mission \`GO\` and restore-condition reference \`RC1\` are pinned; B's last dependency snapshot is dated \`v17\`.
- At reactivation `T3`: A's grant and some applicable checks are current. **B's permission to produce a current proposition P remains suspended.** The first restoration-condition validation cannot simply be marked PASS; whether Ying's second current-intent step can start, wait or use an alternative lawful source is exactly the source-owned question.
- Two other authorized participants may send complementary *qualified* observations: C notes a changed upstream service-profile version \`v18\`, and D reports a criticality/route review touching R. Those messages **may justify checking the relation**, but may be stale, dependent or semantically insufficient to prove isolation is prohibited.
- The native source/PDP now must distinguish **“historical GO still requested”**, **“grant still valid”** and **“current applicability established or not established”**. A domain-authorized hold/review, separately granted observation or continuing full action is determined by its **actual policy and accepted evidence**, **not by an EA signal alone**.

This is **not a claim that C/D already provide these signals in a China Unicom deployment**. Their possible source payloads, visibility, timing, materiality and owner rights are objects for a future bounded Challenge.

## 4. One brief plausibility connection — not a defence of EA

A relevant possibility arises when **several independently governed agents retain complementary *qualified* indications of uncertainty**. Their non-fungible composition can sometimes reveal that the existing basis for a particular decision is incomplete, outdated or worth rechecking—even though no participant, including EA, knows the full hidden context.

**A non-fungible composition example—not a graph join:** B's historical functional output **A** asserts P only within B's named monitoring coverage; its **B** qualification identifies known exclusions. C's current functional output **A** reports a partner-service version or operating-mode change, **without claiming an S→R dependency**. D supplies a grounded but still uncharacterized **C** exploration frontier affecting that partner-service scope, while the effect of B's currently suspended evidential privilege may be **D** if A has no effective lawful evaluation route, **C** if a grounded but uncharacterized route exists, or **B** if a source-valid evaluation route is already characterized. If—and only if—the scopes can be legitimately related, the combined positions identify **which assumption of A's restoration check is no longer sufficiently established and which authorized party might clarify it**. Neither the change notice nor the exploration frontier substitutes for B's sole source-owned proposition P unless its owner expressly permits an alternative. This is not transitive graph closure, a determination that S→R exists, a universal score, or an authorization decision. If source mappings are unavailable, the composition itself remains NOT_ESTABLISHED. A capable conventional comparator can make the same qualified assessment.

For example, A has a valid authorization record; B's dependency assessment is stale; C reports a bounded source-version change; D recognizes a separately scoped criticality indicator. A well-grounded composition preserves **who said what, for which question, from what source/version and with which limitations**. It does **not** add four uncertainties to obtain a probability or turn a suspicion into certainty. It may yield: **“current applicability has not been established for this material dependency; requalify through this source/owner.”** Sometimes a compatible source already carries enough to establish a **specific changed fact**, but not necessarily the policy consequence. At other times the composition is itself **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.

This is the limited mechanism examined in the existing scientific-plausibility work: **[00N — Can Ecosystem Awareness Work?](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00N_FROM_MECHANISM_TO_REQUIREMENTS_SCIENTIFIC_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.7_RESEARCH_NOTE.md)**, with the mathematical semantics and limits in [00M](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/main/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md). They are **plausibility/research notes**, not evidence of a validated deployed EA differential. The existing **T1–T4 sufficiency conditions** correspond, in brief, to **material awareness within declared scope; qualified determination/handoff; source-authorized bounded response; and timely viable minimum-sufficient requalification**. Their realization and possible B2/B3 differential remain experimental, not assumed.

This possibility is **complementary**, not a replacement for the R2 native checks. A conventional system with an equivalent qualified multi-source composition deserves full credit.

## 5. Specific questions for one R3 experiment — not a universal claim

**Experiment object:** With the same frozen, fully competent R2 and original Q, can the system distinguish **(i) current valid restoration; (ii) verified material change; (iii) material dependency question not establishable because another source is asleep**, and avoid promoting (iii) to a false permission or unnecessary global denial? Does **optional, prospectively fixed qualified composition** of other participants' reports make a decision-relevant difference on (iii) without false certainty?

**Control distinction:** a stale snapshot or ordinary timeout already belongs to the known R2 freshness/availability family. The candidate R3 is more specific: B's **own lifecycle authorization to produce the required evidence** is suspended. A real R2 may already provide an authorized data-plane query, event subscription, alternative source or reactivation sequence, and must get credit. The [OpenID CAEP 1.0 Final specification](https://openid.net/specs/openid-caep-1_0-final.html) already defines cross-party **continuous access/security event signals** (for example, session or credential changes). An existing, actually implemented CAEP/Shared Signals subscription is eligible **strong conventional R2 prior art** and must not be removed for the comparison. CAEP's standard event types do **not themselves** prove a new S→R dependency or the legal applicability of B's suspended evidential privilege.

**Preconditions for a scored claim:** independently admitted owner policy for action/exception; B's exact source duty and freshness; records identifying what source data and changes are *naturally* observable; full R2 prefreeze repair (including built-in fallback); sealed, relevant R3 source availability change; participant/source rights, deadlines, costs and legitimate positives; blind oracle; actual decision/effect trace if execution is claimed. Inability to verify B is **not by itself evidence that R2 fails**: an owner-approved HOLD/reauthorization may be exactly the correct R2 answer. No infinite or universal EA superiority follows from one example.

**Meaningful bounded outcomes:** measure **time to justified posture**, **correct source/owner routing** and whether current evidence is genuinely ESTABLISHED, PARTIAL, STALE, CONTESTED, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE or INDETERMINATE. Distinguish these evidence-quality measures from the source-owned authorization decision. Also report native parity; optional compositional signal improves evidence qualification or review timing; signal correctly remains UNKNOWN; signal is irrelevant/duplicated; EA misqualifies source dependence or adds needless delay; properly authorized continuation after sufficient current proof; safe scoped reapproval when proof cannot be completed. Score **decision and metadata separately**. A *GO* cannot be justified solely because the ecosystem emitted no alarm: it requires the source-authorized sufficiency/coverage rule for that particular action.

### The one source-facing question

**When reactivating agent A requires a current dependency proposition from agent B, but B's own privilege to issue that proposition is suspended, may A's verifier use B's last snapshot within a defined validity window, query a separately authorized data source without reactivating B, or must B be reactivated first? If none is available, how is this explicitly INDETERMINATE check returned and revisited through an authorized bounded path?**

This question directly follows [Ying's own description of reassessment and partially verifiable authorization](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6164646309) and her [ordered checkpoint clarification](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6165603429). It **does not** ask Ying to adopt a new EA theory, approve a jointly conducted trial or read a large PR.
