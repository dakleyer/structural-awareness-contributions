## 2. Five substantive review findings and corrective rules

### CR-01: H-ANCHOR-SCOPE (enumerative versus operationally general)

**P-E — enumerative and verifiable:** a declared restore condition contains finite source-owned factual clauses, e.g. grant standing and no critical S→R dependency. Step 1 can detect a freshly evidenced changed edge if the relevant source is accessible. Step 2 still executes under Ying's source proposal. No EA advantage follows merely because a competent native PDP recognizes the declared edge.

**P-G — general but operationally verifiable:** the declaration references a current-context safety/eligibility method with named source, policy, scope, actual evaluator, freshness and UNKNOWN disposition. It may cover new unenumerated relations within its genuine information envelope. A bare declaration “still safe” **without a checkable method** is tautological and **NON_ADMISSIBLE** as a competent predicate. If its evaluation just replays step 2, record **step aliasing**, not two independent information gains.

Both regimes must preserve the same decision, legitimate source facts and authorized policy. Source-owner agreement is required before representing either as China Unicom's specific implementation.

### CR-02: Step-1 incremental value is an open hypothesis, not guaranteed

The **main R2-FULL** source-aligned configuration uses the complete two-step checkpoint, all native grant/NACL/credential/graph checks and its defended revalidation procedures. For **separate, consented synthetic diagnostics**, freeze:
- **R2-AB1:** omit only the historical anchor validation; retain full current intent derivation and all normal source authorization checks.
- **R2-AB2:** retain historical anchor validation; omit only renewed context intent derivation.
- **R2-AB12:** omit both solely as an educational negative; not a research reference.

These variants are **intentionally nonconformant diagnostics**. They may measure *marginal* restoration discrimination, stale authority detection, auditability, redundancy, latency and cost, but **must never replace or weaken the strong conventional B2 used in the main experiment**. If FULL and AB1 tie in correct action outcomes but FULL is more auditable, report **audit value only**, not a safety gain.

### CR-03: R3-C± is an epistemic-limit test, not proof that permanent HOLD succeeds

W+ and W− share **identical legitimately observed packets** but different evaluator-only edge truth. A correct receiver cannot know which world exists. **Do not penalize W− for a false HOLD using information known solely to the independent oracle.** A risk-proportionate bounded HOLD might be the only current legitimate action.

However, permanent global hold cannot be declared operational success by satisfying UNKNOWN preservation. Require two **independent gates**:

**G-INFORMATION:** preserve UNKNOWN, source scope, provenance and risk-proportionate action under the actual policy. No hidden-world leak.

**G-LOSS/REENTRY:** mandatory positive W0 and irrelevant-delta control, plus a separately admitted **legitimate source-query/revalidation opportunity** with known availability, latency, human capacity and burden. If a source-approved action becomes supportable before the horizon, measure unnecessary delay, failure to act and targeted reentry. Freeze a defensible owner loss/constraints model for **unsafe action**, **avoidable hold**, **information collection**, **global suspension externality**, **human review** and **deadline**. Without justified scalar calibration, use prospectively declared safety constraints plus Pareto/lexicographic reporting; never invent numeric loss weights or D probability. If information is truly unavailable, **Type-1 failure is not automatically established**.

### CR-04: Terminal POLICY-ALLOW is a legitimate negative result

Suppose a properly authorized emergency exception permits isolation even after S→R becomes critical. Then S→R is still a real contextual change, but the hypothesis **“this edge necessarily prohibits isolate(R)”** is false or not established under the approved policy. Report **TERM-POLICY-ALLOW**, do not score a permitted action as P or select another case only because the first was not negative. Scope, provenance, emergency exception and source-owner decision/actual consequences may still yield **candidate context-qualification specifications**, but **no measured effect** without independent policy and outcome evidence.

Separately record **TERM-POLICY-BLOCK** (approved policy forbids action), **TERM-NOT-ESTABLISHED** (no legitimate adjudication), **TERM-NO-SOURCE** (unavoidable indistinguishability), and **TERM-PARITY** (native conventional equivalence).

### CR-05: Under B2/B3 parity, the EA-exclusive capability claim fails

A strong competent conventional PDP may realize every behavior. If fair B2=B3, retain the negative technology-differential conclusion. What remains plausible is **a candidate interoperable specification vocabulary**: source and proposition scope, original restoration anchor and current intent basis, provenance and source dependence, time/version, material UNKNOWN/coverage, owner-controlled response, privacy and targeted reentry. It does **not** follow that EA is technically necessary, novel, adopted, or that interoperability is proven. A later conformance study must map **at least two independent source-native producers/consumers or adapters** to those semantics, run positives/negatives across boundaries, and measure disclosure/latency/cost.
