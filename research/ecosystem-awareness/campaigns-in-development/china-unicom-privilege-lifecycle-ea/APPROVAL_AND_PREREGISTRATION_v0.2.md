## 4. Pre-registration and anti-bias checklist

The prospective sealed **D2 run card** shall capture:
- exact issue/source text or pinned version, dates and external owner interpretation;
- authorized principal/grant/action, critical-service precedence rule, emergency exceptions, restore predicate and standalone observation right;
- original T0 world, same question through T3, one-edge R3 event family and materiality source;
- predeclared positive, source-visible negative, hidden-world pair and irrelevant-change controls;
- B2 implemented configuration, repairs, positive results, full code/config/source/permissions/human access hashes;
- B3 generic EA qualification, EHD producer semantics, code/config, common legitimate signal rights and incremental cost;
- evaluator-secret world truth/seed/injection and public participant packet separation; oracle/reference escrow;
- all-case inclusion / pairing / randomization policy, sample size, denominators and named horizons;
- precise full/restricted/hold/deny/Ø allowable dispositions under source-approved policy, and which outcomes are P;
- reporting of decision vs PEP effect, provenance/source dependence, false hold, bounded recovery, human load and total costs;
- the predeclared statistical or deterministic judgement method, significance/power only if actual inferential sampling claims are made;
- version freeze, signature/commit/hash, independent auditor and deviation/amendment handling.

**No reroll rule:** The scorer cannot choose a special R3 *after viewing B2 or B3 outcomes* in order to manufacture a differential. A separately declared “out-of-coverage” challenge stratum must have objective prospective admission criteria and be reported independently; it may not replace failed primary runs. The unscored public calibration cases are not hidden challenge answers.

**Equal-source rule:** A source-dependent qualified signal must be available to both arms under equal rights; the B2 owner is free to implement equivalent native metadata checks. If B3 is allowed an extra raw fact, treat it as an **additional discovery capability** in a different registered comparison. Any time/maintenance overhead is charged to the arm that incurs it.
