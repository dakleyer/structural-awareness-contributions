**Edition:** 14 October 2026 · **Research interpretation:** explicit clarification from the programme owner following the review of Mode X and the recursive R1→R2→R3-U Challenge.  
**Evidence grade:** *candidate architecture and study protocol*, **not** an executed implementation, theorem of universal dominance, cross-domain proof, China Unicom policy statement, completed TEVV or FG-TIDA normative position.  
**Read with:** [R1/R2/R3 recurrence governing principle](R123_OPEN_CONTEXT_RECURRENCE_GOVERNING_PRINCIPLE_v0.1.md) · [Mode-X candidate run card](R123_MODE_X_RECURSIVE_CHALLENGE_RUN_CARD_v0.1.md) · [full current source dossier](THIRD_PARTY_CHALLENGE_DOSSIER_R123_v0.4.md).

## 1. Central clarification: EA **does not solve** uncertainty

**Ecosystem Awareness (EA) is proposed as a non-fungible composition of qualified uncertainties.** It can relate *some* partial, heterogeneous, decision-relevant source positions while preserving which actor knows what, what each actor can evaluate, what remains uncharacterized or inaccessible, and what cannot be inferred. It **does not** remove all blind spots, provide universal contextual truth, aggregate every uncertainty into one scalar, authorize actions, or guarantee that a source-owned decision can be completed.

An EA assessment **itself has blind spots**: sources it does not know exist, semantic mappings it cannot validate, out-of-date profiles, inaccessible observations, source correlations it cannot establish, unresolved cross-domain privacy/authority, and timing/capacity constraints. Every EA output must include *its own* declared coverage, dependencies, unqualified gaps and unresolved residual. Composition can be **partial, non-decisive, contradictory or unavailable**; that can be the correct result.

**Non-fungibility**: the uncertainty surrounding a current grant, a remote service dependency, the legitimacy of an external contractual obligation, a stale datum and EA's own incomplete coverage are not interchangeable units. A specific, calibrated probabilistic estimate may legitimately be output by a competent producer *about its own stated question*; this **does not authorize** universal addition of heterogeneous confidences, assignment of a scalar to an uncharacterized C frontier or unevaluable D residual, or declaration that “ecosystem uncertainty = 0.37”.

**Complementarity, not replacement:** A source may contribute a fresh bounded assertion, another only an exploration avenue, and another may expose a currently unreachable barrier. A receiver may gain a *better characterization of what its decision rests on* without learning the correct hidden world state or obtaining permission to act. The source PDP/issuer and PEP remain responsible for the authorization decision and execution. EA can report: **“these source positions are not sufficient to justify your existing scope of reliance; these distinctions remain open; this source/owner may be the next lawful route to review.”** It may also report **“no justified qualification beyond current input.”**

## 3. A typed, partial relation — explicitly **not** a numeric aggregate

For one receiving decision Q and time t, the candidate research object is a set or structured family of **qualified source-position records**, for example:

\[
\mathcal U_{Q,t} =
\left\{\,u_i=(p_i,\,f_i,\,q_i,\,\sigma_i,\,t_i,\,a_i,\,b_i,\,c_i,\,d_i,\,\ell_i)\,\right\}.
\]

Here \`p_i\` identifies the producer/source; \`f_i\` the function and authority; \`q_i\` its proposition; \`σ_i\` its subject/decision scope; \`t_i\` effective/receipt time; \`a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i\` **source-relative** A/B/C/D *qualified components where established*, not required numeric amounts; \`ℓ_i\` source dependence/lineage. Each field may be explicitly **not established**. These are **working illustrative types**, *not* a published EHD normative schema, implementation, total data model or a proof of compositional closure.

For a *specific* question Q, a **partial** composition operator \(\bigoplus_Q\) may produce a relation/qualified positioning statement **only where legitimate semantic correspondences and provenance exist**. It must be allowed to return **NOT_COMPOSABLE / INSUFFICIENT / INCOMPATIBLE / UNKNOWN**. No obligation exists that \(\bigoplus_Q u_i\) yield a scalar, full world classification, complete graph, risk estimate or permission.

**Non-fungibility controls:**
1. **Typed non-substitution:** credential validity ≠ source-dependent policy applicability ≠ external service criticality ≠ expiry ≠ contractual authority ≠ epistemic coverage.
2. **Lineage non-duplication:** three copies of one root do not count as independent confirmation.
3. **Different-source non-equivalence:** a known B reserve at source 1 does not turn a separate actor's D into B until correspondence, access and effective evaluation conditions are established.
4. **Scope non-expansion:** source PASS for direct graph edges does not imply PASS for all indirect obligations.
5. **EA self-limitation:** if composition lacks a relevant source or mapping, the **EA qualifier must declare its own uncertainty**, not just criticize the native PDP's coverage.
6. **No forced closure or score:** an unsupported scalar such as one “overall 89% safe” is a **category error**. A scoped numeric measure remains allowable *only when meaning, source method, calibration and combining rule are justified for that question*.
7. **Authority non-transfer:** a metadata conclusion is not permission to isolate(R), issue a restricted grant or permanently HOLD; the owner policy governs action.

## 4. One concrete CU-HIB-01 illustration

An incident agent requests reactivation of \`isolate(R)\` after hibernation. Different source-owner viewpoints may contain:

| Qualified source position | Legitimate content | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain A / native grant** | \`A\`: grant G0 valid by the current issuer and relevant purpose, *within* its source-owned authorization scope. | Does not establish downstream critical-service consequences or third-domain contractual obligation. |
| **Domain B / dependency service** | B's process may deliver \`A\`: no new **direct** S→R edge in its graph at its current timestamp; it may expose \`B\`: known scope/freshness/coverage. | “No direct edge” **does not establish** the absence of *indirect* obligations or relations in Domain C. |
| **Domain C / external obligation owner** | Could have a current obligation/proposition affecting the current action; perhaps only a qualified notice or source-owned potential review route is legitimately disclosable. | The obligation's legal/policy applicability may remain unevaluated or inaccessible to Domain A and EA. |
| **EA composition process** | May identify that the grant result and dependency result have **different scopes**; may preserve a grounded B/C/D limitation and identify a source-approved review direction. | **EA can also fail** if it did not receive or could not semantically map Domain C's signal. No knowledge of the hidden contract or universal risk score is implied. |
| **Native PDP / PEP** | Source policy independently determines whether to continue, condition, restrict, deny or seek review; PEP reports actual effect if executable. | A compositional warning is not itself an authorization verdict or observed physical effect. |

**Correct bounded complementarity** might be: “Grant is valid in Domain A's declared sense; direct-edge coverage is current in Domain B's declared graph sense; the present decision also depends on a third-domain obligation for which applicability has **not been established**; contact the responsible owner before relying on the old composite assumption.” This is not a conclusion that the hidden obligation **exists**, that \`isolate(R)\` must be forbidden, or that EA has resolved the question. If even a legitimate indication of the third-domain relevance is unavailable, EA must **not invent that indication**.

## 5. Adversarial reviewer item 1 — symmetric searches without false equivalence

The critique of the current **Mode X** is valid: deliberately searching only for c where a frozen conventional K2 fails can yield a witness that says **nothing about universal or typical superiority**. To test differential performance, use **one independently predeclared eligible context class U, defined without reference to K2 or E** (same policy and rights). It cannot be chosen separately for each mechanism to engineer an advantage. **Warn the native R2 designer before freeze that future R3 changes, including out-of-family sources, are part of the Challenge**, and permit the strongest lawful generic review mechanism.

**Crucial comparison caveat:** \`E\` is a **qualified-composition/epistemic function**, not a standalone PDP or complete action controller. Therefore \(\operatorname{Cov}(K2)\subseteq\operatorname{Cov}(E)\) for *authorization execution* is a **type mismatch**. Compare **B2 = native defended K2** against **B3 = the same K2 + pre-frozen EA qualified composition** on matched owner-approved *operational* objectives. Independently assess \`E\`'s own **qualification fidelity** (including its blind spots), and B2's native qualification capabilities; do **not** force E to make source-authority decisions.

For the comparable, predeclared decision-relative duties and admissible class U, report:

- **Forward search:** same fixed budget/time/search rules, look for \`K2-only\` qualification gaps **while E-in-B3 may or may not help**.
- **Reverse search:** same fixed budget/time/search rules, look for cases where **E itself misqualifies, fails to qualify, expands scope, duplicates source confidence, lacks material evidence, or imposes burden** that K2 alone handles legitimately; similarly measure complete B3 outcomes.
- **Common failures / legitimate unresolved cases:** both remain insufficient/UNKNOWN, perhaps correctly; don't turn this into a failure of a system that cannot legally obtain information.
- **Containment inquiry:** report separately \(\operatorname{Cov}_{Q}(B2)\setminus\operatorname{Cov}_{Q}(B3)\), \(\operatorname{Cov}_{Q}(B3)\setminus\operatorname{Cov}_{Q}(B2)\), intersection, and neither, **only for a declared common qualified-decision property Q**. If U is sampled, describe coverage as **observed within U and bounds**. For the E-only source-qualification function, use \(\operatorname{Cov}_{qual}(E)\) separately, never conflate with PDP/PEP success.
- **No total order expected:** a gain in source-limit integrity may cost time or effort; an apparent decision improvement may suppress relevant UNKNOWN. Different outcome coordinates are **non-fungible** unless an independently authorized utility or constraint model justifies trade-offs.

**Search accounting:** identical evaluator budget per target direction, full list of all attempted/rejected/covered cases, seed mechanism, source policy, visibility, cost and selection rationale; future replays/patches start a *new* freeze. An asymmetrically constructed witness may support a **bounded existential statement about K2**, but **not** a claim that EA is generally better. Reciprocal witnesses likewise do not prove a universal ranking.

## 7. Independent scientific endpoints and allowable conclusions

| Endpoint | Correct measurement | What it cannot claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1 non-fungible integrity — primary EA function** | On typed multiactor evidence, did the composition preserve producer/authority, provenance dependency, scope/version, qualified unknown, and **its own limitation**? Did it avoid manufacturing scalar confidence or nonexistent facts? | Full world knowledge, a correct authorization outcome, a useful detection of every c2. |
| **Q2 decision-relative complementarity** | Did a legitimate receiver gain **additional bounded qualified grounds or a warranted direction of review** not already available from competent native K2, without invalid scope expansion? | That a downstream source owner will actually respond, authorize or restore the privilege. |
| **Q3 forward and reverse comparative coverage** | Same independent U, two targeted Mode X search directions, joint/symmetric witness ledger, and B2/B3 **coordinate-wise** scope-specific outcome sets; count successes, failures, overlap and declared UNKNOWN. | A universal containment theorem without proof, prevalence from adaptive search or a scalar “one wins” ranking. |
| **Q4 generalization and total burden** | Mode Y with preselected diverse source/context families; per-family/cumulative native fixes, EA profile maintenance, graph and query rights, latency, valid positive continuations, false holds and manual review. | A general law that EA's cost is constant, numeric advantage on infinitely many unobserved cases or inevitability of conventional failure. |
| **Q5 self-uncertainty / fail-closed in claims** | Deliberate EA missing-source, conflicting-lineage, stale-profile and untranslatable-domain cases. Does **E record NOT_ESTABLISHED for itself** and avoid falsely elevating others' statements? | That declaring UNKNOWN always improves operational effectiveness or that indefinite global suspension is appropriate. |

When a meaningful source reference is unavailable, “cannot compose/establish relevance” can be the **scientifically correct limit**, not a defect to conceal. When K2 already supports the same source qualification, B3 may be **equivalent, redundant or costlier**. A truthful qualified B3 may also help some decisions; no result is preset. E's own incompleteness is always in scope.

## 8. Working decisions and readiness

**Before scored native/system comparisons:** predeclare U without mechanism-specific bias, primary Q-specific outcome coordinates, lawful source/evidence rights, full cost/time, both adversarial search directions and budgets, K2 foreknowledge of future context challenges, E's self-qualification/profile and independent origin/selection. For the cost-scaling estimand, preregister Mode Y family partition, per-round patch/EA operating costs and complete case reporting. For a genuine source-owned China Unicom example, resolve both c1 and proposed c2 policy/observation semantics with their rightful owners; a synthetic version remains only synthetic.

**Not executed:** no externally defended native K2, no independent oracle or c2 seal, no documented E implementation, no bidirectional witness search, no family-generalization run, no conformance/safety/effect finding, no external FG-TIDA comment posted.

**Short statement for third-party reviewers:**

> Ecosystem Awareness is proposed as **non-fungible composition of qualified uncertainties** across independently governed participants. Its role is **complementary and decision-scoped**: preserve source-dependent limits, relate relevant partial views where legitimate, identify when a reliance basis is inadequately established and point to permissible review. **EA itself remains uncertain and may fail to qualify a material relation.** It cannot force every uncertainty into one number, infer hidden facts, close every gap, issue privileges or guarantee a correct operational decision. The present Challenge tests those limited epistemic functions and their costs against a fully competent conventional peer, including reverse tests where the EA layer adds no value or is worse. All claimed advantages are conditional and unestablished until independently tested.
