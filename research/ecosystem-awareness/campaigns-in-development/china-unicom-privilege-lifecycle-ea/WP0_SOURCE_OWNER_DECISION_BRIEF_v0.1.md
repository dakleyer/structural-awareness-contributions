## The one example for which policy guidance is needed

During incident X, an authorized agent A1 temporarily holds the **isolate(R)** permission, issued by Domain A. At suspension/hibernation the privilege is not exercisable. Service S (already classified critical in independently governed Domain B) **begins to depend on R only during the suspension**. The principal, grant identifier, declared incident purpose and policy reference stay the same. At activation A1 requests the *same isolation privilege*. We want to understand what actual current **policy, contextual observation and authorized fallback** mean for this one request. This is a **synthetic research scenario**, not a factual claim about China Unicom operations.

## Four decisions to ask the #23 policy/semantic owner

| ID | Short question (owner can choose “not defined yet”) | Why the answer unblocks the first challenge |
|---|---|---|
| **WP0-Q1 — materiality and permission** | In the agreed high-impact *example* policy, if S→R is newly critical, is full isolation **forbidden**, **allowed by an emergency exception**, or dependent on further facts/approvals? Who is authorized to establish that test reference? | Prevents treating our invented “edge ⇒ deny” as a source-owned rule. Both POLICY-BLOCK and POLICY-ALLOW must be legitimate terminal interpretations. |
| **WP0-Q2 — two-step restore semantics** | At suspension, would your declared factual restore condition **enumerate dependencies that must remain valid** or use a **general, actually testable current-context predicate**? At activation, who checks it and what fresh evidence is required **before the separate new intent derivation**? What if either step cannot establish its result? | Preserves the actual [Ying two-step proposal](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-6165603429) without treating a tautological “still safe” statement as evidence or equating it to Zhao's earlier anchor-alone framing. |
| **WP0-Q3 — realistic observable footprint** | When Domain B adds a dependency S→R, which authorized source(s) can see and disclose it? What *naturally changes* in graph/versioned event, service telemetry, NACL/policy, tokens or logs—and with what lag/visibility? Is a **small scoped assertion** possible without transmitting Domain B's complete private graph? | Makes the later R3 observable and causal rather than a hidden answer key or a physically implausible graph mutation with deliberately erased traces. |
| **WP0-Q4 — safe response and role** | When material evidence is partial or conflicting, what **source-authorized** full/restricted/hold/reauthorization action is legitimate? Must observation-only access be **separately granted**? Who owns the PDP decision and any high-risk human approval, and would discussing a source-approved synthetic testbed be appropriate? | Establishes a valid positive, negative and re-entry route without having EA issue privileges, inviting unconsented production testing or assuming a formal partnership. |

**Response needed:** even provisional choices and explicit “open” answers are useful. For every item capture **semantic owner, decision, affected scope, source permalink/date, assumptions, exceptions, unresolved points**. Such feedback **is discussion-level input**, not formal adoption, trial permission, or ITU-T normative approval.

## What happens if the answers are available

**Step 1:** Document an independent owner-approved synthetic authorization reference (including legitimate exceptions), and the predeclared observable channels. **Step 2:** Allow a competent native PDP to implement and repair **the complete two-step** restoration check, demonstrate legitimate positives and known-family controls, then freeze the real controller and data/rights budget. **Step 3:** An independent test owner admits genuinely held-out source/visibility probes and reports every result, including native success or parity. The [full audit correction](R3_EMPIRICAL_ADMISSION_AND_REVERSE_FALSIFICATION_v0.4.1.md) explicitly excludes the R2-training-visible/stale/irrelevant cases from “new uncovered R3” claims and treats W+/W− as specification conformance.
