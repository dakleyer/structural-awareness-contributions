## 4. Deeper scientific challenges — a skeptical third party's questions

### 4.1 Does the added S→R edge actually make isolation impermissible?

Not automatically. The evaluator must have **named policy authority and independent materiality criteria**, including emergency exemptions, competing critical services, legitimate alternative routes and reversibility. Without them, the reference world changing proves only that an edge changed—not that the correct privilege decision must change. If the source-owned policy legitimately permits isolation despite S→R, the negative CU-HIB-01 claim is **NOT_ESTABLISHED** and should be narrowed or redesigned.

### 4.2 Does the strongest R2 already solve the exact issue?

It may. Ying's two-step architecture may directly incorporate an open-ended context validation and current dependency graph. **Then R3-A and R3-B should PASS for the native R2 and remain evidence that current context checks matter.** That does not defeat the independent specification question, but it **does defeat an EA exclusivity claim**. A technology that already realizes equivalent semantics counts as sufficient, even if named differently.

### 4.3 What exactly changes in R3: world truth or received evidence?

Maintain three separate records: world graph/event from an independent evaluator; native Domain B source/telemetry and *actual access/lag* for the PDP; and qualified receiving-state/decision. A source-visible edge and source-hidden edge may have the same **world** but different observable boundary. No component can know evaluator-private W+/W− truth when packets are indistinguishable. A properly constrained no-full-restoration decision due to explicit **unknown** is distinct from proof that S depends on R.

### 4.4 Does a new restoration predicate “cover every future context change” by fiat?

No. A broad predicate like “still safe under current context” is not self-verifying. It needs named sources, completeness or qualified unknown, version/freshness, material dependency and action owner. The **first** validation step can be satisfied, contradicted or unestablished; the **second** intent derivation still has its own source-owned temporal claim. A failure in step 1 need not imply the system can know the exact hidden edge. A success in step 1 does not license bypassing step 2.

### 4.5 Can an EA specification be meaningfully evaluated without proving EA software?

Yes, but the claim is about **verifiable semantics**: what source-native evidence must be consumed or referenced, what must remain unknown, who can authorize the next step, when the evidence expires, and how a legitimate unaffected action continues. The candidate requirement is falsified by missing material source, stale evidence mispromoted, unscoped hold, dependent evidence counted as independent, or a privilege fabricated by a metadata producer. These are conformance expectations, **not proof of a particular implementation**.

### 4.6 Is the R3 “out of R2” test rigged?

It is rigged if the evaluator decides to count only a world known to break B2 while hiding attempts where B2 succeeds. The confirmatory stratum must be objectively specified before outcome inspection. A postfreeze stress case may be independently interesting and is allowed as **separately labelled exploratory evidence with complete search record**. It cannot support a frequency/generalization claim unless selection is justified.

### 4.7 Does another component or human approval silently own #23?

No. Theme #16 supplies human-intervention lifecycle, external grant/issuer owns authority, the PDP makes authorized privilege decisions, PEP produces effects. Theme #13/EA qualifies cross-domain basis. Receiving owner MUST remain visible. A human “resume task” is not “renew G0.” The [15:35 Lei discussion](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-5700157082) and [17 Sep Ying response](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/23#issuecomment-5707764621) support keeping them distinct, although final normative allocation remains with respective owners.
