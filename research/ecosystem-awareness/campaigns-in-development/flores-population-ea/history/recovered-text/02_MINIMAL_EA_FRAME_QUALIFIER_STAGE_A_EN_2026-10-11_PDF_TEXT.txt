Minimal EA-side frame-qualification proposal
Proposed identity: EA-POP-FRAME-QUAL-0.3 (author-side working name, NOT a FG-TIDA interface or a UC-4 stage).
Review class: DDS Stage A — Specification Discovery, initially a simplified bounded specification route. Insertion
point: after the unchanged Flores planted-judge Stage 1 and before, or as a separate consideration alongside, a future
receiving-decision study such as UC-21. Owner boundary: Theme #21 owns what population observations support; EA
qualifies whether those statements retain sufficient foundation for a different receiving decision; any intervention,
eligibility re-evaluation or execution sits with applicable Theme #13/#16 and native owners.


1. What is and is not being proposed
Proposed: re-use a few already-published declarations about population scope, reference/taxonomy, evaluator
dependence, source provenance and identifiable limits; relate them to an independently specified receiving question and,
only if actually present, an eligible independently sourced change signal. Output a bounded qualification saying what
remains justified and what is unestablished. Keep the original population computation immutable.

Not proposed: altering one of the six scripted judges, re-running generate.py/detect.py/unblind.py, introducing a
seventh judge, using the authored answer key, asking evaluators to supply introspective doubts, assigning all missing
information to C, changing reference 1.0 ground truth, manufacturing a t1 event, expanding Theme #21’s terms of
reference, or treating a self-authored demonstration as empirical evidence. The current README announces the first
test and its limits but does not report it as an executed Stage 1 result.

Research question: For a declared receiving question Q, can already available qualifications from distinct source
processes allow EA to distinguish (i) a still applicable evaluation frame, (ii) a proven material mismatch, (iii) a frame
whose applicability is not established, and (iv) a producer-relative exploratory frontier C, without directing or reproducing
population measurement? An external t1 event, when relevant, is supplied and adjudicated outside EA; the experiment
must test whether the composition of the pre-existing qualifiers yields an additional, decision-relevant distinction
relative to a strong conventional peer holding the same evidence.


2. Olena’s four-step chain and the two different regressions
Olena Pavlenko, 3 September 2026, proposed:

   1.     Claim / target Q. What does the population result purport to establish?
   2.     Observation/evaluator architecture O. What sources, record eligibility, taxonomy, lineage, evaluator-family
          assumptions and observation windows can actually be observed?
   3.     Identifiability I(Q,O). Can the asserted property be distinguished under that architecture? Which alternatives
          remain observationally indistinguishable?
   4.     Sampling/estimation uncertainty. Only if identifiable, what can finite measurements establish?

We study the re-entrance from step 4 to step 3, and where justified to step 2, for a specified claim under a
materially changed observation regime. Importantly, this does not itself establish an ABCD B→C transition. The
former is a changed applicability/identifiability question. The latter requires that a formerly characterized assessment
route is no longer characterized for a specified aspect and there is a grounded but not sufficiently characterized
route of exploration. If a known recovery method exists, the remainder stays in B. If the process has no effective route,
it may be D. With insufficient grounds, assign no component role instead of guessing.

5. Second exercise: contingent R3 with a legitimate external event
Name: EA-POP-FRAME-R3-CONDITIONAL-01. This remains NOT ADMITTED until an independent source owner can
evidence a legitimate post-t0 material change for a specified pending receiving decision. A realistic and already
source-grounded family is the reference/qualification history around UC17-07 under REF-FSCOMMS 1.0 / 1.1.

Pre-registration obligations (no implicit assumptions):

   1.     t0: the exact population output, observation regime and applicability to a specified decision are frozen, and
          relevant external conditions are actually established, not merely declared; the claim under test must be
          identified independently of the result EA is expected to return.
   2.     t1: the authoritative reference/standing owner, not EA, supplies a verifiable event with effective time, subject,
          decision class and validity scope after t0, and an independent evaluator privately adjudicates it. Merely
          including 1.0 and 1.1 in a static fixture is insufficient.
   3.     Observable distinction: the unchanged world and changed world must differ in at least one legitimately
          available producer-authored metadata/evidence input. If not, the possibility of reliable detection is falsified;
          this is not a software bug.
   4.     No extra work for Flores: published Stage 1 source declarations, any released result reference and ordinary
          native source records only. No new population evaluation, no new judge self-report, no query directed to
          measurement from EA with desired answers/budgets.
   5.     Admissible result states: CONTINUITY_SUPPORTED, FRAME_MISMATCH_SUPPORTED,
          FRAME_NOT_ESTABLISHED, NO_OBSERVABLE_CHANGE, UNVERIFIED_EVENT or OUT_OF_SCOPE. Keep epistemic
          classification on a separate axis, B_CHARACTERIZED, C_GROUNDED_UNCHARACTERIZED,
          D_NO_EFFECTIVE_ROUTE, ROLE_UNCLASSIFIED.
   6.     Fair peer: a competent B2 receives the same native published declarations, owners, timestamp/source events,
          reference history, time budget and candidate actions as B3+EA. The B2 may already perform version, lineage
          and scope qualification correctly.

6. One stable, testable vocabulary
To avoid circularity, the suggested envelope 04_review_matrices/FRAME_ENVELOPE_SCHEMA_CONCEPT.json carries
observations/evidence status separately from EA’s assessed outcome. The event is not labeled C by its publisher.
SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE is never accepted as VERIFIED_PRIMARY. UNKNOWN is not a favourable default. Every B/C/D
assignment identifies producer, process, question, scope and time. No grade.startswith('verified') or route
field can supply the answer by itself.

Frame qualification axis: CONTINUITY_SUPPORTED, FRAME_MISMATCH_SUPPORTED, FRAME_NOT_ESTABLISHED,
NO_OBSERVABLE_CHANGE, UNVERIFIED_EVENT, OUT_OF_SCOPE.

Epistemic role axis (when enough grounds exist): B_CHARACTERIZED, C_GROUNDED_UNCHARACTERIZED,
D_NO_EFFECTIVE_ROUTE, ROLE_UNCLASSIFIED.

Evidence provenance axis: PRIMARY_VERIFIED_AT_SOURCE, SOURCE_DECLARED_ONLY,
PUBLIC_REPORT_NOT_BINARY_REPRODUCED, UNVERIFIED, SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE, NOT_GENERATED, RESTRICTED. A
source’s own A/record fields continue to live at that source; the role assignment is an independent EA hypothesis.


7. Place in the research corpus and the external audit route
   •      DDS Stage A (canonical challenge–trajectory profile): candidate requirement specification, independent
          adjudication and explicit boundary/falsifiers. It does not establish architecture or implementation validity.
   •      EA 00M/00N (00M; 00N): meanings of B/C/D and limits of composition; no magical recovery of erased
          distinctions.
   •      00D comparator (B2/B3, §§4 and 12): peer fairness; conventional equivalence is a valid negative differential.
   •      00I (semantic TOCTOU synthetic scenario) provides an analogue, not executed proof about Flores or an
          automatic family admission.
   •      00K/A25 (X1–X7 extension requirements): not claimed satisfied. Family, preserved kernel and mapping/proof
          have not been selected. An auditor may request a separate extension-admission exercise later.
   •      Native owner comments: Flores permits EA feedback identifying the missing qualification 30 Sep 2026, but
          reiterates downstream re-evaluation belongs outside #21 1 Oct 2026. He recognizes UC-21 as a later receiving-
          decision study 4 Oct 2026.

Proposed decision to external reviewers: accept the bounded read-only native-source qualification as a worthwhile
Stage A specification question. Reject any statement that a genuine Flores R3 or a B→C regression has already been
observed. Request independent owner recognition of a future t1 event only if there is a practical reason to run the
second exercise. Neither a research bonus nor an expectation of EA superiority is a valid reason to alter Flores’s first
study.
