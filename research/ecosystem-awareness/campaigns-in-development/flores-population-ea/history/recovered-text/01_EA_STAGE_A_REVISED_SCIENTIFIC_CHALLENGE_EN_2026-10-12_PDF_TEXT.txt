Executive determination
Scientific status: candidate DDS Stage A challenge, NOT a passed EA test. The earlier v0.4 casebook is
insufficiently discriminating for a claim of EA requirements satisfaction: its ten documentary questions mostly repeat
source-known limitations; its authored classifications were not independently adjudicated; and some public materials
leaked branch-specific falsifiers. This revision retains the source corpus, removes the flawed scoring surfaces, and
defines a narrower, prospectively admissible study. Neither an EA implementation nor a strong conventional B2
comparator has been executed. No post-Stage-1 exogenous change in Flores’s six scripted judges has been
established.

The scientific question is not whether EA statistically beats Flores. It is: Can a proposed ecosystem-qualification
specification, using only eligible pre-existing producer statements and a separately admissible event if one exists,
preserve context-dependent sufficiency across heterogeneous functions while meeting the materially applicable
canonical EA requirements? The Stage A adjudicator must judge a requirement route, not a raw multiple-choice
accuracy percentage.

An important evidentiary limit

Receiving a directly asserted, already-adjudicated “basis lost” event and copying it into a new field is not context-loss
detection. Any claim of distinctive composition must show a justified relation between multiple eligible partial
grounds, a controlled information transformation that preserves source/scope/dependence, and a materially different
qualified result. If the event is wholly unobservable in eligible inputs, both candidate EA and B2 must refrain from
claiming to distinguish the hidden worlds. This is the impossibility control, not a success rate.


5. Independent acceptance and comparison rule
All first-order source facts, positive control branches, forbidden inference categories, affected decision scope and
materiality must be frozen by an independent adjudicator before either candidate runs. The adjudicator may adopt,
amend or reject the author-proposed private oracle. In the v0.5 package, all private expected outcomes remain explicitly
UNFROZEN and all roles require human review. The generic source-to-output requirements are legitimately public;
branch-specific falsifiers, private world labels and draft answers are not.

A Stage A specification-level finding requires: (i) exact native-result preservation (including A where necessary); (ii)
justified process-relative epistemic roles and material scope; (iii) event observability and source dependence discipline;
(iv) correct positive continuity and bounded unresolved posture; (v) a finite, declared burden/horizon; and (vi) a complete
auditor-readable causal trace. Any material forbidden promotion, invented external event, oracle leak or unauthorized
action defeats the affected route. Missing evidence produces NOT_ESTABLISHED, not a compensating averaged score.

Only afterward compare a competent B2 peer and EA-profiled B3, with the same source access, authority,
compute/communication/human budget, timing and native A availability. If B2 meets the same requirement vector at
equal/lower burden, record peer closes gap/equivalent semantics, not EA uniqueness. If both fail, do not award EA for
relative superiority. The Stage A result concerns specification reachability, not implementation effectiveness or statistical
power.

For an actual R3, a stronger causal comparator is necessary: hold the previously accepted R2 configuration fixed,
change only a legitimate external observation/authority condition at t1, and independently adjudicate its material effect.
The study currently lacks this evidence. The DDS Stage A method governs admissions; there is no 00I/A25 family
extension until its kernel, mapping h, failure reflection X3, requirement route X4 and controls X1–X7 have been
demonstrated. Thus the former v0.4 00K gate row remains not admitted, not silently omitted.
