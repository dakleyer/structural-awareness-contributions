# Flores initiative — chronology, author attribution and adversarial audit history

**Purpose:** historical no-loss ledger, not a selective success story or an execution log. Source links in [SOURCES_AND_CUSTODY.md](./SOURCES_AND_CUSTODY.md). Differences in dates or claims are retained as versioned history.

## 3–10 September 2026: methodological and interface origins

- Justin Philip Flores opens FG-TIDA [Theme #21](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/21), separating population-level evaluation from an individual record, operational oversight (#16) and decision qualification (#13/EA). Per-flag rates against explicit taxonomy/policy versions, per-evaluator by default; pooled results require justified failure-mode diversity. Population growth helps identifiable sampling error, not structural non-identifiability.
- [Olena Pavlenko](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/21#issuecomment-5532364422) clarifies the order **claim → observation architecture → identifiability → sampling uncertainty**. The classification of a claim can change with the observation regime; a change must be evidenced, not attributed to sample size alone.
- [Lei Gao](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/21#issuecomment-5564561699) preserves #21 measurement and #16 operational-human-oversight as separate functions, with a qualified handoff. [Cyril Gorlla](https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/21#issuecomment-5584167406) keeps #6 verdict/record semantics upstream. Four separately operated provider judges do not prove independent failure modes. “Agreement ≠ correctness” remains central.
