## 3. Scientific contribution we are preparing

**Native population result** belongs to Theme #21. **EA receiving qualification** asks whether that result, plus separately legitimate references/observations, meets a distinct consumer decision's claim, scope, provenance, source dependence and time. EA preserves upstream results **without recomputing them** and requalifies **their applicability**, not their hidden statistical truth.

The foundational four-step sequence from Olena Pavlenko is **claim → observation/evaluator architecture → identifiability under that architecture → sampling uncertainty, if identifiable**. Increasing n never establishes a claim that is structurally unidentifiable from the same accessible observations. Distinguish this sequence from EA's process-relative **A/B/C/D** semantic roles in [00M](../../baseline/00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md): source A is an operative producer result; B a characterized basis/limit/reserve; C a grounded not-yet-characterized evaluation frontier; D an effect beyond the effective route of a named process. Indeterminate at the producer is **not automatically** EA-C or EA-D.

**No magical detection:** if two worlds produce identical admitted observable data, neither EA nor conventional B2 can identify the hidden world. Nothing in Flores's fixed scripted generator establishes a genuine post-t0 loss of observer independence or a measured R3 shift.

**No authority laundering:** an EA insufficiency signal is not a license, operational approval or enforcement instruction. #21 can keep measurement independent of the receiver's desired answer. EA may report an assessment question, affected scope or *named missing qualification* without telling #21 how to score or feeding its preferred outcome upstream. Theme #16/UC-4/enforcement retain their own owners and authorizations.
