## 3. Original R1 probe suggestion — hypothetical only

The auditor illustrated an independent response-known probe family: 96 cases, 12 shared-blind-spot positions, k∈{0,8,16,32,64} probes. Under uniformly random placement and sampling without replacement, the chance of exposing at least one known blind spot rises with k. These figures were a **hypothetical calculation**, never a generated Flores result.

The new probe control would itself introduce obligations: authorship/validity of the probe key, representativeness and whether probes expose other latent shared modes. Closing one sampled part is not proof that residual mass outside probe coverage vanished.

**Decision:** do not ask Flores to add probes to the frozen Stage-1 design. Keep this as an optional later control chosen by its owner during joint review. The primary EA campaign uses the observation regimes already planned by §9 instead.

## 4. Why this is useful despite B2 parity

A generic strong B2 can produce R0 and the hypothetical R1 delta with simple coverage/lineage rules. Accept full credit. This ledger clarifies what the source has and has not established and supplies named limitations for the receiver — but cannot itself claim source-regime requalification.

P0–P3 adds an independent test of **local receiver proof-sufficiency** when its authorized comparison view changes, including a loss and restoration of the *receiver's* ability to justify a qualified inference. It does not secretly rename the residual-ledger check “Ecosystem Awareness.” Exact source A/B/C/D are process-specific; no exhaustive partition of “all possible unknowns” is asserted.
