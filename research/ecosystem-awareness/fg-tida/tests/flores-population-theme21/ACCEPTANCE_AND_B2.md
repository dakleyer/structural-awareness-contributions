# DDS Stage A acceptance plan, adversarial controls and fair B2 comparison

**Stage A object:** candidate requirement-level *receiving qualification* specification and profile, NOT the Stage-1 seeded statistical detector or a future product implementation. The public examples are instrument calibration. Independent adjudication/Stage A acceptance remains open.

## 2. One campaign with staged, source-native and synthetic observations

Set P0 blind → P1 authorized unblind → P2 withheld receiver proof association → P3 restoration on the **same Flores-produced files**. P2 changes only a local receiving view. Native output is unchanged and must be hash-pinned after release. For the pre-run reference fixture, P0/P1 are explicit mock receipts and must not be described as executed Population results.

Prospective controls selected/frozen by an independent reviewer after candidate and B2 profiles are locked:

- Positive valid handoff P1: comparison with authored reference is supported *as such*; no independent key truth.
- Three withheld essential association classes: report-role/scope, reference applicability, EHD profile/version interpretation (do not give the candidate loss label).
- Benign removal of unused decoration: no downgrade to any legitimate source statement.
- Explicitly *refuted* seal linkage: REJECT the claim of key-relative comparability; do not imply the authored key is false.
- Alternative *legitimate reconstruction* of a missing link: continue if an eligible substitute actually establishes the needed association.
- Bounded absence: no native unblind report yet → NOT_ESTABLISHED, not REFUTED and not C by default.
- Scope mismatch/timestamps, duplicate same-origin notices, REF-version mismatch, attested absence vs permit and content verdict vs record appraisal.
- Observation-equivalent twins with different unobserved truth: neither candidate may claim universal discrimination.
- No source result may be inferred from generic parser errors. A parse failure is a receiver failure, not empirical signal of changed judges.

## 3. Machine-readable response contract (proposed)

Each assessed claim has: campaign_id; claim_id; role_process; target/source scope; source_ids; source commit/ref; observation_phase; receiver_profile_version; qualifier [SUPPORTED | REFUTED | NOT_ESTABLISHED]; missing_or_contradicted_associations with causes; unaffected_claim_ids; residual_indeterminacy; evidence_mode; authority_owner; clocks; source_access/profile_labor/compute/comms/human costs; assessment_origin [source-derived | receiver-inferred | externally-adjudicated | mocked]; and optional future_requalification_route with its grounds. The response must not carry the source-native action verdict inside the metadata-only lane.

## 4. Comparing B2 fairly

At minimum B2 includes **typed validation, checksums, version/currency, source and record identity, lineage graph, conditional consistency, partial unknown, local recovery, correlation/duplicate detection and catch-all NOT_ESTABLISHED**. B2 is allowed to maintain the *same dependency graph as EA*, and may tie or dominate. Refuse a comparison against a simplistic trigger table.

Freeze the same observations, time, human authority, tools and access. Count **actual** configuration/profile authoring labour, semantic obligations, code/review time, tokens/CPU, bytes, external calls, observer and auditor steps, review demand and duplicate alerts. Cost and complexity are a vector, not line count only. Include cases where extra EA work causes missed useful deadlines. One matched case is not a theorem that finite trigger families cannot scale.

Decision rule: **first** eliminate materially unsafe/incorrect responses and false alarms/blanket denials on positive continuity; **then** report per-claim completeness/selectivity and total burden vectors for each arm; **finally** report B2 tie, EA improvement, B2 advantage or insufficient evidence under a predeclared equivalence margin. Do NOT claim uniqueness on P0–P3 alone, nor infer lower run cost while excluding EA profile curation.

## 6. Decision and stopping conditions

The first meaningful milestone is a reviewed one-page agreement on *receiving question, exact sources, allowable read-only observation views and joint-review timing*; a study may stop with a scoped zero differential if B2 accomplishes the same at equal/lower burden. There is no obligation to keep changing the experiment or adding an endless chain of disclaimers. No request to alter Flores's blind detector, key, scripted judges or acceptance thresholds.
