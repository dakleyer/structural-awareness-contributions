## One scientific question

Can a read-only *downstream* receiver, using only the measurement qualifications that Flores and Cyril already publish (plus the planned ordinary blind/unblind artefacts **if** subsequently released), correctly distinguish **supported inference**, **contradicted inference** and **unestablished inference about its own discrimination capacity**, with exact affected scope, preserved legitimate continuity and explicit residual — even after a reviewer selectively withholds a material local proof association? Can it do so without upstream resampling, new source B/C/D assertions, hidden answers or false claims of independent truth?

The required output is a source- and decision-relative **qualification of reliance**; it is neither a new per-judge verdict nor an instruction to Population. A source may remain unchanged while the receiver loses a justification. A lost receiver association is not a demonstrated change in judge behaviour or world state.

### Stronger-than-trivial prospective contribution

The primary observable is *selective, source-justified requalification and explicit loss of discrimination capacity* across a receiver's available information and profile; it is NOT merely noticing that an enumerated field differs. The source-prose-to-qualified-input mapping is currently human-authored and its labour is part of campaign cost. A strong conventional B2 with the same proof graph can tie; this case alone cannot demonstrate unique EA advantage.
