## 1. Producer and process are compulsory

Source function A is a *native result* (for Flores: blind flags or the post-unblind report, depending on the process). EA cannot relabel a native A as B, C or D to meet N07 or win an evaluation. A reference/ID/digest of A may locate a B declaration only when the profile states its meaning and admissibility.

B: characterized source basis/coverage/versions/dependence/limitations outside A and assessable remainder.  
C: grounded route of exploration **without** sufficient method/variables to assess that route.  
D: witnessed material residual beyond effective evaluation (may be partly monitored).  
UNKNOWN: no sufficient basis to assign a role; an omitted field or unfrozen mapping is not C or D.  
SOURCE CONTENT INDETERMINATE: Cyril's native action verdict on UC17-07; it stays SOURCE A and is unrelated to the classification above.

A, B, C, D are process-relative components, not four mandatory bins. The EA-side assignment of B/C/D is owned by EA's profile, not by Flores or Cyril. Their underlying text retains independent authorship, exact source version, provenance, time and uncertainty.

## 3. Proposed read-only receiving profile

For each source qualifier: {source_owner, producer_function, source_native_artefact_ref, source_version, observed_at, source_declared_basis, source_limit, scope, qualification_owner=EA, EA_profile_version, source_assertion_status, receiver_question, dependency_ids, availability, interpretation_reason}. Every payload field is *proposed* for this local exercise; never assert universal adoption.

The receiver output is a **new local result** A of the receiver's qualification function:
{receiving_claim_id, role_process, receiving_scope, qualified_state, missing_basis, source_linkage, affected_and_unaffected_claims, uncertainty_residual, requalification_route_if_grounded, action_owner, source_type}. It does not direct the source measurement. Unknown/correlated/not measured remain explicitly named.

Input admission profiles:
- METADATA_ONLY: B-like source qualifications and linkage; no native content verdicts. Good for method/scope qualification but cannot reconstruct judge correctness.
- SOURCE_A_BY_REFERENCE: native A remains outside EHD content, accessible only to an authorized downstream consumer after unblind as a separate referenced object; no pre-unblind leakage.
- POST_UNBLIND_AUTHORIZED: verified report-to-seal/key relation to support **key-relative** comparison, not truth of key.
- SYNTHETIC_RECEIVER_ABLATION: only locally simulated EHD/proof association, not a source-native assertion. Must be isolated from empirical results.

## 4. Temporal and ownership discipline

The validity of P1 receiving qualification depends on proof of its source/report relation at t1. On loss of relation at t2, retain the original P1 as a historical qualified snapshot; do NOT rewrite P1 or Flores's native result. If profile correctness is unknown under P2, the receiving assessment is NOT_ESTABLISHED and signals a bounded review need, not an observed external regime change. If a replacement source supplies an equivalent valid association, preserve supported continuation.

Cyril's R-UC17-07 at REF 1.0 versus R-UC17-07-2 at REF 1.1 is **historical versioned supersession before** Flores's frozen Stage 1; it is NOT a claimed t2 drift observation. UC17-08 absence appraised is NOT a source action verdict and is not part of Flores's scored content set.

## 6. Minimal output completeness test

An output is insufficient if it omits the specific receiving claim/process, scope, source/provenance, time/versions, violated or unavailable proof links, surviving justified claims, state (SUPPORTED/REFUTED/NOT_ESTABLISHED), limitation on causal interpretation, residual, and authorized response owner. Producing a generic UNKNOWN string or treating it as permission is a failure.

The source truth, the accuracy of the EHD producer mapping and the current validity of EA's own mapping are all distinct. Tests must separately document each check and its information source.
