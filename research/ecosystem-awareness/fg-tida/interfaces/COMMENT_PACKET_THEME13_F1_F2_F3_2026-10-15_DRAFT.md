## Essential boundary and posting order

Theme #13's current discussion has two independently testable but communicating mechanisms: Ward's Incident / Signal Lifecycle (signal birth, distribution, amendment/corroboration, response coordination and resolution) and Ecosystem Awareness (EA). The shared #13 signalling interface is a proposed outward communication route, not an assertion that all #13 work reduces to EA; non-defence ecosystem functions remain in scope for later exploration. Ward's expressed preference is a proposal at contributor level, not FG-TIDA charter approval.

The functional architecture distinguishes:
- **F1 — Mission & Decision Context Qualification:** identifies decision-relevant domains, evidentiary needs, risk/sensitivity, temporal/capacity boundaries. It qualifies the *need for sensing*, not the physical sensing function itself.
- **F2 / F2.APQ — Decision-Relevant Window Qualification and Acquisition Pathway Qualification:** selects/qualifies evidence windows, sources, acquisition/sensing pathways; publishes W(d,t), coverage, gaps, freshness and (if applicable) pathway sufficiency. Its outputs are not local epistemic verdicts.
- **F3 — Local Epistemic State Qualification:** preserves the source-native local result and qualifies its determinacy, scope, residual and provenance as QL(d). It does not re-evaluate the source domain or make a systemic decision.
- **F4/F5/F6/F8:** external signal qualification / scope-indexed composition / systemic assessment / general publication where those responsibilities are required.

**Candidate outbound alternative (not established in frozen 03/04 or FG-TIDA):** A neighboring Theme that needs only a *local, bounded, already-qualified input* may consume a profile sourced from F3/QL(d), with any material F1/F2 context/window qualifiers, *instead of demanding the full system-level EA F6/F8 assessment as its input*. F3 replaces the generic **EA outbound result** only in that particular bilateral contract; it does not replace the EA architecture, rewrite original evidence or assume F3 by itself proves ecosystem-wide sufficiency. Transport/publication can remain through the shared #13 signalling/EHD route, subject to review of 04/F8 egress semantics: no mandatory independent F3 wire channel is presumed. A full EA system-level output remains available when sources, dependencies or operating-frame sufficiency must be composed. Each route must preserve producer/native-result provenance, version, assessed scope, validity/freshness, source relations, UNKNOWN and the authorized action boundary.
