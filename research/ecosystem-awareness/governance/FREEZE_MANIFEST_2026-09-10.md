7\. INTEROPERABILITY FREEZE

&nbsp;

EA-ITP-01 is the controlling first interoperability test specification. It includes both under-specified and richer-than-kernel producer cases. A producer may emit more than the EHD kernel; receivers must preserve material extra qualifiers without silently discarding them and must not over-read producer-specific fields as if they had EA semantics. A failed test first opens the profile/adapter/semantic-mapping layer. It does not automatically reopen F1–F9, O1–O6, IF-S1–IF-S13 or the EHD kernel.
