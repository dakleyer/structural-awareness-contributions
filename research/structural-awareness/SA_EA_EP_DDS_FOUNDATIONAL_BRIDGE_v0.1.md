### Executive reading — the placement correction

1. **Structural Awareness (SA)** is the mathematical programme: open semantic frontiers, qualified existence/exclusion, processor/context-relative determination and the conditions under which meta-information can change a decision. It does **not** itself grant authority, implement an agent, or prove useful gain in every instance.
2. **Ecosystem Awareness (EA)** operationally specializes that problem into six testable principles **P1–P6**, and separately typed requirements **S1–S14** with **T1–T4** conditions. This refinement is richer than a relabelling; its proof obligations and open reverse implications remain visible.
3. **DDS** is a **transversal development and verification method**. In the present research route its **Stage A begins from EA's S/T specification and originating challenge**. **UC21 is an externalisable challenge/falsification instrument connected at this EA entry**, not a child or product of EP. DDS and UC21 may be reused for other domains.
4. **Ecosystem Positioning (EP)** is **our concrete, integrated candidate architecture** combining EA with signalling, cartography, regime awareness, MSCA, objective-conditioned gradient, ACC/lineage and legitimate authority interfaces. Within this DDS exercise EP is a **Stage B architecture candidate tested against the selected/frozen Stage A specification**. EP is not equivalent to all of EA and does not currently have an owner-adopted independent set of EP principles.
5. The current **A/B/C/D agentic model** is a **bounded synthetic experimental model / partial architectural instantiation**. Its experimental status does **not** imply that every EP constituent is experimental: the modules have different maturity histories. It is not the full EP integration, nor automatic DDS Stage C evidence.

These are different kinds of objects. A diagram showing SA → EA → EP describes **conceptual specialization**, whereas the independent DDS route begins at **EA**, tests an architecture at Stage B and requires a pinned implementation plus observed effects at Stage C. No arrow licenses authority, completeness, uniqueness or universal superiority.

## 2. The mathematical entry — three *candidate characterization families*, not new axioms

The three-family description is a compact reading device for existing SA sources. It does **not** recanonize their mathematics into three axioms or replace independently stated premises.

### SA-F1 — Non-closure/openness is a qualified model property

Let \(\Omega\) be a **stipulated** semantic universe and \(\operatorname{FinAdm}(\Omega)\) an admissible family of frames whose denotation covers only finitely many constituents. The open-model property is:

\[
\forall F\in\operatorname{FinAdm}(\Omega)\ \exists x\in\Omega:
x\notin\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{den}_F).
\tag{SA-F1}
\]

This follows for infinite \(\Omega\) under **finite denotation**, or is a premise of an explicitly open model. A short finite syntax can denote an infinite set; finite code or processor size **alone** does not prove (SA-F1). Nor does \(\exists x\notin F\) prove an external compatible agent, a reachable witness or a task-relevant solution. A fully represented finite universe is a counterexample to an unconditional universal claim.

The external \(A'\) is therefore a **frame-relative semantic frontier**, not a globally enumerated or permanently disjoint inventory. The operational process may extend or change its frame; no fixed outside set carries a universally valid B/C/D tag.

Source: [Part I, §§3–5](./A_APRIME_OPEN_SEMANTIC_TRANSFER_BETWEEN_AGENT_ECOSYSTEMS_v0.1.md).

### SA-F2 — Scoped existence and scoped exclusion are different valid metapropositions

For a task-specific predicate \(P_\chi\) and a **declared** quantification region \(Q_\chi\), indexed by the composite context \(\chi\) defined below:

\[
\exists x\in Q_\chi:P_\chi(x)
\qquad\text{versus}\qquad
\forall x\in Q_\chi:\neg P_\chi(x).
\tag{SA-F2}
\]

A sound existential certificate can establish **that** a target exists without individuating it, giving its coordinates, or making it reachable. A sound exclusion certificate can eliminate a specified search region; it cannot establish global nonexistence without a domain-wide proof. Absence of a witness, inability to evaluate, disproved permission and proved nonexistence are **four different statements**, and a certificate must retain its quantified scope.

The mathematical decision gain is **conditional** on the task, proof soundness, search cost, reachability, available alternatives and loss model. In particular, the [Meta-Existence/Exclusion Calculus](./SA_METAEXISTENCE_CONTEXT_AND_EVALUABILITY_CALCULUS_v0.1.md) gives explicit qualified examples; it does not assert that every open frontier is useful.

### SA-F3 — Determination, exclusion and indeterminacy are *positioned*, not intrinsic universal labels

Use a **proposed composite indexing context** without redefining the source mathematical symbols:

\[
\chi=(p,\ q,\ F,\ t,\ E,\ \rho,\ \sigma,\ \kappa).
\tag{SA-F3a}
\]

Here \(p\) is the processor, \(q\) the proposition/question, \(F\) the admitted/operative frame, \(t\) the assessment time and \(E\) the evidence material. As in [SA Part I §2](./A_APRIME_OPEN_SEMANTIC_TRANSFER_BETWEEN_AGENT_ECOSYSTEMS_v0.1.md), **\(\rho\)** remains the resource profile, **\(\sigma\)** the scope/time interpretation and **\(\kappa\)** the evidence, permissions and authority qualification. **\(\chi\)** is a *new bridge-only composite index*, not a change to any of those source-owned variables.

A relative qualification should be understood as a **typed record**, not an exhaustive three-valued truth function. One possible, non-normative illustration is:

\[
\operatorname{Qual}_{\chi}(q)
=(\mathrm{polarity},\mathrm{evidence\ basis},\mathrm{proof\ scope},
\mathrm{evaluability},\mathrm{freshness},\mathrm{permissions},\mathrm{residual}).
\tag{SA-F3b}
\]

The status vocabulary remains **open and typed**: qualified positive/negative, unresolved semantic question, TIMEOUT, BAD_FRAME, authorization refusal, incompatible scope and partial output must not be conflated. Other source-native qualifications remain valid. This illustrative record is **not** a new protocol, event schema, global partition or exhaustive classifier; the underlying full source/evidence objects stay addressable.

The same underlying proposition may be established for one processor and unresolved or out of scope for another; its **truth in a fixed interpretation does not change merely because a processor moves**. What changes is the processor's qualified assessment, available action or represented frame. If a process determines its local delivered \(A_r\), it has not thereby exhausted the semantic obligation \(A'_r\). The latter is **not a set of identifiable exterior elements**: actual constituents may lie beyond a frame, while \(A'_r\) records a relative open-frontier obligation rather than an extensional complement.

A/B/C/D may then be **characterizations of operational positions or residual uses under \(\chi\)**. They are optional process-relative interpretations, not the three SA foundation families or independent metamathematical axioms. See [Part I §9](./A_APRIME_OPEN_SEMANTIC_TRANSFER_BETWEEN_AGENT_ECOSYSTEMS_v0.1.md), [SA context/evaluability calculus](./SA_METAEXISTENCE_CONTEXT_AND_EVALUABILITY_CALCULUS_v0.1.md), and [Part II metasyntactic utility](./METASYNTACTIC_UTILITY_CLASS_THEOREMS_v0.1.md).

### Minimal negative controls at this level

- **Not valid:** open universe \(\Rightarrow\) reachable new agent; **valid only if** the source separately establishes task relevance and reachability.
- **Not valid:** not yet evaluated \(\Rightarrow\) does not exist; **valid:** decision status is unresolved within a registered qualification boundary.
- **Not valid:** \(\forall x\in F:\neg q(x)\Rightarrow\forall x\in\Omega:\neg q(x)\) when \(F\subsetneq\Omega\).
- **Not valid:** a change of processor/context automatically improves information or action; gain must be shown against a task-relative comparator, including verification and transition cost.

## 3. Foundation → EA principles: a conditional, typed specialization

The existing owner of this derivation is [EA 02A — Foundation-to-Operational-Principle Derivation](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/02A_FOUNDATION_TO_OPERATIONAL_PRINCIPLE_DERIVATION_PROOF_v0.1.md). **02A derives P1–P6 from the richer EA Foundation/02 invariant set F-A–F-H**, not from only the three SA-F families above. A new bridge may show correspondence to SA; it may **not** silently replace F-C (finite effort), F-H (external authority) and the other EA premises.

Formally, writing \(\mathsf{SA}_{open,exist,pos}\) for the SA reading and \(C_{\mathrm{op}}\) for **separately justified operational facts** (decision scope, capacity/deadline, qualified claims, legitimate authority, time of use and non-substitutable owners), the scientifically honest direction is:

\[
\mathsf{SA}_{open,exist,pos}\ \land\ C_{\mathrm{op}}
\quad\overset{\text{typed EA operationalization}}{\leadsto}\quad
(P1,\ldots,P6).
\tag{B1}
\]

The arrow denotes a **reviewable interpretation/refinement**, not yet an unrestricted logical entailment or equivalence. Without \(C_{\mathrm{op}}\) one can model an open domain with scoped certificates while the system still ignores human authority or pursues an unbounded search; SA alone cannot force P2/P4.

## 4. Principles ↔ S1–S14: preserve the information *added* by requirements

[A19](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00K_A19_PRINCIPLE_REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY_AND_CONSERVATION_PROOF_v0.1.md) gives one **primary** P projection for every S, plus supporting P edges. Its full mapping above is a navigational condensation, **not** a many-to-one substitution of the actual requirements.

[A24](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00K_A24_PRINCIPLE_REQUIREMENT_INFORMATION_GAIN_AND_NON_EQUIVALENCE_v0.1.md) formalizes why the projection to principle labels is **non-injective**: S carries extra subjects, lifecycle, source, owner, scope, evidence, failure dispositions, KPIs and T conditions. Projecting S back to a P label discards these extra fields. A genuinely conservative relationship keeps **the original S/T object** addressable.

A minimal typed record is:

\[
r=(\mathrm{S\_id},\mathrm{P\_primary},\mathrm{P\_support},
\mathrm{claim},\mathrm{owner},\mathrm{subject},\mathrm{action},
\mathrm{scope},\mathrm{time},\mathrm{evidence},
\mathrm{residual},\mathrm{authority},\mathrm{T},\mathrm{test},\mathrm{status}).
\tag{B2}
\]

The record is **illustrative**, not a new runtime event schema. Source-native representations prevail; absent/not-applicable/prohibited-to-disclose fields must not be fabricated, and private facts need not be copied to another system. Conservation means that the **original qualified meaning can be reconstructed or referenced by an authorized reviewer**, not that every actor receives all data or that entropy monotonically increases.

The cross-source mapping is therefore two separate arrows:

\[
\text{P invariant}
\xrightarrow{\text{typed specification / forward trace}}
\text{S/T obligation}
\]

\[
\text{S/T conformance evidence}
\xrightarrow[\text{proof required, currently incomplete}]{\text{preservation test}}
\text{P invariant}.
\]

**Current reverse status:** [A23](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00K_A23_CANONICAL_REQUIREMENT_CONFORMANCE_SUFFICIENCY_P1_P6_v0.1.md) has **bounded finite projections for P1, P2 and P4 only**; it explicitly retains countermodels/unclosed obligations for **P3, P5 and P6**. No global theorem of S/T \(\Rightarrow\) all six P is adopted. The independent [S1–S14/T1–T4 owner](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00_CANONICAL_REQUIREMENTS_CHALLENGES_SUFFICIENCY_HYPOTHESES_KPIS.md) remains authoritative for full requirement clauses, including T1 material awareness, T2 qualification/handoff, T3 bounded authorized response and T4 evidence of problem-relevant effects.

## 7. Bounded model: where the partial experimental A/B/C/D agentic model sits

The agentic experimental model uses **process-relative classified information** plus ordinary operations such as coordination, feasible optimization and, where assumptions permit, repositioning resources at a bottleneck. Its important question is not whether classical optimization is invalid. It is:

> After a well-specified conventional method reaches its evidenced decision boundary or best feasible local result, can *qualified* information about remaining alternatives, existence/exclusion and changing context improve a decision **under equal legitimate constraints and full search/verification/transition costs**?

The toy may represent A/B/C/D information roles and partial multi-agent cooperation. It **does not** yet instantiate all of EP's signalling, cartography, RA, MSCA Type/posture, gradient, ACC/lineage, revocation, multi-group non-substitution, external authority and post-action evidence boundaries. No general architecture verification is inferred.

The test must preserve the classical competitor at its actual capable frontier, including resource reallocation and pivoting when admissible. A gain attributed to meta-information is only established where the comparator demonstrably does **not** already use equivalent information, and where the additional mechanism/measurement cost is included. A failure is reportable evidence, not a reason to redefine the conventional route as artificially weak.
