## 1. Three meanings of residual

At time t, let E_t be the finite event traces **actually available** before source admission, U_t the frame accepted by admission K, and A_t the qualified operative outputs. Let Q_t contain finite **observed but currently unresolved** events, with provenance. Distinguish Q_t from (i) the open semantic obligation A′_t and (ii) the potentially unobserved extensional exterior of U_t in the declared semantic universe.

An event can be represented as a **received unresolved trace** without its source peer, role or final semantic value being identified. This is a finite machine operation. It is not proof that the event originated physically outside U_t. If K dropped an event before any authorized capture and no independent acquisition/replay exists, no later metadata label can reconstruct it. A meta-observer that retains relevant pre-admission distinctions must operate **at or upstream of the lossy formalization event**.

## 2. SA-O1 — conditional witnessed residual

L1 OW-9 proves that, for the H-EXP effectively described peer family, every **sound, total, computable** three-valued evaluator of the fixed substantive question returns INDETERMINATE for at least one peer. If an effective encounter schedule exhaustively visits the family and the meta-interface preserves every such verdict, then

\[
\exists k<\infty:\quad Q_k\ne\varnothing .
\tag{1}
\]

**Proof:** OW-1 supplies an index lacking a sound definitive verdict; exhaustive traversal reaches it at some finite index, and retention records its UNKNOWN output.

There is **no uniform k**, no guarantee that an arbitrary deployment traverses H-EXP, and no inference that this witnessed UNKNOWN is an individually observed **outside-U physical agent**. A live finite residual and an open semantic exterior are different typed objects.

## 3. SA-O2 — retained difference versus irreversible discard

Let Z_C be a closed external report which permanently drops a particular received but not classifiable event and offers no replay; let Z_M preserve the same final results and a qualified trace of the event, so Z_C=g∘Z_M. If a later question r has two admissible states w_0,w_1 such that

\[
Z_C(w_0)=Z_C(w_1),\quad r(w_0)\ne r(w_1),
\quad Z_M(w_0)\ne Z_M(w_1),
\tag{2}
\]

then **no decoder of Z_C alone can answer r on both worlds**. If r additionally factors through Z_M and the decoder is effective, the retained carrier can.

**Proof:** equal reduced messages force equal downstream computed decisions, contradicting the distinct required r-values; factorization plus effective decoding proves the positive clause.

This is the original *Given Universe* loss-of-distinctions argument applied to **live retained evidence**. It is a conditional strict information opportunity for some future tasks, not a guarantee that every retained UNKNOWN will eventually become useful. An ordinary controller retaining identical information ties.

## 4. SA-O3 — when a closed filter really shrinks its candidate pool

For a **fixed candidate pool**, suppose a sequence of accepted-operand filters gives

\[
S_{t+1}=\{x\in S_t:F_{t+1}(x)=1\}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad S_{t+1}\subseteq S_t.
\tag{3}
\]

No rejected candidate reenters without replay, a new admission route or a rule revision. The proof is simple set inclusion. This **does not** mean canonical 00M A always becomes smaller, or that a stream with new incoming candidates shrinks. Ordinary syntactic programs can also support replay and rule revisions. The open-aware choice is to preserve **a separately qualified unresolved ledger** so that a context change need not always rediscover the old event.

## 5. SA-O4 — one certified test replacing ten object-level tests

Let D_10(x) be a ten-check decision process, for a particular task and validity profile κ, with substantive REJECT output. Suppose a cheap computable guard G has a qualified proof π that

\[
\forall x\in S_\kappa:\quad G(x)\Longrightarrow
D_{10}(x)=\mathrm{REJECT}.
\tag{4}
\]

Define D'_10,G to return REJECT immediately when π applies and G(x) is true; otherwise run the original D_10.

**Theorem:** For every eligible input, D'_10,G and D_10 yield **the same substantive accept/reject decision**. Proof: (4) proves the guarded branch and the other branch executes D_10 unchanged. This does not automatically preserve auxiliary side effects, logging or unrelated mandatory checks. An observed UNKNOWN may not be turned into a NO without a valid substantive proof.

Let N be the eligible invocations, c_G the per-input guard cost, K_π the **total discovery, source, qualification, installation and renewal** cost of π, p=Pr(G(X)=1) under a specified workload, and C_avoid(X) the cost of actual checks skipped on a G-positive case. Under additive common cost units, the guarded method is strictly cheaper than the specified baseline exactly when

\[
\boxed{
N p\,\mathbb E[C_{\rm avoid}(X)\mid G(X)=1]
>
N c_G+K_\pi .
}
\tag{5}
\]

This proves a nonempty **cost-advantage class**, not cheap discovery of G or improvement over an incumbent already using the same sound optimization. The guard can be derived from a **negative metaproperty** about a population without determining each excluded individual; its proof applies only within κ.

## 6. SA-O5 — on-the-fly context revision without necessarily recompiling

Let a resident meta-interpreter already represent rule state θ_t (task scope, guards, recorded unresolved cases, authority and validity). If it has effective procedures Revise and Interpret, an authorized and validated certificate e can update:

\[
\theta_{t+1}=\operatorname{Revise}(\theta_t,e),
\qquad
D_{t+1}=\operatorname{Interpret}(\mathcal P,\theta_{t+1}).
\tag{6}
\]

This is an effective **runtime state change**, not necessarily a redesigned binary. It may **expand** the interpreted scope of a retained Q-event when new qualified evidence arrives; **contract** search by installing a proved guard; or **hold** while no sound new interpretation exists. Neither rule discovery nor the availability of a previously invisible event is automatic. A universal computer can implement this meta-interpreter in ordinary instructions; no stronger Turing machine is claimed.

## 7. Connection with earlier mathematical sources and limits

**Shannon/Part II:** preserving a new task-relevant observed distinction may increase conditional target information. A constant OPEN flag may increase typed expressivity yet add **zero** conditional information about the current task. A correct cheap guard can save *computation* even if its theorem is already implied by shared formulas and adds zero Shannon information.

**R11/R12:** positive witnesses and reusable proof certificates may be faster to verify than to discover; all discovery and revalidation costs remain in K_π. **R14/R16:** information coverage and compression statistics are not substitutes for task sufficiency or processing work. **Semantic Window:** a certified guard must be requalified when its question, evidence or context changes. **Given Universe:** only information captured before irreversible admission loss can subsequently be reconsidered.

**Closed-world nuance:** metatheoretic program optimization, one-test guards, memoization and dynamic rule engines can also improve performance in a completely closed finite model. The *specific* open-world architectural improvement is **refusing to promote current unclassifiability to permanent global irrelevance** and preserving a reviewable source admission frontier.

These conditions support an actual **operational plasticity class**: meta-operations can change the task-relevant interpretation or search cost **during execution**, not only for a later human designer. They do **not** guarantee that every open ecosystem yields an observed relevant unknown or that every meta-controller is efficient. No new tests are conducted by this document.
