## 1. What the reviews have found

The articles provide several useful research directions: information sufficient for one legitimate action, partial translation between perspectives, qualified reuse of evidence, revalidation after a source changes, honest treatment of unknown coverage, preservation of hidden capability, and the human effort required to obtain clarity and act in time.

Some formulations need localized correction: reversed formula/sign conventions, overly strong quantifiers, mismatched citations or a falsifier pointing in the expected direction. Other claims need further definitions, proof, instrument validation, temporal observation or comparison. These are different findings. **An additional proof or measurement belongs in development; its absence is not itself an article refutation.** Correcting a local claim need not change the essential direction of discovery, although it can change the valid scope or numerical interpretation.

The mathematical records contain conditional constructions and bounded reviewer fixtures. The other records contain working-paper/field-reconstruction analysis, measurement proposals, rival explanations and prospective designs. They do not all have the same evidence or coverage. R02 is a partly reconstructed compound source; D15's full standalone original remains unavailable in the recorded review. Tests of finite models do not establish universal physical claims, real human effects or product superiority.

The entries below are intentionally short. Each article title links to the **review document**, not merely to the original paper. The linked review contains the source identity, argument, derivations, localized findings, development obligations and scientific comparisons. Mathematical review links target the private workbench; other review links target controlled Google Docs. Their existing access permissions remain unchanged. This public annex does not reproduce restricted manuscripts or release private evidence.

## 4. What can contribute to DDS

The near-term contribution is an inspectable specification or discriminating case, not a predeclared success for our approach. The main routes are:

- **Decision-relative evidence:** determine which lawful distinctions are needed for a fixed receiving action, and what they cost to obtain/check.
- **Reuse and selective revalidation:** retain sufficient current support while withdrawing stale or inapplicable source/permission paths; compare actual provenance and verification mechanisms.
- **Unknown coverage and capability continuity:** distinguish observed count, qualified estimate, legitimate closure and UNKNOWN; test actual preservation of mission-critical capability rather than component count.
- **Human capacity and acquisition burden:** distinguish free time, competent review, authority, response latency and effective action; price looking, deciding and absorbing without double-counting.

These directions connect to the existing [DDS Stage A/B/C method](../ecosystem-awareness/DDS_CANONICAL_METHOD_INDEX_v0.1.md). A Stage A candidate studies a specification against a frozen Challenge; Stage B verifies an architecture against that package; Stage C validates a pinned implementation through observed effects. Pure mathematics, causal organizational research and human-retention studies keep their own scientific methods when they do not test a technical DDS object.

The detailed, accumulating [DDS Potential Contributions Register](../ecosystem-awareness/DDS_POTENTIAL_CONTRIBUTIONS_REGISTER_v0.1.md) contains 20 candidate families with source crosswalks, strong comparators, proposed studies, development and acceptance conditions. All transfers in its initial release remain **PROPOSED**. An existing competent method may tie, cost less or already solve the case; that is useful evidence rather than a reason to weaken the comparator.

- **MIT CISR:** decision preconditions, authority and the cost of acquiring usable source information; distinguish its eligible enterprise-executive interview intake from a possible methodological conversation.
- **TechConnect:** actual versus intended technology use and qualified compensatory work; compare existing complementarity instruments with independent task/effect outcomes.
- **SkillAIbility:** human–AI complementarity, competence retention, transfer and recovery; distinguish immediate assisted performance from longitudinal skill preservation.

These are proposed reciprocal studies. Sources, current routes, first experiments, strong nulls and preparation needs are in the linked document and individual reviews. No outside expressed interest, accepted partnership, participant access or executed joint study is inferred. A bibliography alone is not a collaboration opportunity; a useful proposal must state what each side could contribute and what result would show the current method already suffices.
