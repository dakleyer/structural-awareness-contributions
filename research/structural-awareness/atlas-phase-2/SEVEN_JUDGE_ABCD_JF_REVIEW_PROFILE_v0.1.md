# Seven-judge review of situated epistemic paths

The Atlas qualifies a relationship occurrence within a declared path, rather than assigning an epistemic role to the relationship once and for all. Seven judges examine the same positioned occurrence independently. Three passes use fresh contexts before the results are compared. This method turns disagreement into a precise source question and keeps editorial improvements local to the distinction that needs them.

## 1. The object being judged

An assessment identifies the graph revision, dictionary version, origin node, activity, question, scope, effective evaluation capability, assessment time and complete ordered path prefix. Each step references the original Level 1 edge, its traversal direction, the source items used and the proposed ABCD role with a specific explanation.

The meanings remain those of [00M v0.8, section 1](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/atlas/phase-2-epistemic-distance-20261104/research/ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md#abcd-canonical):

- **A:** the functional result or use actually established and delivered by the declared process.
- **B:** the established basis, limits or characterized reserve that the process can defensibly evaluate.
- **C:** a grounded exploration route whose evaluation basis remains insufficiently characterized.
- **D:** potentially material influence beyond that process's effective route to evaluation under its declared conditions.

The [Atlas traversal procedure, section 4](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/blob/atlas/phase-2-epistemic-distance-20261104/research/structural-awareness/atlas-phase-2/PROCEDIMIENTO_LENGUAJE_Y_RECORRIDOS_v0.2.md) governs continuity, relevance and direction. An irrelevant step is cut for lack of relevance. A judgment with insufficient grounds remains open for review. Consecutive C steps are possible. Tags help identify descriptions and source conditions; they do not determine ABCD by themselves.

The confirmed stopping rule follows the canonical procedure: continue through A/B/C and terminate that branch at D. Iván explicitly corrected an earlier first-B transcription. B is not a traversal terminal. Irrelevance, cycles, unavailable judgments, budget limits and end-of-graph keep their own recorded causes; they are not renamed D.

The separate, restricted Jumps & Falls review does not change that rule. Its current pilot evaluates F1–F4 only on already qualified A/B occurrences. C remains available for ABCD continuation toward D, while its F reading stays outside this experiment's scope.

## 2. Seven criteria, shared responsibility

Every judge reads the declared position and judges the complete occurrence. The criteria focus attention without dividing responsibility for the final meaning.

| Judge | Primary criterion | Central question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 | Source fidelity and hooks | Does the exact source, revision and passage support this reading and preserve its voice? |
| J2 | Mathematical reasoning | Are the objects, quantifiers, premises and evaluation method sufficient for the proposed role? |
| J3 | Operating mechanism and chronology | Is this actual use, a proposed use or a later comparison, and whose activity is described? |
| J4 | Whole-path relevance and direction | Does the step remain relevant to the origin question through the entire prefix, in a defensible direction? |
| J5 | ABCD boundary discrimination | What is the strongest alternative role, and which evidence distinguishes it? |
| J6 | Explanation and taxonomy | Does the card explain the needed distinction accurately, with suitable typed tags and sufficient context? |
| J7 | Adversarial interpretation and distortion control | Could shared assumptions, selective evidence or an unnecessary edit produce misleading agreement? |

J7 also checks the proposed meaning invariants before any editorial change. This role does not acquire authority to rewrite the corpus or override another judge without an evidential reason.

## 3. Three independent elicitation passes

Each role/pass runs in a fresh context with the same identified inputs. Judges receive no other verdicts, previous-pass results or suggested group answer before submitting their own record.

1. **First judgment.** Read the position, both endpoint cards, the relation and the relevant original source. State the role, its basis and the strongest competing interpretation.
2. **Source challenge.** A fresh run re-examines the source and searches for a counter-reading, missing condition or mismatch between the source and the position.
3. **Stability and minimal-edit review.** Another fresh run tests whether the role survives a careful reading of the full prefix and whether any proposed card amendment is necessary and local.

This produces up to 21 independently elicited records per positioned occurrence. Operational independence means isolated contexts, sealed outputs and absence of mutual persuasion. The model family, shared evidence and common instructions remain recorded; agreement is interpreted as reproducibility under those inputs.

Source access is recorded at its actual level: original passage read, card read, locator checked or source unavailable. An inaccessible source does not become a read source because the panel agrees about its title.

## 4. Sealed records and comparison

The comparison key is the positioned occurrence: origin, activity, question, capability, graph/source revisions and ordered path prefix. Two judgments on the same edge under different origins remain separate records.

Each record retains its role, reason, alternative considered, position fitness, source selectors and proposed amendments. No result overwrites an earlier pass. The integrator compares:

- the proposed ABCD role;
- the meaning and scope of the reasons;
- agreement about the source and its conditions;
- the strongest unresolved alternative;
- any proposed change to a node or tag.

Matching letters alone do not establish substantive agreement. Different letters can also expose a misplaced question or two aspects that deserve separate assessment. Counts organize the review; they do not replace source adjudication.

An adopted role needs a source-supported explanation that satisfies its recognition criteria. A reasoned objection about mathematical validity, source identity, direction or position remains open until the specific evidence resolves it. A majority cannot turn missing evidence into an observed result.

### Evidence binding and citation-only errata

Structural validation checks coverage, identifier types, permitted factors and sealed fingerprints. A separate source audit checks that each item ID, URI, revision and passage selector identify the same original. Membership in the item register alone does not validate that binding. A line selector from a mathematical paper cannot be attached to a catalogue simply because the catalogue is a registered item.

If a sealed record contains a citation serialization error, preserve it byte-for-byte. A separate erratum identifies the exact JSON pointer, original binding, corrected binding and the pre-seal source-read entry supporting the correction. Another reviewer checks every mapping. Unsupported mappings remain unresolved. A correction to a citation does not become a new vote or silently change a role, reason, alternative or reading claim.

The human reader displays the defect and its erratum together. Consolidation uses verified original passages and retains the defective references as provenance records. A correction cannot retroactively claim that an unread source was read.

## 5. Controlled convergence without a debating crowd

Independent judgments come first. Only after they are sealed may the integrator identify two or more judges who independently noticed the same missing distinction.

If useful, those judges receive one bounded reconciliation request: formulate the smallest source-supported correction to that particular span. One exchange and one candidate text are enough for this stage. They do not negotiate a new interpretation of the whole corpus.

The integrator records the independent observations, the shared source, the candidate wording and any remaining difference. If the source does not settle the question, the existing text remains and the follow-up stays identified.

## 6. What “surgical” means

A surgical amendment has a located problem, a minimal textual or tag change and explicit meaning invariants.

Its record includes:

1. The node ID, exact affected span and current record fingerprint.
2. The item ID, source revision and passage that justify the change.
3. The missing or inaccurate distinction and why it matters to this positioned assessment.
4. The exact proposed replacement or insertion.
5. The conditions, chronology, source voice and evidential strength that must remain intact.
6. The independent proposals supporting it, dissenting reasons and disposition.
7. The applied difference and post-change validation, if it is actually integrated.

For the first review cycle, a local prose proposal is limited to one to three sentences and at most 75 added words. A larger correction becomes a separate follow-up. This practical limit prevents a local review from becoming a rewrite.

Typical amendments define an omitted variable, retain a mathematical premise, identify the actor of an implementation, distinguish a retrospective comparison, or add a source-supported tag. A new taxonomy key is proposed with its definition and closest existing keys.

The unaffected text remains byte-identical. Stable node and item IDs, source hooks, relationships and earlier assessment records remain resolvable. The amendment does not rename entities, change dates, strengthen causation, convert a hypothesis into a fact, add generic reservations or place universal ABCD labels on the graph.

## 7. Integrate one bounded change and review its consequences

Before writing, match the exact span and fingerprint. If the card has changed meanwhile, reconcile against its current version. Apply only the accepted local difference and validate the card's explanation, item hook, typed tags and retained relationships.

Re-evaluate the positioned assessments that used the changed distinction. Keep their prior version and explain the change in the new record. Do not reclassify unrelated paths merely because they contain the same word.

Use existing version control and comparison hashes. Storage follows the workspace's policy; this method requires no new backup, source mirror or pre-write copy.

## 8. Coverage and continuation

Coverage is reported by origin and positioned path occurrence, judge and pass. Distinct-edge coverage alone cannot establish that every accumulated prefix has been assessed.

For every declared batch, report expected and received judgments, omitted occurrences, source-read scope, agreement and disagreement, accepted or rejected amendments, actual path terminals and the remaining queue. Initial departure registration, semantic assessment and completed traversal are different completed activities.

The process remains incremental: a new source distinction can improve a card, create a useful relationship or change a later situated qualification. Earlier nodes, edges and reviewed positions remain part of the record.

## 9. A separate seven-judge reading for Jumps & Falls

The same seven criteria and three fresh-context passes can examine the functional reading after ABCD has been qualified. Each factor belongs to a positioned occurrence, never to the Level 1 edge or node globally. Each assigned factor has its own source-based reason. Multiple factors and an explicit abstention are possible.

The general source procedure defines F1 as input and cost of usable clarity, F2 as processing and proportional means, F3 as output/objective/value, and F4 as collateral effects. F4 is transverse to the input–processing–output chain. These are Jumps & Falls factors, not similarly named factors in other research architectures.

Iván's **restricted experimental profile of 5 November 2026** has the following eligibility:

| ABCD occurrence | Factors eligible for examination | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | JF.F1, JF.F2, JF.F3 | Examine actual input, processing and output functions where the declared activity supports that reading. |
| B | JF.F2, JF.F3, JF.F4 | This is the region in which F4 is examined; F2 or F3 can also be justified by the actual function. No factor follows automatically from B. |
| C | None in this profile | Record `excluded_by_scope`; continue the ABCD analysis without exploring a JF factor here. |
| D | None in this profile | Record `excluded_by_scope` and stop this ABCD branch at its genuine D boundary. |
| Unresolved ABCD | None yet | Preserve the unresolved assessment; do not use a factor to decide its ABCD role. |

Under this bounded profile F1 is examined on A, and F4 only on B. The requested predominance concerns where the factors operate; it supplies no quota or statistical prior saying that most B occurrences must be F4. This is a local experimental scope, not a redefinition of the general factor meanings: the earlier source procedure permits wider factor/component combinations. Its examples and history remain identifiable. Later expansion requires a named profile revision.

Reviewers distinguish `not_applicable` (the declared activity does not support the transformation lens), `review_pending` (eligible but insufficiently supported), and `excluded_by_scope` (C/D or unresolved ABCD in this profile). A mathematical or documentary card does not acquire a business process by being read with the factor vocabulary.

ABCD observations are sealed before the factor panel receives its adopted ABCD inputs. Factor judges do not read peer factor verdicts. Their criteria respectively challenge source fidelity, formal conditions, operating mechanism, whole-path fit, factor alternatives, explanation/tag precision, and distortion. Comparison remains source-based, without majority adoption or forced F4 assignment.

## 10. Experiment diary without conditioning the judgments

A separate process diary records what actually happened: inputs read, access or identity problems, observed effort, points that required interpretation, useful information and proposed improvements to the method. It references the sealed judgment records rather than rewriting them.

The [5 November experiment diary](https://github.com/dakleyer/perspective-beyond/blob/main/methodology/experiments/seven-judge-diary-2026-11-05.md) records the actual first-batch observations, bounded exchange, citation correction and continuation checkpoint.

Judges make short process observations **after sealing**. The diary distinguishes a verbatim comment, an integrator's summary and a measured metric. It never invents dialogue, time measurements or agreement. Only an actual bounded reconciliation exchange is recorded as a conversation.

The diary is not part of a fresh judge's input packet. An operational repair can be recorded and made available neutrally when necessary; interpretive observations remain outside blind input. A method change takes a versioned decision with its reason. The amount of disagreement, difficulty or agreement alone does not decide a node's meaning.
