# Positioned residual reuse and local adaptation — three epistemic regimes, Turing limits, telework and sensor examples

**Status:** Structural Awareness working mathematical research, Stage A; proposed annex to PR #62, owner review pending. **Not** an adopted axiom, new oracle, closed-world census, claim of universal speedup, empirical demonstration, DDS Stage C result, or replacement of the canonical A/B/C/D definitions.

**Research question.** Can an agentic or other operating process reuse a *small, deliberately selected* portion of the unresolved evidence encountered by its current exploitation procedures, because metastructural positioning identifies which process boundary is worth examining, **without** first enumerating the exterior, constructing a global exploration generator, or knowing which epistemic regime describes the wider world?

## 1. The contribution, stated narrowly

There are **two distinct selections**; neither is a general-purpose capture of residuals:

1. **Architectural/metasyntactic positioning, before residual counts are available.** From a bounded and sourced map of processes, dependencies, questions, interfaces, declared scope, constraints, established exploitation reserve, and available authority, qualify *which process or cross-dimensional boundary* is worth examining for a declared decision. This yields a **candidate observation site**, not a list of invisible objects, and does not imply that a useful exterior object exists.
2. **Selective residual reuse at the selected site.** Let the already operating process continue its exploitation task; where accessible, observe/replay specific unresolved or timed-out events that arrive in the process's existing input flow. Retain and requalify **only** those that are relevant to the local decision. No complete enumeration, screening of every unknown, or global reclassification is required.

A third, independently validated **decision-specific requalification** may convert a selected case into an authorized, locally useful action. A successful local intervention may improve an ecosystem-level objective, but that implication requires a separately qualified dependency/aggregation proof. The source event need not become a globally classified or universally adopted solution.

**Why metasyntax first?** In the motivating situation we do **not** have a trustworthy heat map of where all UNKNOWNs occur. If we already possessed a complete useful UNKNOWN census, a conventional count/ranking rule could choose where to inspect. The metasyntactic step instead uses **structural information about possible blind spots**: the map and geometry of where observations, models, contracts, capacity, authority, and crossing dependencies cease to cover the declared question. A rear-view-mirror analogy is helpful: the architecture identifies where a vehicle *could* be unseen; it does not transmit the unseen vehicle or prove one is there. We inspect a selected blind-spot position rather than scanning the entire road.

Nothing here assumes that raw unseen objects were communicated. The available evidence is a *description of observational reach and limitations*, together with whatever encountered event traces are genuinely accessible. Metaprocessing cannot reconstruct a previously erased input unless replay or another legitimate acquisition path exists.

## 2. Keep the three kinds of residual and the adopted A/B/C/D taxonomy separate

Use three distinct types:

- \(\mathcal R_{U}\): the **open, decision-relevant semantic residual** relative to bounded representation \(U\), **not** an enumerated complement \(\Omega\setminus U\).
- \(A'_r\): a **relative open semantic obligation** concerning the current producer's determination, **not** a known set of exterior members and not simply \(U\setminus A_r\).
- \(Q_{i,t}\): **finite, witnessed and accessible but unresolved records** encountered by process \(i\) at time \(t\), with qualification and provenance; potentially a selected subledger rather than every available unknown.

Canonical \(\Pi_i(d,t)=(A_i,B_i,C_i,D_i)\) remains **process-, question- and time-relative**, non-exhaustive and not necessarily disjoint:

| Canonical role | Meaning preserved for this annex |
| --- | --- |
| \(A\) | A result of active exploitation, whether that result is a positive determination, a *sound negative determination*, a scoped estimate, or another actual functional output. |
| \(B\) | The established basis and characterized, assessable reserve; an operational claim that this reserve is inadequate/exhausted for a specific objective requires separate evidence. |
| \(C\) | A grounded but not yet adequately characterized exploration avenue. No inference that every UNKNOWN belongs to C, nor that a member of C has been detected. |
| \(D\) | Potentially material influences beyond the current effective evaluation route; not an enumerable known class. |

Missing labels remain UNKNOWN/NOT DECLARED. A timeout or an out-of-scope trace is **not automatically C or D**. It may evidence a boundary *related to* a proposed C investigation; source validity, evaluability and permitted actions remain separate.

For simple three-way *object-predicate* examples use \(+\) (qualified predicate true), \(-\) (qualified predicate false), and \(?\) (undetermined for the declared question). Do **not** use \(A,-A,?\) as though they were the canonical A/B/C/D: both a qualified \(+\) and a qualified \(-\) can be an A result when classification is the operating task. Timeout, BAD_FRAME, authorization denial and source absence remain separately typed and are not an exhaustive logical triad.

## 3. The three epistemic regimes and the same bounded procedure

These are **alternative evidence regimes or analytical scenarios**, not a mutually exclusive global partition that a machine must decide before acting. The session's working operational phrasing is *conditional non-closure; actual/possible residual indeterminacy; processor-relative positioning*. This is **not** an unauthorized replacement of the existing SA foundation characterization families SA-F1 (qualified non-closure), SA-F2 (scoped existence/exclusion) and SA-F3 (positioning), nor of EA's separate P1–P6.

| Regime | What is warranted | Possible advantage; exact limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E — scoped existence/exclusion is certified** | A valid certificate may establish that a target-relevant possibility exists outside the current reach, or exclude a defined subset, without individuating or enumerating all exterior members. | Target-specific existence can change a rational explore/no-explore decision (SA-E2); scoped exclusion can prune work (SA-E3). Neither generic openness nor an unverified assertion of existence supplies a witness, feasible new action or guaranteed time saving. |
| **I — indetermination is witnessed** | At least one actual received case remains unresolved for a processor/question under its declared scope, e.g. qualified UNKNOWN or finite-budget TIMEOUT; the record is available before loss. | Retention and selective later requalification may strictly distinguish task states that a lossy conventional report cannot (SA-O2); feasible interpreter revision can act without recompiling all source functions (SA-O5). This neither proves permanent nontermination nor reveals physically unseen agents. |
| **P — only possible residual indetermination** | The architecture cannot justify universal closure; a blind-spot position is qualified without any positive existence proof or received unresolved case. A domain may be simple or even adequately closed for the current task. | Avoid unjustified certainty and use a bounded structural watch or explicit no-action posture. There may be **no** information, speed or outcome gain; a fully informed conventional Turing implementation can tie. |

The three conditional principles *change strength across positions*, not truth by fiat:

1. **Qualified non-closure:** do not extend a bounded scope's closure to an undeclared universe. Formal closure *within an expressly proved frame* is permitted.
2. **Actual or possible residual indeterminacy:** distinguish a witnessed unresolved case from the unproven possibility that one might arise. Neither openness nor a timeout implies a useful candidate.
3. **Positioning:** index each claim by \(i,d,t,\sigma,\rho,\kappa\) (processor, decision, time, scope, resources and legitimacy/evidence profile). A fixed proposition's truth does not change merely by moving the assessor; its evidential status, question, applicability and permitted operation can.

All three cases can use the **same operational sequence**: architectural qualification \(\Phi\), selected-source observation \(W\), and scoped local requalification \(\Psi_d\). The procedure need not decide whether the world is E, I or P if its *local* answer is supported independently of that global classification. Formally, for worlds \(w\) consistent with the acquired evidence, decision independence requires a certificate that \(\operatorname{Valid}_d(x,w)\) yields the same authorized conclusion on every relevant \(w\), or else a reported bounded/conditional result. Positioning is what makes this invariance question explicit; the invariance is not automatic.

## 4. Architectural localization without an UNKNOWN histogram

Let \(G_t=(V_t,E_t,L_t)\) be a **bounded, sourced architectural graph**: processors/agents, interfaces, declared coverage, process questions, input/output contracts, constraints, dependencies, observation windows, authority and available evidence. \(G_t\) is not a census of the physical ecosystem.

Let \(d\) be a concrete objective (e.g. adequate work conditions without acquiring another office). Define an **illustrative**, non-normative structural operator

\[
\Phi(G_t,d,\kappa)\longrightarrow
\{(\text{process},\text{interface/boundary},\text{reason},\text{scope},\text{confidence},\text{candidate check})\}.
\]

A qualified target can be selected when evidence shows jointly that:

- the **known B-side options** provide insufficient remaining improvement under the objective's hard constraints (an actual assessment, *not* just a low percentage of positive outputs);
- a dependency/cross-dimensional interaction makes a particular observation boundary material to \(d\);
- the architecture gives a plausible *route for a bounded inspection* at that boundary;
- costs, source permissions, safety and authority allow the inspection.

This identifies **where to look**, not **what hidden element is there**. It does not require observing all indeterminate outcomes before selecting a sensor. If the structural graph is untrusted, too incomplete, or equivocal, the honest output is CANDIDATE/UNKNOWN/NO JUSTIFIED TARGET, not fabricated localization. The cost of learning and maintaining \(G_t\) must be counted.

Architecture alone does **not** prove the existence, frequency, usefulness or mathematical proximity of residual cases. In particular, \(C\) is an epistemic exploration frontier, not a set with a defined metric distance from \(B\). The metaphor that C lies "near" B refers only to a *justified structural relation between a bottleneck and an encountered limitation*, unless a metric and theorem are independently supplied.

## 5. A three-way exploitation machine degenerates to a two-way observable task — with exact limits

For a declared predicate and qualified input distribution \(\mu_t\), let a **total, sound under its stated semantics** evaluator produce

\[
M_i(x)\in\{+,-,?\},\qquad
p_++p_-+p_?=1.
\]

If, on a **particular task-scoped traffic distribution**, \(p_+\le\varepsilon_t\) and \(\varepsilon_t\to0\), then \(p_-+p_?\to1\). On the restricted observed cases whose output is not \(+\), the same evaluator is *exactly* a two-output discriminator \(-\) versus \(?\); no new general exploration discriminator is needed for already witnessed responses.

**Negative control:** \(p_+\to0\) alone says nothing about how many unknowns occur. One process may emit \(p_-=1-\varepsilon,p_?=0\), another \(p_-=(1-\varepsilon)/2,p_?=(1-\varepsilon)/2\). Both have equally few positives. Low \(p_+\) also does **not** prove B exhausted: traffic mix, bad calibration or changed questions can produce it, and the full machine may still spend the same computation checking the positive branch.

A modest conditional residual-concentration result *is* valid:

\[
p_+\le\varepsilon_t,\quad p_-\le h<1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
p_?\ge1-h-\varepsilon_t.
\tag{R1}
\]

This concerns a **declared, admitted input distribution**, not unobserved exterior elements, and does not entail that any unknown is a useful solution. A stream will accumulate retained UNKNOWN records only if observed arrivals outpace qualified resolution/discard; no permanent residual queue is presumed.

**Why the original machine is useful:** for an input it actually processes, its own qualified \(-/?\) outcome is the appropriate operational witness of *its* determination boundary. Replacing this by a new inferred characterization of "what usually causes UNKNOWN" is not required to recognize the actual witnessed UNKNOWNs. It can nevertheless be helpful in other designs, and a strong classical comparator can do the same monitoring.

## 6. Conditional constructive proposition: select, reuse, requalify

Let \(\mathcal E_t\) be events **actually available** in an admitted or replayable stream, \(s=\Phi(G_t,d,\kappa)\) a justified selected process, and

\[
Q_{s,t}\subseteq\mathcal E_t
\]

the **accessible** unresolved traces of that process, with input reference, qualification and provenance. No assumption \(Q_{s,t}=\mathcal R_U\) or any knowledge of its complete membership.

Let a bounded selector produce **only a task-relevant subset** \(S_{s,d,t}\subseteq Q_{s,t}\), possibly by event-triggered observation without first traversing all of \(Q_{s,t}\). A second ordinary computable evaluation \(\Psi_d(x,\kappa)\) checks the task's genuinely necessary constraints, authority and possible local value. The exploitation function remains available unchanged; a meta-observer or rule interpreter may operate alongside it.

**Proposition SR-1 (conditional local recovery).** If there exists \(x^*\in S_{s,d,t}\) that the finite procedure actually examines, and independently valid local evidence proves both admissibility and \(\Delta V_d(x^*)>0\), then the procedure can deliver a **scoped local improvement** without enumerating \(\mathcal R_U\), determining E/I/P globally, constructing a new candidate-generating population model, or deciding the general halting problem.

*Proof sketch.* \(\Phi\) identifies a site from finite architecture/evidence. The normal source or qualified replay supplies the witnessed \(x^*\). The bounded selector and local verifier execute effective procedures on this trace. The assumption supplies a sufficient certificate for the local positive decision. None of the steps requires answering a universal query about unreceived exterior members. \(\square\)

The proposition is **conditional on the witness and certificate**: it does not prove that B exhaustion *implies* such a witness, that every useful candidate will reach the selected process, or that targeted selection always finds the best local or global option.

For a finite selected admissible neighborhood \(S\), "local best" means best **among the verified candidates actually compared**, e.g.

\[
x_S^*\in\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{x\in S\cap\mathcal F_\kappa}
\bigl(\Delta V_d(x)-C_d(x)\bigr),
\]

not an optimum over the unbounded ecosystem. If the operating objective has multiple hard constraints, a previously indeterminate hard requirement **cannot** be ignored: the local decision needs a proved irrelevant condition, a valid restriction, an independently authorized contract/amendment, or explicit non-acceptance.

### A stronger probabilistic research hypothesis, not a theorem of openness

Suppose task-relevant proposals are repeatedly generated *by the existing actors*, a positive share reach the selected source's observation interface, unresolved traces survive the lossy boundary, and selected cases have conditional probability at least \(p>0\) of admitting a justified local improvement under a stated checking procedure. Then after \(n\) such attempts the probability of observing at least one improvement is at least \(1-(1-p)^n\) when the lower bound holds conditional on prior attempts.

The important unknown is **why \(p>0\)** and how structural positioning makes this lower bound plausible. Neither diagonalization, non-closure, a shrinking B-reserve, nor a timeout alone supplies it. Selection bias, hard rejections, data loss, correlated attempts and hostile environments are countermodels. An agentic ecosystem may continuously propose local adaptations, but that is an **extra source/generation premise**, not a universal property of Turing machines.

## 7. Worked example A — telework before 2020 (illustrative 2018–2019 firm)

**Scenario, not a historical prevalence study.** In 2019 an illustrative software company has **120 staff and 100 on-site workstations**. Its employees face travel congestion, scheduling constraints, requests for more home-like ergonomic spaces, and a **hard budget prohibition** against buying the adjacent premises.

1. **Conventional exploitation remains productive** for furniture/ergonomics, shared spaces and staggered arrival times. We do **not** inspect every unresolved case in those dimensions.
2. The **capacity/premises budget** is a confirmed hard **NO** for the particular action "purchase the adjacent premises"; it is **not** an indeterminate and must not be relabelled C.
3. A sourced architecture map relates **space capacity, on-site presence, travel distance, ownership/insurance and contractual authority**. The problem is a cross-dimensional binding dependency: improving schedules alone does not create the required physical capacity, and acquiring extra premises is not authorized.
4. Existing worker requests to work remotely one or more days may have **reached the normal process** but received UNKNOWN, HOLD, scope mismatch or an explicit policy refusal. These statuses must remain distinct: a **validly determined NO** is not retroactively a mathematical UNKNOWN. The meta-operation selects **this specific boundary** for review, without characterizing the whole labor-market or societal universe.
5. A bounded **local requalification** checks whether a subset of those requests can meet the *current* employer objective and hard requirements through an authorized local arrangement: workstation ergonomics, information security, labor/insurance obligations, supervisor consent, service continuity and a documented arrangement for home-office use. Unknown legal/authority conditions cannot simply be waived by calling them residual.
6. **Illustrative sufficient local calculation:** if the firm can *guarantee* that at least 20 of 120 staff are off-site on every relevant working day, and no special seat constraints intervene, on-site peak demand is at most 100 seats. A mere *average* of 20 remote workers is **not** enough; the peak schedule must be proved. This demonstrates how a limited set of local cases may satisfy one organization-wide capacity constraint without establishing that telework is globally preferable for society.
7. Source demand and the proposed mode already existed; neither the company nor the meta-observer needs a future pandemic or societal-wide relabeling to test it. Whether remote work yields positive net ergonomics, safety or productivity remains an empirical/contractual question for this company, not guaranteed by the arithmetic.

**Why this illustrates the mechanism:** the meta-operation identifies the **capacity × distance × premises** boundary *from architecture and constraints*, not from a map of all employee indeterminates. It revisits a **small group of requests already encountered** and seeks an authorized local improvement. This can affect a wider organizational problem while leaving global social adoption and the classification of other residual requests unresolved.

**What would falsify the case:** no previously submitted requests; no preserved evidence/replay; those requests were sound hard rejections under irrevocable rules; the home workspace is unlawful/unsafe; service requires full presence; or a fair conventional scheduling/telework assessment produces the identical outcome with the same information and lower cost.

## 8. Worked example B — sensor timeout and why the original sensor is sufficient

**Positioning first; no UNKNOWN histogram.** An ecosystem contains many agents/sensors. The structural map indicates that a specific sensor \(M_s\) is the one whose observation window, process question and dependency interface limit a material decision. This conclusion comes from declared coverage/capability, process relationships and bottlenecks, **not** from counting all unknown events across every sensor. It qualifies a site worth checking, not an assertion that its timeouts are frequent or valuable.

Let a bounded watchdog observe that request \(x\) reached \(M_s\) at time \(t\) under context \(c\), but no **qualified response** arrived by a declared finite deadline \(\tau\):

\[
\operatorname{TO}_{\tau}(M_s,x,c)=1
\iff
\text{no qualified output was witnessed within the declared budget}.
\tag{S1}
\]

For a deterministic abstract machine with a fixed finite **step** budget, this predicate is decidable by bounded simulation. For real wall-clock devices it is an **observed, context-dependent operational event**: queuing, network delay, load, configuration and fault can matter. TIMEOUT is **not** proof of permanent divergence or semantic truth of an exterior proposition.

**Direct route (preferred for already arriving inputs):**

1. Keep the regular exploitation sensor running.
2. At or upstream of the lossy boundary, retain a minimal task-relevant input reference and context until a watchdog can classify an actual TIMEOUT; the sensor itself may be blocked and need external monitoring.
3. Select **only timeouts linked to the metasyntactically chosen observation site and task**, not all global UNKNOWNs and not necessarily all of that sensor's timeouts.
4. Locally reexamine selected cases against the *new* decision/question, with proof/evidence/authorization and bounded effort. The original sensor need not change role.

**Optional sandbox/replay route:** if a new candidate must be tested or a witnessed event reconstructed, use the same sensor or a sufficiently equivalent copy with controlled input/context. A copy is **not required** to recognize timeouts of an already monitored input stream, and an "exact" copy of hardware or a stateful distributed process is generally not an exact reproduction of wall-clock timeout conditions.

**Why no separate universal exploration generator?** The normal sensor already implements the relevant *bounded discrimination on its own encountered inputs*; a watchdog supplies a finite event witness. A second machine that first infers the shape of all timeouting inputs, synthesizes candidates and then retests them is an optional **alternative**, not a logical prerequisite. The metadata layer selects *which sensor* before seeing a complete graph of all timeouts. The output can be a single valuable case, not a census of \(A'\).

**Limits:** an input erased before capture with no permitted replay cannot be recreated; unknown inputs never received cannot be discovered by monitoring; a TIMEOUT does not imply that requalification has value; ordinary Turing-computable controllers can implement identical watchdogs, replay and rule updates.

## 9. Exact comparison with Turing machines, oracle machines and conventional alternatives

| Comparator or claim | Defensible result | Forbidden inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal ordinary Turing machine | An effective meta-observer, structural graph scorer, watchdog, rule interpreter and local verifier can be simulated by an ordinary universal Turing program (Part I compiler theorem TR-19). | New super-Turing computational class, solving halting generally, or a distinct nonmechanical faculty. |
| Conventional system discarding unresolved received events | For certain later questions, qualified retention of a previously dropped source distinction yields **strict task-relevant information opportunity** (SA-O2). | Every UNKNOWN will become useful; one may recover events never captured. |
| Conventional system requiring global reclassification or a fresh candidate generator | Selective bounded reuse can be **cheaper for the specified task/workload** if architectural qualification, monitoring, selection, verification and context costs are lower than the actual avoided generator/classification/search costs. | That every conventional Turing implementation requires global closure or that an arbitrarily strong classical optimizer is inferior. |
| Strong, fully informed Turing-computable adaptive comparator | If it has the same qualified input/history, source/authority, operations and matched resources, it can implement the same method; **no strict superiority** from the SA label alone (SA-U10). | Universal computational or informational dominance of SA over equivalent algorithms. |
| Turing 1939 oracle machine | An oracle answers prescribed queries in a relative computation; **it does not need to enumerate or close all undecidables first**. The present mechanism does not receive an arbitrary noncomputable oracle answer. | Calling selective witnessed-timeout testing "an oracle" in the formal Turing sense; suggesting oracle machines must solve every possible indeterminate. |
| Cantor/Gödel/Turing diagonal or meta-definition | Shows expressibility limits/nonuniformity or can construct a formal outside object relative to hypotheses; a targeted valid certificate may change the value of a decision. | "Definition is cheaper than discovery, therefore any useful physical witness exists and is found faster." |

A testable cost condition is:

\[
K_{\Phi}+K_{\mathrm{capture}}+K_{\mathrm{select}}+
K_{\Psi}+K_{\mathrm{validation}}+K_{\mathrm{maintenance}}
<
C_{\mathrm{specified\ conventional\ route}}.
\tag{C1}
\]

All terms count the *full* relevant work and authorization costs; the right side is a declared competitor, not "a Turing machine" in the abstract. If the comparator already uses equivalent structural localization, event retention and local validation with matched evidence, it may tie or beat the proposed stack. Metastructural interpretation itself is an ordinary computable operation, and **is not costless**.

The descriptive/semantic opportunity is distinct: knowing the source's **boundary and relevance** can be more useful for a receiving local task than receiving a larger unqualified bag of object outputs, even if no additional object was identified. That result is task- and evidence-relative, not a claim of universally greater expressive computability.

## 10. Tests, countermodels, empirical programme and claim ledger

**Already source-supported distinctions, not new claims of external proof**

- SA-O2: a witnessed trace retained upstream of irreversible admission loss can distinguish later task states a reduced closed report merges.
- SA-O5: effective rule-state revision can change how bounded cases are interpreted without recreating an entire agent binary.
- SA-E2/SA-E3: task-specific certified existence/exclusion may change search decisions or prune work under explicit premises.
- SA-U10/Part I TR-19: same-info effective equivalence and no hypercomputability; open semantic obligation does not provide an oracle.
- Adopted 00M/topology: source-owned A/B/C/D meanings and explicit UNKNOWN remain unchanged.

**New proposed claims, currently HYPOTHESES / conditional constructions**

- H-LOC: observed architecture and constraints can identify worthwhile observation sites *without* a prior residual-frequency histogram.
- H-BRIDGE: some task-conditioned selected sites have a positive share of reachable, requalifiable local improvements among their actually received unresolved traces.
- H-COST: for a matched implementation and workload, focused structural qualification + selected reuse is cheaper than a specified global generator/reclassifier.
- H-GLOBAL: a locally verified intervention can satisfy a separately demonstrated larger ecosystem-level requirement without determining the global exterior.

**Mandatory countermodels and adversarial controls**

1. **No residual despite B exhaustion:** all new received requests are sound hard NO; \(p_?\!=0\). No "crowding" follows merely from few positives.
2. **High residual but no value:** the selected source has many timeouts caused by irrelevant overload, invalid data or missing authority.
3. **Absent or lost event:** a candidate never arrives, or is erased before admissible observation; no requalification from architecture alone.
4. **Confounding context:** identical input times out in a loaded sensor and succeeds in an idle copy; no immutable "timeout element" property.
5. **Ambiguous structural map:** the same bounded graph admits multiple possible hidden dependencies; localization must remain candidate/UNKNOWN.
6. **Hard legal/operational constraint:** home work may improve space capacity but fail authorization, safety or service continuity.
7. **Strong conventional parity:** a competitor with the same graph, traces, permissions, compute and selected rule returns the same result at equal/lower full cost.
8. **No global implication:** isolated local benefit does not remove a peak-capacity, traffic, dependency or governance bottleneck.
9. **Evidence-world mismatch:** an open or possible residual does not establish actual target-specific existence; a sound existence certificate does not guarantee cheap discovery.
10. **Low positives without B exhaustion:** distribution shift or deliberate rejection makes \(p_+\) small although there remains characterized exploitable reserve.

**Suggested staged tests (not executed by this annex):** small finite toy graph with two blind-spot sites and zero UNKNOWN-count priors; sensor monitor with controlled pre-admission loss; hard-reject/UNKNOWN adversarial distributions; telework peak-schedule constraint checker; fully-informed conventional monitor comparator; stress test with context shift and stale evidence; cost accounting under multiple throughput regimes; independent reviewer attempts to break H-LOC/H-BRIDGE/H-COST.

**Claim-status discipline.** Sections 5 and 6 contain straightforward conditional mathematical implications, **not** a proof that the real environment satisfies their premises. The probability, local gain, source availability and relative implementation costs are not measured here. Telework and sensor are explanatory **constructed fixtures**. No independent scientific referee has yet validated these new hypotheses, no external standard is changed, and no PR merge/owner promotion is requested.

### 12.1 Mathematical result: admissible-set expansion and task-relative determination

Let \(\mathcal U\) be a semantic domain for a particular interpretation; it need **not** be extensionally enumerated or computably decidable. Let \(H\) contain non-negotiable requirements for the current objective and \(R=\{r_1,\ldots,r_n\}\) its additional conjunctive admission gates. With unchanged objective and hard conditions,

\[
\mathcal F(H,R)=\{x\in\mathcal U:H(x)\land\bigwedge_{j=1}^{n}r_j(x)\}.
\]

For a legitimate relaxation consisting solely of removing gate \(r_k\),

\[
\boxed{\mathcal F(H,R)\subseteq \mathcal F(H,R\setminus\{r_k\})}.
\tag{CR-1}
\]

This is an elementary theorem of conjunctive admissibility. It is **not** a theorem that a useful real-world witness exists, that the optimizer will find it, that global inter-agent viability is maintained after other conditions change, or that the relaxation is authorized.

For an *already evaluable* referent \(x\), use the explicitly declared three-valued admission rule with \(T,F,?\), where conjunction is \(F\) if any required gate is \(F\), \(T\) if every gate is \(T\), and \(?\) otherwise:

\[
\left[\ \forall j\ne k,\ r_j(x)=T\quad\land\quad r_k(x)=?\ \right]
\Rightarrow
\left[
\operatorname{Admit}_{H,R}(x)=?
\quad\land\quad
\operatorname{Admit}_{H,R\setminus\{r_k\}}(x)=T
\right]
\tag{CR-2}
\]

provided \(H(x)=T\). The old \(r_k(x)=?\) **stays unknown**. The *decision* has become determined **under the changed set of conditions**; nothing has determined the old property or retrospectively converted the old model's UNKNOWN into YES. The same rule with \(r_k(x)=F\) changes a former rejection to admission only when removing that requirement is independently legitimate. If another retained condition is \(?\) or \(F\), the new conjunction is not automatically \(T\).

This gives a precise **relative-determination theorem**: uncertainty can cease to be *decision-relevant* by subtracting an unnecessary gate, rather than by discovering information that settles that gate. The objective/contract/version must accompany both statuses; silently replacing the old label destroys provenance and creates a false proof.

### 12.2 No smuggled population of “cases”: relax the admission rule before finding a witness

A harder case is **pre-admission non-recognition**. An exterior phenomenon may fail the old *grammar, type or question* of a proposal; the system cannot honestly call it a previously logged UNKNOWN item. If its evidence was never received, retained or reconstructible, CR-2 does not identify or recover it.

The metasyntactic inquiry can nevertheless operate on the *existing inferior machine's description*:

1. Identify the declared business objective, interfaces, local role, admission gates and dependency/authority architecture.
2. Determine which gate is a genuinely required invariant and which is a narrower **frame assumption** whose necessity is not established by the other hard requirements. Lack of derivation is **not** proof that the assumption is false.
3. Specify a **local rule-contraction proposal**: weaken/remove one justified gate, or broaden the admitted *question* (e.g. from “improve the physical office” to “improve working conditions”) without enumerating all possible solutions, ideas, objects or exterior participants.
4. Ask whether the existing process and its boundaries can now **admit and assess** some future, observed or legitimately generated signal under the revised objective conditions. A logical enlargement is not already an observed candidate, a guarantee of reachability or a grant of authority.

At theory level, for a consistent ordinary logical theory \(T=H\cup\{\alpha\}\), removal of \(\alpha\) yields the conditional inclusion

\[
\operatorname{Mod}(T)\subseteq\operatorname{Mod}(H).
\tag{CR-3}
\]

This enlarges the class of *possible interpretations* without a census of an exterior \(\mathcal U^+\). It neither entails a particular real-world object nor evades the need for an interpretable vocabulary, evidence and computable evaluation. If \(H\models\alpha\), removing its written occurrence does not actually change the model class. This is theory contraction / constraint relaxation on an ordinary effective representation, **not** a non-Turing oracle.

An explicit “indeterminate” label, timeout watchdog, diagnostic rule or additional predicate is *not inherently a new restricting axiom*: it may merely preserve a qualified status. Adding an admission gate can narrow \(\mathcal F\); reporting UNKNOWN does not necessarily do so. A new gate may also be helpful. The proposed contrast is between (i) solving every non-recognition by adding classifiers/filters and (ii) asking which **existing** gate makes that recognition necessary for the decision.

### 12.3 What the original exploitation machine contributes near its frontier

Keep §5's exact trivalent result, for a specified admitted traffic distribution: \(p_++p_-+p_?=1\); \(p_+\to0\) **only** implies \(p_-+p_?\to1\). It does **not** imply \(p_?\) rises, B is exhausted, C is reached or worthwhile witnesses accumulate. With a separate \(p_-\le h<1\), the bounded lower estimate \(p_?\ge1-h-p_+\) follows. Even then, useful relaxation candidates need an additional relevance/validity premise.

Under those conditions, the **existing exploitation processor** is an appropriate witness of its own observed determination boundary: as established positives become scarce, its remaining observed judgments distinguish qualified negatives from qualified unresolved outcomes. No universal exterior classifier is needed to *observe the qualified residuals it actually emits*. Its watchdog/interface may need to preserve pre-loss event references. Nor does “near C” imply a spatial metric: the relation is **architectural and objective-conditioned** until a metric and supporting result are established.

Crucially, **a qualified NO is not UNKNOWN**; a real budget/safety/legal impossibility remains a hard NO for that operation. The target for reconsideration is a gate found potentially nonessential *for the new positioned objective*, not an arbitrary rejection whose constraint is inconvenient.

### 12.4 Metasyntactic selection: identify the constraint and interface, not the hidden item

The earlier site selector \(\Phi(G_t,d,\kappa)\) (§4) can be refined as a **research proposal** to return a bounded *gate-position* rather than an imagined exterior population:

\[
\Phi_{\rm relax}(G_t,d,\kappa)
\longrightarrow
(\text{process/interface},\ r_k,\ \text{objective dependency},\
\text{hard/negotiable/unknown status},\
\text{reason for materiality},\
\text{evidence and authority path},\
\text{confidence}).
\tag{CR-4}
\]

The evidence inputs may include: a sourced architectural dependency graph, objective shortfall, verified saturation of B-side changes, the origin of admission rules, shared resource/use restrictions, observed \(+/-/?\) outcomes **where available**, and preserved boundary discrepancies. This operator does **not** receive an oracle list of all residual cases or a graph of unseen indeterminates.

A bottleneck or shared cross-dimensional gate can be worth testing because changing **one** obligation may affect several objective dependencies. But a merely nonredundant gate can be **inactive at the current optimum**; its removal enlarges the feasible set yet may not improve value. Some gates are jointly limiting, so no single relaxation is sufficient. The selector must preserve NO TARGET / INCOMPARABLE / UNRESOLVED when support is missing, and count the cost of architectural mapping and diagnostic inference.

### 12.5 Translate constraint relaxation into the **existing** agentic opportunity gradient

For participant \(i\), let \(\tau_k\) be a **candidate transition** to a role/contract position in which \(r_k\) is legitimately relaxed or made inapplicable under the same declared Objective Envelope. Do not introduce a new universal gradient symbol. The [source-owned Objective-Conditioned Agentic Gradient Law](../../architectural-contributions/ecosystem-positioning/01_OBJECTIVE_CONDITIONED_AGENTIC_GRADIENT_LAW.md) defines, for a justified scalar conditional model,

\[
G_i(\tau_k,t\mid\Delta_{\rm RA})
=
R_i(\text{current}\mid\delta_i)
-
\mathbb E[R_i(\text{after }\tau_k\mid\delta_i)].
\tag{CR-5}
\]

For declared normalized fulfilment \(V_i=1-R_i\), this is an expected fulfilment increase. It may express a reduction in objective shortfall, “frustration” or distance **only if those notions are explicitly defined in that profile and mapped to its risk functional**. For multidimensional or incommensurable outcomes, preserve the source's vector/Pareto/robust and tie-break semantics; do not invent a single number.

The **logical** best feasible value weakly improves after an authorized pure relaxation under CR-1 and identical hard conditions, but a **strict** better outcome requires a genuinely better admissible witness and evidence. The **estimated** gradient can be positive, zero, negative, bounded or UNRESOLVED once validation costs, inter-agent dependencies, risk and regime change are counted. For differentiable convex problems, binding-constraint dual sensitivity can assist estimation; it does not replace the existing regime/objective-conditioned gradient or prove a strict benefit in general.

The gradient is **opportunity, not permission**. Canonical [MSCA Operation & Repositioning](../../standards/minimum-sufficient-control/04_CANONICAL_MSCA_OPERATION_AND_REPOSITIONING.md) retains its established distinction:

\[
T_{\rm exec,i}\subseteq T_{\rm ACC,i}\subseteq T_{\rm reachable,i}.
\tag{CR-6}
\]

A restriction may be “hard” for the current **role-bound ACC** and still be *negotiable by its legitimate issuer*; a protected Objective Envelope, safety, legal, lineage or delegated-authority invariant is not waived by a favorable gradient. This research annex cannot grant that power to itself.

### 12.6 Full agentic sequence: qualified opportunity → signalling → consent → effective repositioning

This is a **specific candidate source of a transition** inside the already owned [canonical repositioning cycle](../../standards/minimum-sufficient-control/04_CANONICAL_MSCA_OPERATION_AND_REPOSITIONING.md#24-end-to-end-cycle), **not** a replacement protocol:

1. **Normal operation / qualified evidence.** Source agents continue exploitation; preserve bounded outcome, residual/provenance and actual event references when authorized. EA, Cartography and Regime Awareness maintain participant-local and regime-qualified A/B/C/D roles, not a universal external census.
2. **Structural positioning.** Map the material objective and constraints, distinguish hard NO from UNKNOWN, find where B-side exploitation is demonstrably inadequate, and select a possibly limiting rule/interface with \(\Phi_{\rm relax}\). Low positive rates alone are insufficient.
3. **Counterfactual relaxation and role target.** Form \(\tau_k\): a *proposed* less restrictive gate, qualified new admission question and corresponding candidate role/ACC position. For already evaluable referents, calculate a **hypothetical** \(?\to T\) requalification under the proposed contract version; pre-admission non-members remain unobserved until supported. Calculate the **existing** objective-risk gradient of \(\tau_k\) with confidence and unresolved effects.
4. **Governance gate before action.** MSCA's Type/posture/drift and Objective Envelope checks still precede candidate selection as specified. Apply ACC, lineage, current delegation and authority gates. Within-role/within-ACC changes can follow their delegated path; outside-authority candidates remain **opportunities or requests**. No autonomous bypass, silent objective pivot or global governance assumption.
5. **Communicate the qualified gradient and intention.** Where agreement/authority is needed, emit the canonical **RepositionIntent** carrying source evidence, candidate \(\tau_k\), relative \(?\to T\) claim, objective-risk basis, requested gate/role/ACC change, expiry, addressees, lineage/authority and confidence. This is a *proposed position*, not an already-effective repositioning.
6. **Receive legitimate response.** The existing [ACC/authority and signalling model](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/01J_ECOSYSTEM_SIGNALLING_SELECTIVE_DISCLOSURE_AND_CHOREOGRAPHED_REPOSITIONING_v0.1.md) permits APPROVE, REJECT, MODIFY, REQUEST_EVIDENCE, ESCALATE or expiry/no valid response. Approval is not inferred from silence. HOLD/UNRESOLVED preserves the status; rejection of authority is **not** evidence that the opportunity hypothesis was false.
7. **Re-contract and make the position effective.** Only after valid approval/issuer/version/lineage binding as required: produce a within-role adjustment, permitted same-lineage ACC successor, approved new binding, or legitimately issued new lineage/MSCA relation. Update bound/effective role, granted authority, dependencies and interface criteria. A valid new contract can make a formerly UNKNOWN **admission decision** determinate relative to that new contract, without falsifying its old status.
8. **Verify, emit and iterate.** Test delivered value, safety, objective adherence, conflicts with other participants and resource effects under bounded observation. Preserve failure/rollback authority, old/new provenance and unresolved parts. Emit the bounded post-transition signal; other agents independently update Cartography/RA and may reposition in subsequent cycles. Local beneficial admission is not automatically a global optimum, a collective agreement or a completed DDS Stage C proof.

The key timing is: **gradient/candidate communicated → legitimate accept/modify/reject → authorized re-contracting → effective repositioning → evidence and next-cycle signalling**. If the transition is already within currently delegated permission, its applicable local authorization gate still applies; an external acceptance message is not universally mandatory.

### 12.7 Telework and sensor revisited — the earlier-than-candidate path

**Telework:** The 2019 firm's original question “improve *our office*” may encode an *office-only* admission rule. The employer's genuine objective is adequate working conditions and capacity under budget/security/service/legal commitments. Architectural analysis of simultaneous desk-capacity, cost and travel constraints may select **mandatory physical presence / employer-owned workplace** as an important rule to investigate. At this stage the processor need not have named, enumerated or even accepted “telework” as a possible *office-improvement proposal*. Relaxing the **question's admission rule** broadens the class of possible interpretations. A later observed or justified practice of producing a legitimate work result without the old office requirement can then be locally qualified and, only after applicable employer/worker/IT/security/insurance authority, admitted under a revised contract. This **does not conjure telework from nothing** and requires no society-wide classification. The previous §7 path using already received requests is **one possible evidence route, not the necessary origin of this mechanism**. If work-from-outside is never observed, proposed or testably generated, opening the rule alone supplies no specific physical witness.

**Sensor:** The normal sensor's own finite-budget observed outcomes, and/or a watchdog at its interface, can expose *qualified* unresolved boundary observations without a second universal classifier. The metastructural map chooses **which sensor and which admission/test gate** deserve attention (for instance an unnecessarily strict decision threshold, an outdated frame assumption, or a time/coverage budget **if legitimately adjustable**). A local relaxation can render a particular downstream *decision* determinate under a revised rule if all retained safety/objective conditions are true. Extending timeout does **not** automatically decide an uncomputable property or guarantee an eventual result; an unreceived signal remains unreachable. A sensor that returns only hard NOs is a countermodel to inferred unknown concentration. The original exploitation process may continue normally while the allowed gate/contract is changed through authorized control.

### 12.8 Research propositions, countermodels and tests for PR review

- **CR-1 set inclusion:** proved under conjunctive restrictions and unchanged hard/objective definitions. Strict improvement requires an admissible better solution and no missing inter-agent hard condition.
- **CR-2 relative requalification:** proved for a *recognized/evaluable* referent under the declared \(T/F/?\) conjunctive decision; the discarded property remains unknown. Extend separately to schema/admission changes with explicit source and observation assumptions.
- **CR-3 theory-level opening:** removing a non-entailed axiom admits more interpretations without naming every exterior item; mathematical-model existence does **not** prove a physical or useful witness.
- **H-RELAX-LOC (OPEN):** sourced cross-dimensional architecture and B-reserve evidence can select a more useful *gate to relax* than indiscriminate residual capture or random relaxation, without the solution's identity or a global UNKNOWN histogram.
- **H-RELAX-GRAD (OPEN):** the source-owned local gradient can be qualified **before** acceptance and can forecast useful risk/shortfall reduction for selected relaxations, with explicit accuracy/confidence/cost limits.
- **H-RELAX-GOV (OPEN):** the complete authorized ACC/MSCA role transition can preserve all declared global hard constraints while enabling a previously blocked local result and a subsequent, independently verified larger objective effect.
- **Frontier-concentration hypothesis (OPEN):** exploitation of its B reserve may correlate with increasing relevant qualified unresolved observations **only** with separately stated task-distribution, hard-rejection, coverage, logging and retention premises. Do not infer positive \(p_?\), C reachability, a “bingo” or a high gradient solely from dwindling positive results.
- **Required countercases:** all nonpositives are sound hard NO; the selected rule is active but irrevocable; the rule is nonredundant yet slack at the objective optimum; the physical candidate never exists/arrives; an old input grammar cannot see it; multiple coupled constraints still block gain; timeouts reflect noise; a local ACC gain damages another owner's hard objective; signer/lineage denies permission; stale gradient is invalid after regime shift; an equally informed conventional adaptive Turing system ties or outperforms.
- **Proposed measured study, NOT performed here:** fix objective/constraints and comparable workloads; record the architecture/evidence **before** knowing any held-out “telework-like” solution; compare (i) baseline exploitation, (ii) add-classifier/filter exploration, (iii) indiscriminate UNKNOWN replay, (iv) structurally targeted rule relaxation with contract-gated requalification, (v) equally informed strong adaptive comparator. Measure gate-selection precision, new determinacies *by contract version*, verified local and whole-ecosystem outcomes, safety/authority violations, false escalations, full cost and context drift. A reviewer should attempt leakage checks: no solution identity, answer class or secret population may enter \(\Phi_{\rm relax}\).
