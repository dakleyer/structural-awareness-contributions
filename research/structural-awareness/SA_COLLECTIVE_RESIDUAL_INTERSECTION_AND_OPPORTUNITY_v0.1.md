## 2. SA-M1 — what the complement of a union actually represents

Let \(\Omega\) be a **common, independently interpreted mathematical universe of candidate descriptions**, not necessarily enumerated or physically observable. For N agents with presently admitted classes \(A_i=\{x:P_i(x)\}\), define their extensional admission complements \(R_i=\Omega\setminus A_i\). Then:

\[
\boxed{
R_{\mathrm{joint}}
=\Omega\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}A_i
=\bigcap_{i=1}^{N}R_i
=\{x:\bigwedge_{i=1}^{N}\neg P_i(x)\}.
}
\tag{3}
\]

**Proof:** De Morgan relative to the same \(\Omega\). If each agent's admission requires several subconditions, \(P_i=\bigwedge_jP_{ij}\), then the common residual is instead **conjunctive normal form**:

\[
R_{\mathrm{joint}}
=\bigcap_i\bigcup_j\{x:\neg P_{ij}(x)\}.
\tag{4}
\]

It is not generally the conjunction of **all** negated atomic predicates.

**Critical distinction:** \(\neg P_i(x)\) says **the described input was not accepted by agent i's current admission rule**. An agent returning UNKNOWN on a substantive property does **not** thereby prove the negation of that substantive property. The shared complement is a **characterization of the admission failures**, not a complete classification of the hidden physical exterior A′.

De Morgan alone does **not** prove \(R_{\mathrm{joint}}\ne\varnothing\), that an individual belongs to it, that its members are near-optimal, or that the N sources are independent. A large number of Boolean conditions can be redundant. The "hyper-characterization" claim is correct only in the **qualified, interpreted sense** of having several simultaneously valid predicates on an **actually received candidate**, not automatically more Shannon bits.

## 3. SA-M2 — why a useful intersection requires a second kind of information

Let \(Q_i(x)\) denote an additional, **source-qualified and task-relevant** proposition that agent i can legitimately report. The candidate region of interest is not the bare residual, but

\[
H_\kappa=
R_{\mathrm{joint}}\cap\bigcap_i\{x:Q_i(x)\}.
\tag{5}
\]

To conclude that these candidates improve the present solution, a valid source/cost model must prove:

\[
\boxed{
\forall x\in H_\kappa:\quad
u(x)\ge v_{\mathrm{current}}+g,\qquad g>0.
}
\tag{6}
\]

This implication is **additional mathematical content** that cannot be derived solely from (3). It can be a certified resource/utility inequality as in the ACC additive portfolio, or a conservative interval bound **when** the regularity/coverage/validity hypotheses of PR #45 actually hold. Without such evidence, the common blind spot might contain worthless, dangerous or irrelevant candidates.

To call H "near-optimal" rather than merely "better", one additionally needs an **independent upper bound** \(u^*_\kappa\le U^*_\kappa\); then

\[
0\le u^*_\kappa-u(x)\le U^*_\kappa-(v_{\mathrm{current}}+g).
\tag{7}
\]

An arbitrary "high probability of being close" does not follow from the number of labels. It requires a calibrated joint distribution or a transparent probabilistic arrival/quality model. This document uses **deterministic lower bounds**, not fabricated probabilities.

A statement that **H is nonempty** also needs its **own source-qualified existence evidence**: one can know that at least one qualifying project exists without receiving the project's identity or obtaining any guarantee of encounter. The finite synthetic test explicitly includes such an **anonymous issuer/referee**, and does not claim the original agents had a public certificate of the hidden project.

## 4. Three stages and an optional fourth

**Stage 1 — unchanged synthetic suboptimal delivery.** The original referee computes (1). The local agents operate only with the current SCALE contract and **four restrictive, different admission predicates**. The masked agents do not receive the original INTEGRATE identifier or future utility before its actual raw-input encounter. Their delivered utility remains 19. The referee can characterize the technical gap of 58, but the agents cannot claim to know that exact optimum just because the referee can score it.

**Stage 2 — shared meta-information without new reach.** Agents exchange descriptions of their own **not-admitted region**, validity/scoping, and conservative project-contribution conditions. The meta programme forms (3) and the conditional quality region (5). If a separately qualified anonymous source attests \(H_\kappa\ne\varnothing\) in the synthetic reference scenario, the agents can state **"a qualifying improvement of at least 48 exists"**, yet neither its **identity** nor the **time of first arrival** is provided. Their operative output remains **19**, with a declared **second-order blocked opportunity lower bound** 48. The actual technical gap G1 and this G2-like lower bound are **not summed**.

**Stage 3 — first encounter and recognition without replanning search.** An agent's **unchanged raw input stream** brings a candidate descriptor to a **pre-admission record**. Under the strict closure-only comparator, local syntax rejects the descriptor and the source information is permanently discarded. Under the preinstalled meta-qualifier, the candidate is tested against (3), the **independent positive quality predicates (5)** and all rights/contract gates. Only if every requirement passes is the candidate admitted to the **same additive portfolio decision/accounting logic**. There is no need to resense all the earlier inputs or recompile the object-level processor; the runtime **admissible rule/typed context** is updated. This is not a claim that arbitrary new sensor modalities or data types can be invented at runtime.

**Stage 4 — dissemination, conditionally.** The agent may publish a qualified *newly admitted category/example* after its result is validated, with source version, permitted disclosure, expiration and receiving interpreter requirements. Other agents can adopt the **new syntax or admissible type** only where their rights and own programme support the necessary interpretation. Broadcast does not grant universal capability.

**Second-order uncertainty** concerns a known or qualified **potential improvement which cannot yet be executed** because the required opportunity has not been encountered/authorized. In the didactic simulation, the time and first actor are unknown before the event sequence is presented. Calling this "frustration" is strictly an operational gap metaphor, never a psychological measurement.

## 5. SA-M3 — no unqualified theorem of eventual arrival

Neither the mathematics of an open universe, nor (3), (6), nor even a valid anonymous proof \(\exists x\in H_\kappa\), entails a finite input time at which some real agent **must** encounter that x. A simple countermodel places one qualified x outside every agent's actual accessible stream forever.

L1 OW-9 proves that under a **sound total three-valued evaluator and an effective exhaustive H-EXP traversal**, some finite encounter yields a substantive INDETERMINATE. It does **not** say that this particular opportunity class H (let alone a near-optimal candidate) will arrive. The uniform finite-horizon bound is absent.

For illustration only, if an independently justified stream model were genuinely **i.i.d. with per-trial probability p>0** of presenting an eligible H candidate, its first-arrival time \(\tau\) would satisfy

\[
\Pr(\tau>t)=(1-p)^t,\qquad
\mathbb E[\tau]=1/p.
\tag{9}
\]

This is **not estimated from the current 30×6 or the finite ACC scenario**, and is not invoked in the executable fixture. The fixture uses an **authored deterministic sequence** whose observed outcome proves a possibility, not a general arrival frequency.

## 6. SA-M4 — a conditional recognition/gain theorem

**Theorem.** Suppose all N agents' admission and meta predicates have a **compatible, typed interpretation**; a candidate x actually reaches at least one **authorized pre-admission capture** mechanism; the combined H predicate is **computable and valid** on that raw descriptor; a source-qualified lower-bound proof (6) is applicable; the original service/permission/cost/Pareto requirements are met; and the resident agent is already capable of processing newly admitted finite descriptors under that context. Then it can **recognize and act on x at runtime** with a guaranteed task-specific utility improvement at least g, without enumerating the exterior or recompiling a new object-level executable.

**Proof.** The pre-admission capture produces finite x; applying the computable admission failures and Q predicates establishes x∈H. The valid bound gives \(u(x)\ge v_{\rm current}+g\). The other gates certify an allowed transition. The existing evaluator executes it under the extended **input/admission state**. Nothing in this proof requires naming or examining any other x∈H. \(\square\)

This is **conditional strict opportunity gain against a specifically defined permanently discarding comparator**. A conventional agent with the same pre-admission capture, source credentials, rules and permissions can reproduce the identical result. De Morgan and meta labels do not create a different Turing degree or costless source.
