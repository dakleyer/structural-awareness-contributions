## Abstract

The central hypothesis is that **a correct, source-qualified proposition about a region that cannot yet be identified or determined exhaustively may be more relevant to a present question, and cheaper to obtain or verify, than further exact determinations within the current represented universe**. This is not the proposition that every unknown carries useful information, or that labeling the universe OPEN directly supplies a hidden fact.

We separate two mathematical stages. **Phase I — comparative metasyntactic value:** determine which originally available facts cease to be task-relevant under a changed context, and which *properties* of a still-unidentified exterior are necessary or decision-relevant. Establish the distinction between a metacertificate that only proves inadequacy, and a new qualified external source value that changes a target decision. We derive strict conditional Shannon advantage, a verifiable witness for insufficiency, a parameterized family with irrelevant internal observations and relevant exterior properties, and a separate verifier-work advantage. **Phase II — metasyntax-to-object-program requalification:** only after a certified insufficiency or relevance diagnosis, specify what new observable question, interpretation or operation is needed; select eligible additional information; and construct a new effective syntactic programme when and only when a sufficient, computable and authorized requalification is available. If none exists, retaining a qualified gap is a correct output, not a failed oracle.

The resulting research programme is **Structural Awareness** at the mathematical level: *determine the value and cost of boundaries of determination, then determine which changes of representation are justified by those boundaries*. No social-model experiments, mandatory B/C/D roles or Ecosystem Awareness requirement projections are used as premises.

## 1. The additional hypothesis is already in the foundational research, but at a different scope

The current [Integrated Foundation, §1.0](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/01_FOUNDATIONAL_THEORY_v0.5_INTEGRATED.md) and [FH-C/FH-E note](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/01A_A01_MISSION_DRIFT_CAUSAL_PRIORITY_AND_EPISTEMIC_ECONOMY_HYPOTHESES_v0.1_RESEARCH_NOTE.md) distinguish two propositions:

**FH-C (comparative causal priority):** for certain changes of mission, forgetting the limits of legitimate evidence may be a stronger cause of unjustified changes than lacking additional subject facts. The **relative causal effect** is an untested hypothesis, not proved by a two-world factorization example.

**FH-E (conditional computational economy):** for the *same mission-requalification question*, a minimum sufficient epistemic qualification may use less computation than broad subject-domain expansion. Its scope is explicitly **inference-time computational cost**, not unconditional net total-cost or design-cost superiority. It remains a comparative applied hypothesis even if this note proves existence of a narrow mathematical cost-separated family.

The foundations also distinguish two independent reasons to requalify: a process's **incomplete representation of a relevant open domain**, and a **change in the context that invalidates the former response mapping**. The present two-phase formulation **couples but does not identify** those reasons.

No mathematical content from the previous [Part II v0.2](./METASYNTACTIC_UTILITY_CLASS_THEOREMS_v0.1.md) is discarded: the conditional Shannon identity, R11 existential witnesses and local-fiber bound, R14 coverage/KL support qualification, R16 algorithmic description caveat, R12 reusable verification accounting, R03 relational distinctions and task-specific pairs, and the Semantic Window contextual sufficiency construction each play a separately typed role below.

## 2. The mathematical objects and the meanings of "more useful" and "cheaper"

Fix an admissible, possibly open source-state class \(W\). Let \(c\in\mathcal C\) denote a **declared context**, which may change the relevant question and operations. Let

\[
O=o(W),\qquad
R_c=r_c(W),\qquad
Z=z(W),\qquad
M=m(W)
\tag{1}
\]

be, respectively, **the currently available qualified object-level result**, the independently specified target, **additional internal object-level determinations within the admitted representation**, and a **typed and source-qualified metasyntactic proposition/observation** about its scope, unexplored relation, validity, source, proof status or external context. When Shannon information is used, these denote **random variables under an explicitly specified joint distribution**; when a logical model is used, they denote deterministic maps from W. Neither treatment assigns a prior over all of an unenumerated universal cosmos.

**Residual \(A′\)** denotes the **open semantic obligation not exhausted by the finite observation O**. It is *not* the complement of a closed list or a random variable consisting of all unknown individuals. A metalevel M may express a **true property of the residual** without individuating its members. A claim about a particular outside-domain kind or value requires an independent model/source warrant; universal model openness alone is not a fact about peers, sensors or communications.

For a fixed target \(R_c\) and receiving loss \(\ell_c\), say M has **greater task utility** than Z if the receiver's optimal attainable loss given \((O,M)\) is strictly smaller than that given \((O,Z)\) under the *same* declared action/information model. Under expected log-loss, the difference is exactly a comparison of conditional mutual information; under arbitrary loss, use task-specific Bayes or minimax risks, not raw Shannon bits.

Say M is **computationally cheaper** only under a typed resource ledger in which the **cost of source discovery, certificate generation, validation, access, transmission and use** is charged; compare it with Z under the same receiver task, privileges and response horizon. A short reported string does **not** prove a cheaper construction.

A metatheorem known in advance and constant on all worlds is a **structural assertion**; it may be valuable for what is expressible or what is certified, yet yield

\[
I(R_c;M\mid O)=0
\tag{2}
\]

when it carries no world-varying additional information. Do not erase this negative result.

## 3. Phase I — the source-representation relevance test

Define the observational equivalence \(w\equiv_O w'\iff o(w)=o(w')\) and the target equivalence \(w\equiv_{R_c}w'\iff r_c(w)=r_c(w')\). The standard [PR #60 F1](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/pull/60) and [Part II SA-U1](./METASYNTACTIC_UTILITY_CLASS_THEOREMS_v0.1.md#2-from-primalidad-y-lógica-to-a-task-relative-algebra-of-distinctions) imply:

\[
r_c=h_c\circ o
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\equiv_O\ \subseteq\ \equiv_{R_c}.
\tag{3}
\]

The **metasyntactic** question is not "give me every hidden element" but "does the old O still preserve the distinctions demanded by the new \(r_c\)?"

**Theorem SA-V1 (contextual relevance reversal).** Suppose O is adequate for an original \(r_{c_0}\), but under a new context \(c_1\) there are worlds \(w_0,w_1\) with the same O and distinct \(r_{c_1}\). Then the original O is **insufficient** for the new target, despite remaining a correct record of its previous task. A qualified witness pair \(\pi=(w_0,w_1,o(w_0),r_{c_1}(w_0),r_{c_1}(w_1))\) is a metasyntactic certificate of that insufficiency **without specifying which world is actual**.

**Proof.** The witness violates (3) for the new context. Its finite syntactic verification in a decidable finite model requires only checking its equality/difference claims; no full reconstruction of W is needed for validating that *counterexample*. The certificate does not determine the actual \(r_{c_1}(w)\). \(\square\)

A formerly useful **determined fact** may thus be irrelevant for the revised target, while an **unrepresented distinction** has become relevant. This is a **context-relative logical statement**, not a claim that an unidentified outside entity has somehow been physically acquired.

For a vector of determined object features \(O=(A_1,\ldots,A_k)\), an exact **task-relative irrelevance** test is: \(A_j\) is removable from the description for \(r_c\) when \(r_c\) factors through the projection that forgets \(A_j\). In a probabilistic model, \(I(R_c;A_j\mid A_{-j},M)=0\) is an **information condition**, not automatically a causal-intervention statement. Do not conflate feature dependence, semantic necessity and causation.

**Negative control:** if \(r_{c_1}\) still factors through O, the changed-context flag alone provides **no mathematical reason** to seek further distinctions or redesign an object programme.

## 4. Phase I — information from a residual property may strictly outrank more determined facts

**Theorem SA-V2 (relative Shannon dominance criterion).** Let \(R_c,O,Z,M\) be finite-valued random variables with a valid joint law, M an **admissibly obtained** metasyntactic observation and Z further exact object-level determinations. Under expected logarithmic loss:

\[
\boxed{
R_{\log}^*(O,Z)-R_{\log}^*(O,M)
=
I(R_c;M\mid O)-I(R_c;Z\mid O).
}
\tag{4}
\]

In particular, M provides strictly *more task-relevant information* than Z if and only if the right side is positive.

**Proof.** The best achievable log-loss predictor from any input T uses \(P(R_c\mid T)\), with risk \(H(R_c\mid T)\). Subtract risks for \((O,Z)\) and \((O,M)\) and apply \(H(R_c\mid O,Y)=H(R_c\mid O)-I(R_c;Y\mid O)\). \(\square\)

This criterion is classical Shannon theory; the mathematical contribution is to specify the **metasyntactic source and task meaning** of M versus "another determined item in A" Z. Information about a theoretically unidentified outside **class** is not automatically a realized sample of M.

**Theorem SA-V3 (nonempty infinite family with strict information and a cheaper qualified route).** For every \(n\ge3\), consider an admissible finite but arbitrarily large model

\[
W_n=\{(a_1,\ldots,a_n,b,t):a_i,b,t\in\{0,1\}\},
\tag{5}
\]

with independent uniform coordinates. The initializer's original object vocabulary U admits the \(a_i\) coordinates but **does not contain b or t**; these remain outside its initial interpreted scope. The original internal report is \(O=(a_1,a_2)\), further internal determinations are \(Z=(a_3,\ldots,a_n)\), and the initial context question is \(r_0=a_1\). A new contextual question is:

\[
r_1=a_2\oplus b.
\tag{6}
\]

The coordinate b is **outside U's original typing/coverage**, but a separately authorized source may provide a **qualified one-bit property record** \(M=b\) without disclosing the unrelated residual constituent t. The *existence* and *correctness* of this source are explicit premises, not a consequence of universe openness: if admitted, the reporting system has now learned this **property** but has **not identified the whole exterior**. Under this precisely declared law,

\[
\boxed{
I(R_1;Z\mid O)=0,\qquad
I(R_1;M\mid O)=1\text{ bit}.
}
\tag{7}
\]

Moreover, \(a_1\) was task-defining for \(r_0\) but is *irrelevant* for \(r_1\) given \(a_2,b\). The outside t remains fully unidentified:

\[
H(t\mid O,M)=1\text{ bit}.
\tag{8}
\]

**Proof.** Given \(a_2\), the independent uniform b makes \(r_1=a_2\oplus b\) uniform, and independent additional a-features in Z add no target information. Observing qualified b determines \(r_1\) from \(a_2\), giving a one-bit improvement. The independent t is still uniform after observing O and M. \(r_0=a_1\) whereas \(r_1\) does not depend on \(a_1\). \(\square\)

**Cheaper route is a separate, explicit assumption.** If the complete valid cost of sourcing and qualifying b is \(c_M\), and gathering the chosen additional Z features costs \(c_Z\), with \(0<c_M<c_Z\), the **more relevant** meta route is also **cheaper than the specified redundant object-expansion route**. Since \(n\) is unbounded and Z may contain \(n-2\) irrelevant coordinates, the family permits arbitrarily large differences **under the declared positive marginal internal-cost and bounded qualified-b-source premises**. No fact about the cost of a *real* source has been inferred merely from its one-bit message length.

**Same-target solution comparison.** Suppose another valid method reconstructs a complete hidden \((b,t)\) or broader context at a charge \(c_{\rm full}\), while the qualified b-source costs \(c_M<c_{\rm full}\). Both permit the correct \(r_1\), but the latter does not identify t and is cheaper **under the stipulated cost/source model**. A competent programme with identical access to the qualified b-source can adopt exactly the same shortcut and **tie**. There is no different Turing degree.

**Why this is not an oracle construction:** b is **provided by an independently specified legitimate source whose availability and cost are premises**, not inferred by a mere constant OPEN tag or invented from the known a-coordinates. If this source is absent, the metaprocess can still certify O's insufficiency from a valid model pair, but it **cannot compute the actual \(r_1\)**. The theorem is a parameterized mathematical existence result, not a guarantee every ecosystem exposes a cheap b.

The case formalizes two previously separate source directions: [R14](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/07-field-notes/REVIEW_R14_DISTORSION_ENTROPICA_v0.1.md) shows equal coverage can have different task information, and [R16](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/07-field-notes/REVIEW_R16_COMPUTATIONAL_EXERGY_v0.1.md) shows equal coding/exergy summaries can require different legitimate receiver outcomes. The mere number of known items never determines their **target-relative significance**.

## 5. Phase I — advantage without new Shannon information: a cheaper adequacy certificate

The structural metaproposition **"the original O is not sufficient for \(r_{c_1}\)"** is a property of the declared *observation/target mapping*, not necessarily a random variable which identifies hidden world state. A single proven witness pair \(\pi\) from SA-V1 settles that metaproposition for the model. This is useful for the **separate adequacy/reliance question**, even when \(\pi\) does not supply the actual \(r_{c_1}(w)\).

**Theorem SA-V4 (certificate versus exhaustive black-box discovery).** Let \(W=\{0,1\}^n\) and o be constant. Suppose the receiving question is **whether an arbitrary total Boolean black-box target function r is constant on W**, and the only way a fresh object-level analyzer obtains target values is by querying r at individual points. To certify *constancy* in the worst case, a deterministic analyzer needs to inspect **all \(2^n\) inputs**; otherwise an unseen point could disagree. On the other hand, a finite, valid **nonconstancy witness** \((w_0,w_1,r(w_0),r(w_1))\) with distinct answers can be **checked by two black-box value queries**, plus finite verification of the presented input encodings.

**Proof.** For constancy, if one input is unqueried after the analyzer has observed only zeros, define a second function which agrees on all queried inputs and is 1 at the unqueried point; the observations are identical while the truth of constancy differs. Hence the lower bound. For nonconstancy, two correctly checked distinct outputs suffice as a counterexample. \(\square\)

This is **a verification/discovery asymmetry**, not a theorem that finding a valid witness always takes only two queries. A source may have spent the full search to find it. That discovery work must be counted in a *total* cost comparison and could make the metalevel route unattractive on a single use.

The separate [R11 witness theorem](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/07-field-notes/REVIEW_R11_PRIMITIVA_ENTROPICA_v0.1.md) similarly distinguishes **finite verification of a positive witness** from the absence of a total negative answer in halting-complete c.e. sets. The [R12 compiled-source review](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-workbench/blob/main/02-workstreams/ecosystem-awareness/07-field-notes/REVIEW_R12_PRIMITIVA_ENTROPICA_COMPILED_TEX_v0.1.md) provides the precise reuse accounting.

**Theorem SA-V5 (amortized qualified proof advantage).** Suppose producing a source certificate costs \(C_s\) once, a recipient can authenticate/apply it at cost v, and independent fresh reconstruction would cost c per recipient. For k independent eligible receiving uses and total integration overhead h:

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm copy/reuse}=C_s+kv+h
\quad\text{versus}\quad
C_{\rm fresh}=kc,
}
\tag{9}
\]

so qualified reuse saves total work **if and only if**

\[
\boxed{k(c-v)>C_s+h.}
\tag{10}
\]

**Proof.** Subtract the two source-to-receiver work totals. \(\square\)

The *work reduction* need not coincide with an increment in Shannon information: the conclusion can be a deterministic mathematical fact already derivable from publicly available data, yet expensive to derive repeatedly. The relevant advantage is **bounded proof availability** or amortized design/verification, not newly observed world entropy. An equally competent conventional certified-proof cache is information/computation-equivalent for this task.

## 6. Phase II — from a qualified metaproposition to a different executable object syntax

Phase I can establish one or more of the following typed results:

\[
\operatorname{INSUFFICIENT}(o,r_{c_1},\pi),
\quad
\operatorname{IRRELEVANT}(a_j,r_{c_1},\mathrm{proof}),
\quad
\operatorname{REQUIRED\_DISTINCTION}(\pi,r_{c_1}),
\quad
\operatorname{OPEN\_OTHER}.
\tag{11}
\]

These results are **real metasyntactic outputs about the current programme**. They are not yet a solution of the new substantive task, and the witness \(\pi\) need not identify an actually present outside constituent.

Define \(Q_1,\ldots,Q_m\) as a **separately specified** finite menu of eligible supplementary observations, newly representable predicates, proof sources or interpretation operators; their computation/availability/authority are not inferred from (11).

**Theorem SA-V6 (conditional metasyntax-to-syntax requalification).** Fix a declared class W and changed target \(r_{c_1}\). If there exists an admissible, effectively evaluable finite subfamily \(J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}\) such that

\[
\bigl[o(w)=o(w')\ \land\
(\forall j\in J:\, Q_j(w)=Q_j(w'))\bigr]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
r_{c_1}(w)=r_{c_1}(w')
\tag{12}
\]

for all w,w′ in the **qualified interpretation domain**, then the new target factors through the enriched, executable input

\[
o_J(w)=\bigl(o(w),(Q_j(w))_{j\in J}\bigr).
\tag{13}
\]

Where a corresponding effective decoder h is supplied or constructed (for example from a **finite exhaustively characterized admissible model**), the object programme

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_{c_1}'=h\circ o_J
}
\tag{14}
\]

computes the new target correctly **on that declared domain**, subject to its authorization and resource qualifications.

**Proof.** Condition (12) is the standard factorization criterion for the enriched observations. Effective evaluations of o and Q_j, plus the stated effective decoder h, compose into an ordinary programme. The programme's truth guarantee is limited to the domain where the premises and source qualifications actually hold. \(\square\)

**Important logical limit:** the bare set-theoretic factorization of (12) does **not** prove existence of an effective h on an arbitrary infinite representation; the effective-decoder premise is independent and required. This is exactly why Part I did not claim an oracle.

**Minimality question from PR #60/PR #59.** On a declared finite admissible model, the queries J need separate only the **target-conflicting pairs still indistinguishable by o**:

\[
\mathcal U_{r_{c_1}\mid o}
=
\{\{w,w'\}:o(w)=o(w'),r_{c_1}(w)\ne r_{c_1}(w')\}.
\tag{15}
\]

The cheapest qualified J is a weighted set-cover problem only when the eligible source, costs and independence conventions actually satisfy the set-cover premises. If no valid query menu covers the pairs, the metalevel may diagnose and **request a different source, narrower task or justified abstention**, but cannot simply "resolve A′."

**Theorem SA-V7 (separation of two operations).** Phase I's correct **representation-insufficiency diagnosis** does not imply Phase II **successful reprogramming**. Phase II succeeds exactly under its additional effective-information and decoder premises for the requested exact task. These are logically distinct:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{MetaDiagnose}(o,r_{c_1})
\ \not\Longrightarrow\
\exists\,\mathcal P_{c_1}'\text{ meeting the full target}
}
\tag{16}
\]

without qualified information sources and effective reconstructibility.

**Proof.** Take any halting-complete target with a trivially insufficient constant o and no eligible new queries; inadequacy is provable, but an all-input total correct deciding programme does not exist (Part I / OW-1). In contrast, SA-V3 supplies a finite typed class where valid b is available and \(h(a_1,a_2,b)=a_2\oplus b\) is an explicit effective decoder. Thus diagnosis and successful reconstruction are separate, with positive examples under added premises. \(\square\)

**The programme difference is operationally meaningful:** Phase I may cause a *new* object-level input type, relation, admissibility check or context-specific response decoder to be synthesized in Phase II. Its implementation is ordinary computable syntax, and a different programmer can compile exactly the same code. Structural Awareness names **the operation of recognizing, justifying and translating the missing distinctions**, not a hardware power unavailable to classical computers.

## 7. Semantic Windows and context-relative reallocation of determination

The existing [Semantic Window source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PpwYraPHiFoOaat_ZeP2Pw5dk-d5y1Fg/view) assumes regime-labelled contexts and demonstrates a conditional bias caused by mixing distinct regimes. In [Part II §10](./METASYNTACTIC_UTILITY_CLASS_THEOREMS_v0.1.md#10-semantic-windows-a-context-is-part-of-the-meaning-not-just-a-fixed-amount-of-history), the correct tradeoff is not merely "always prefer fewer data":

\[
\mathrm{MSE}(\text{mixed})
=
\frac{\sigma^2}{n}+
\left(\frac{n_p}{n}\right)^2(\mu_p-\mu_c)^2
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\mathrm{MSE}(\text{pure})
=\frac{\sigma^2}{n_c}
\tag{17}
\]

under independent observations and common variance. A qualified context boundary makes the pure window preferable **only when its squared bias reduction exceeds the variance loss**.

That gives a mathematical example of **changing what counts as useful determined information** with context. Older, exactly known values may become **inappropriate for the current inference** when treated as undifferentiated observations, while a relatively small *boundary/label* can permit a more accurate interpretation, possibly without identifying every cause of a regime transition.

Together with SA-V1/SA-V6, the general ordering is:

\[
\text{context changes}
\ \to\
\text{former sufficiency challenged}
\ \to\
\text{metasyntactic boundary/relevance certificate}
\ \to\
\text{qualified new window or required query}
\ \to\
\text{effective recompiled syntax, if available}.
\tag{18}
\]

The source's proposed dynamic finder and viscosity contraction claims require their own hypotheses and are **not imported** as a universal metasyntactic solution. A new window need not be unique or efficiently discoverable in all real-world domains.

A new M value may instead reduce \(H(R_c\mid O)\) when an external qualified source actually supplies a relevant property. Reused proof material can reduce work even if M is deterministically recoverable from a fully computed object description. These must remain **three different types of advantage**.

Neither a finite count of unknown objects nor a compression ratio determines the computation cost of the required *meta-deduction*. Construction, source availability, compilation, source rights, time, revalidation and ongoing use must all be accounted for separately.

## 9. Scope, counterexamples and failed routes

**Counterexample C1 — constant OPEN.** Suppose M only says H-ND-UNIVERSAL, true in every admissible world and already known by the receiver. It can correctly preserve the open interpretation of results, but \(I(R_c;M\mid O)=0\); no strict Shannon superiority follows.

**Counterexample C2 — a known fact remains useful.** If the new target still factors through O, extra processing and context metadata may be unnecessary or merely add cost. Not every context shift invalidates a previously sufficient representation.

**Counterexample C3 — a useful question has no obtainable answer.** A certificate can prove \(\ker o\nsubseteq\ker r_c\), yet no authorized computable Q may separate the relevant states. Phase II cannot legitimately return the unknown substantive result.

**Counterexample C4 — every metacertificate has equal-cost conventional handling.** A conventional programme that carries the same source claims, proof objects and permitted operators can implement identical metasyntactic work using ordinary instructions. Strict superiority **over that equivalent architecture** does not follow from word choice or programme branding.

**Counterexample C5 — source cost dominates.** If qualified b-source acquisition/maintenance costs exceed the value or cost of a suitable ordinary source route, the metasyntactic path is not cheaper. In R12, reuse can lose once verification, integration and source-production costs are included.

**Counterexample C6 — invalid source or changed authority.** A true statement in a source's scope may be irrelevant to a new question, stale, unlicensed or corrupted. Prematurely promoting it to a receiver answer is exactly the unsupported-closure error that Part I sought to avoid.

**Counterexample C7 — irrelevant determination outside A.** A structurally open universe guarantees some exterior relative to a finite frame, **not that this exterior contains a distinction affecting \(r_c\)**. The useful-exterior premise is *stronger*, task-indexed and must be justified.

**Counterexample C8 — unbounded effective quotient.** Set-theoretic factorization does not entail a computable decoder on an arbitrary open W, and an added external context bit may not be accessible to the particular receiver. Phase I does not automatically grant Phase II success.

## 10. Correcting the residual terminology: existence is not the same as undecidability

The earlier term *indeterminate/open A′* referred to **what the current process has not exhausted**. It must not be reinterpreted as a universal assertion that every member of the residual is intrinsically undecidable or cannot be evaluated by another procedure. This is the exact conceptual repair sought by the [mathematical taxonomic review](./SA_METAEXISTENCE_CONTEXT_AND_EVALUABILITY_CALCULUS_v0.1.md), recorded as a [candidate observation in the open taxonomy PR #56](https://github.com/dakleyer/structural-awareness-contributions/pull/56#issuecomment-6441844401).

The adopted [00M §1 vocabulary](../ecosystem-awareness/baseline/00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md#abcd-canonical) already distinguishes characterized **assessable reserve B**, grounded uncharacterized exploration **C**, and effects beyond the current process's effective evaluation route **D**. These are **partial, context-relative relations**, not exhaustive sets of undecidable objects. We do not amend 00M in this Structural Awareness note.

The research extension distinguishes two generic, **not adopted**, mathematical-use hypotheses, separate from canonical **FH-C/FH-E**:

**SA-H∃ (existence-guided exploration).** Knowing through valid mathematics/source evidence that **a target-specific property** \(\exists x P_c(x)\) holds can justify exploration without naming the witness. Under a declared prior p, probability \(\alpha\) of a successful reachable discovery given existence, benefit V and exploration cost C,

\[
p\alpha V<C<\alpha V
\tag{19a}
\]

gives a strict change from NO-SEARCH without a certificate to SEARCH after a valid proof of target-specific existence. **Generic openness alone does not imply target-specific existence.** Source/certificate acquisition costs and search reachability are additional conditions.

**SA-H¬ (exclusion-guided context reduction).** A valid theorem \(\forall x\in S\setminus T:\neg P_c(x)\) permits a *task-preserving restriction* of a search:

\[
\exists x\in S:P_c(x)
\iff
\exists x\in T:P_c(x).
\tag{19b}
\]

The result does not require enumerating individually all excluded elements. It supports the **existence of computational savings** if verification/reuse of that theorem is cheaper than evaluating the excluded region, *not* the universal cheapness of discovering it.

**Constraint reversal under a fixed model \(\Omega\).** For two constraints \(\Gamma_2\Rightarrow\Gamma_1\), the admissible mathematical regions satisfy \(S_{\Gamma_2}\subseteq S_{\Gamma_1}\) and \(\Omega\setminus S_{\Gamma_1}\subseteq\Omega\setminus S_{\Gamma_2}\). A stronger restriction shrinks the mathematical admitted region and grows its exterior. But this extensional complement is **not identical to all epistemic questions of A′**, cannot be presumed enumerable, and cannot be substituted for canonical A as a delivered result.

**Modal boundary.** \(\exists xP_c(x)\) (proved in the actual declared model), \(\neg\exists x\in S P_c(x)\) (proved absent in a specified S), possible in **some** admissible world, impossible in **every** admissible world and unproved/unresolved status must not be collapsed. A *negative* certificate can be exceptionally helpful for changing context even when the exterior still contains many uncharacterized entities. No numerical confidence or Shannon measure follows from those labels alone.

**Phase alignment:** in Phase I, the metalevel may certify a target-specific property of an unresolved region, rather than characterize the entire region. In Phase II, under independently available sources, constraints and an effective decoder, that certificate may justify **expanding**, **contracting** or **recompiling** the object's search/decision syntax. Otherwise the meta-result remains a qualified statement without an executable new answer. The earlier SA-V1–SA-V7 conditional Shannon, proof reuse and effective-decoder premises remain intact.

Detailed formal proofs and limitations: [SA-E1–SA-E6](./SA_METAEXISTENCE_CONTEXT_AND_EVALUABILITY_CALCULUS_v0.1.md). No sociotechnical experiment, agent-population composition or EA requirements mapping is added to the present mathematical work.

## 11. New operational branch: rule-state revision without binary redesign

The earlier Phase-II proof [SA-V6](#6-phase-ii--from-a-qualified-metaproposition-to-a-different-executable-object-syntax) gives one **sufficient** way to produce a changed object-level decoder when effective qualified Q and h exist. It is **not** a necessity to recompile a binary whenever the context changes. The [SA-MATH-OP-01 paper](./SA_OPERATIONAL_RESIDUAL_AND_ONLINE_CONTEXT_PLASTICITY_v0.1.md) separates an alternative, **already programmed online plasticity** route:

- A typed **pre-admission record** of an observed but not substantively classifiable event can be retained before a lossy closure stage, when finite capture and source access are genuinely available.
- A resident metalevel rule interpreter can accept an authorized new certificate and revise the **parameterized context, guard or interpretation** of that existing record during execution.
- A **negative implication certificate** \(G(x)\Rightarrow D_{10}(x)=\mathrm{REJECT}\) can yield the *same* existing decision without running all ten ordinary checks on G-positive cases; its actual cost improvement requires the priced inequality from SA-O4.
- Under L1 OW-9, an encountered finite UNKNOWN is guaranteed only for an effective **exhaustive H-EXP traversal and a sound total three-valued evaluator** with record retention. There is no universal finite run bound or default physical exterior-object identification.

This is **Phase-II reconfiguration of an already effective controller**, not a new oracle nor proof that every system can invent arbitrary new semantic types. It should not be conflated with Phase-I proof of the existence of a meaningful frontier. Closed-world syntactic rule engines can implement exactly the same certified prefilter when given the same source proof and runtime capability; the **open-world distinction** is the refusal to declare a permanently discarded event irrelevant solely because its current admission schema could not substantively classify it.

**Research status:** this branch proves the mathematical possibility of live reinterpretation and a conditional savings criterion, not general automatic meta-rule discovery or superiority of all metasyntactic controllers. It preserves every previous SA-V1–SA-V7 proof and the unmerged PR #60/PR #59 source boundary.
