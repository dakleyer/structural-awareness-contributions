## 1. De Morgan and the shared blind spot

Fix a **common, declared mathematical candidate domain** \(\Omega\), and n locally admitted regions \(S_i\subseteq\Omega\). Define extensional relative complements \(R_i=\Omega\setminus S_i\). Their intersection is

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm joint}=\bigcap_{i=1}^{n}R_i
=\Omega\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}S_i .
}
\tag{1}
\]

This represents candidates admitted by **none** of the original object classifiers. The union \(\bigcup_i R_i=\Omega\setminus\bigcap_iS_i\) is a **different region**, excluded by at least one classifier.

**A necessary correction:** if \(S_i=\bigcap_j P_{ij}\), then

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm joint}
=\bigcap_i\bigcup_j(\Omega\setminus P_{ij}).
}
\tag{2}
\]

One outside-\(S_i\) label establishes **a disjunction of failed local predicates**, not that every \(P_{ij}\) failed. Four unqualified UNKNOWN messages do **not** prove four independent properties or a near-optimal region.

To obtain the proposed **hyper-characterization**, require legitimate, **candidate-linked**, source-qualified meta-predicates \(H_i\) whose truth implies exclusion from the corresponding object schema: \(H_i(x)\Rightarrow x\in R_i\). Only then

\[
\boxed{
H(x):=\bigwedge_iH_i(x)
\ \Longrightarrow\
x\in R_{\rm joint}.
}
\tag{3}
\]

The conjunction legitimately retains the distinct affirmed predicates, even if no original object agent admits x. It is not a complete enumeration of the epistemic A′. A candidate identifier, common version/context, source validity and proof scope are essential. Logical conjunction does not require independence of source probabilities, but duplicated sources cannot fake missing dimension evidence.

## 2. From a joint residual property to a near-optimality proof

A detailed intersection need not contain a good solution. An **additional warranted evaluation relation** is necessary.

For the explicitly declared separable example, let \(\Omega=[0,1]^n\) and \(u(x)=\sum_i x_i\), giving technical maximum \(u^*=n\). Suppose each source correctly bounds a **single concrete arriving candidate** by \(x_i\in[\ell_i,h_i]\). Then

\[
\boxed{
u(x)\ge L(x):=\sum_i\ell_i,\qquad
u^*-u(x)\le n-L(x).
}
\tag{4}
\]

**CROSS-1 (qualified joint certificate).** If each \(\ell_i\ge\tau_i\), and every \(H_i(x)\equiv(\ell_i\ge\tau_i)\) is valid within one declared task/version, the candidate is certified in \(\bigcap_iH_i\). If \(L(x)>u_{\rm current}+c_{\rm adoption}\), the candidate offers a **provable positive net improvement** under this objective, **provided action is feasible and authorized**. If \(n-L(x)\le\varepsilon\), it is also **certifiably epsilon-near the technical optimum**.

**Proof:** combine the n coordinate inequalities and the given utility upper bound. The improvement and regret inequalities follow by subtraction. \(\square\)

This is a **deterministic** claim, not a calibrated probability. In the actual ACC economic model, the objective, resource conservation, rights, service/consent, reachable transitions and Pareto conditions require their own valid comparison; a sum of four normalized attributes is **only an illustrative mathematical decision target**. A material unassessed dependency invalidates unqualified global adoption even if (4) establishes a local bound.

## 3. Nonempty mathematical region, observed candidate, and reachable action are separate

The region \(\bigcap_i\{x_i\ge\tau_i\}\) in the **declared** cube is nonempty when all \(\tau_i\le1\); point \((1,\ldots,1)\) witnesses nonemptiness in the model. But mathematical nonemptiness **does not ensure physical arrival** or implementation feasibility. The [L1 OW-9](./OPEN_WORLD_AGENTIC_INTERACTION_INDETERMINACY_L1_EXTENSION_v0.1.md) theorem guarantees an encountered INDETERMINATE for a total sound evaluator under exhaustive H-EXP traversal. It does **not** guarantee that a near-optimal candidate in \(R_{\rm joint}\) appears in any arbitrary stream or at any fixed time.

Only an independently declared arrival model can provide a waiting-time result. For i.i.d. arrivals with qualified per-arrival chance \(p>0\) of falling in the target region,

\[
\Pr(T\le N)=1-(1-p)^N,\quad \mathbb E[T]=1/p .
\tag{5}
\]

Even then no finite N guarantees success; p is **not** supplied by general open-world semantics. Also, without trusted actual-world source data, high-value predicates cannot be attributed to a specific arriving object.

## Conclusion

This closes a **conditional mathematical link** between the universe-given admission loss and a potentially actionable open residual: **an encountered object can be unclassifiable by every local operative syntax while remaining the subject of several valid meta-properties**. A source-qualified conjunction plus independent objective bounds may recognize it as a near-optimal opportunity; a resident effective meta-controller may then act *without a new binary*, while agents with only irreversible object-level admission cannot recover the lost event from their own outputs.

This is a **source- and capability-relative theoretical mechanism**, not a universal inevitability of near-optimal emergence from openness.
