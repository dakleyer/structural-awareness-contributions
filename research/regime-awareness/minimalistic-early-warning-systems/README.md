# Minimalistic Regime-Aware Early Warning Systems — corpus index

The EWS line asks a narrower question than forecasting: given a declared observation map and finite history, does present observable behaviour remain compatible with its recent regime, and can a **bounded, authorized response** be justified while the response window is still useful? The hard boundary is often contextual: which observations, historical extent, resolution and transformation preserve the regime distinction without requiring the whole world as input?

This folder is the **public reading index for the EWS anchor line**. The articles remain at their canonical Tegrity.AI publication URLs; this index does not claim to mirror, amend or independently validate them.

## Main documents and their roles

1. [Minimalistic Regime-Aware Early Warning Systems — anchor article](https://tegrity.ai/minimalistic-regime-aware-early-warning-systems/): definition and engineering interpretation of the “Sufficiently Good” detector, representation/detection/action contracts, contextual sufficiency boundary, ten strong-class requirements, conditional constructive schema, toy witness and explicit empirical limits.
2. [Full parent working paper](https://tegrity.ai/1379-2/): existence, realizability, operational maximality, safety governance and contextual-boundary argument. It separates definitions and conditional theorems from the toy witness and untested domain claims.
3. [Regime Awareness Capability — field-case reconstruction](https://tegrity.ai/evolution-of-regime-awareness-capability/): historical engineering provenance across four field cases; not independent proof of this detector class.
4. [Regime-Aware Semantic Windows — mathematical assessment proposal](https://tegrity.ai/regime-aware-semantic-windows-mathematical-validation/): fenced H1 alerting/safety-governor, H2 prediction-add-on and H3 compute-efficiency hypotheses, baseline and leakage safeguards; proposed preliminary assessment, not completed validation.
5. [Regime Change Detection / preliminary QAVA–UV review note](../regime-change-qava-uv.md): quantitative objects, proposed comparator design, negative findings and institutionally bounded review status.
