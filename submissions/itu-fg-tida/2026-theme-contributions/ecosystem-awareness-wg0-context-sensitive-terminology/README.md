## Scope

The proposed contribution asks WG0 to review T1-T9 against the filed candidate baselines and relevant Theme terminology, place agreed entries in D1, preserve legitimate contextual variants in D2, and link the contribution to UC20 and UC21 in D3. It does not rewrite other glossaries, treat D2 as a holding area for unresolved wording, create a universal INDETERMINATE verdict or change the status of UC20 and UC21.
