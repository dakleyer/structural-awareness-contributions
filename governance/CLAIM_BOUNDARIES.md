# Claim and Attribution Boundaries

> **Working control note — use these boundaries in repository text, issues, presentations and external references**

## Current roles and limits

| Entity or route | Defensible description | Do not infer or claim |
|---|---|---|
| **The Integral Management Society** | Institutional host of the Tegrity.AI working-circle context | Validation of work produced by related entities solely because it hosts the circle |
| **Tegrity.AI** | Public theoretical, publication and working-group context for Structural and Regime Awareness | Separate legal personality, funded research beneficiary, standards body or independent validator of related-party work |
| **OÜ JUBAP** | Owner and applicant for the Cost of Clarity RUP applied-research line | EIS funding, approval or validation before written confirmation |
| **JUBAP.Net engineering lineage** | Source of historical systems, architectural mechanisms and field cases | Proof of a universal, transferable regime-awareness framework |
| **EIS Estonia / RUP** | Programme authority and assessment route for Cost of Clarity | Funding award, programme acceptance, technical validation or endorsement |
| **TalTech Information Systems Group** | Prospective supporting R&D capability identified for intended contracted work | Confirmed supporter, partner, subcontractor or validator without an agreed scope and contract |
| **TalTech Next Gen Digital State Research Group** | Prospective supporting R&D capability identified for intended contracted work | Confirmed supporter, partner, subcontractor or validator without an agreed scope and contract |
| **QAVA — Universitat de València** | Preliminary quantitative and methodological review route for the Regime Change Detection subline | Institutional validation, endorsement, co-authorship, formal partnership or agreed work package |
| **ITU Focus Groups** | Standards-oriented discussion and contribution routes relevant to selected workstreams | Adoption, endorsement, Recommendation status or representation of ITU without explicit evidence |
| **FG-TIDA GitHub discussions** | Open theme-development forum in which related concepts may be discussed | Working Group status, accepted deliverable, chair role or adopted theme merely because a comment or issue exists |

## General evidence rules

1. A sent document proves transmission, not acceptance.
2. A positive or interested reply proves interest, not partnership.
3. A review proves that material was examined only to the extent expressly evidenced; it does not automatically prove correctness.
4. Field implementation proves engineering provenance within its context, not domain-agnostic transferability.
5. Shared personnel or institutional relationships do not collapse separate legal entities, projects, evidence sets or funding routes.
6. Public conceptual alignment does not transfer validation between Cost of Clarity, Regime Awareness, Regime Change Detection and Minimum Sufficient Control.

## Publication gate

Repository visibility, licensing, external issue comments, pull requests and statements of institutional status require a fresh evidence check and Ivan's explicit approval.
