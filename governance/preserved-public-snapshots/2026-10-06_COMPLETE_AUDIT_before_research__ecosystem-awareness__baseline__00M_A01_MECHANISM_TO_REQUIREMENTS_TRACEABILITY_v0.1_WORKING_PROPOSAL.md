# 00M-A01 — Mechanism-to-Requirements Traceability — Working Proposal

> **Status:** v0.1 Working Proposal · 2 October 2026.  
> **Control rule:** this document is a traceability aid only. It does not amend [00M v0.8](./00M_ABCD_AND_MATHEMATICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.8_RESEARCH_NOTE.md), [00N v0.7](./00N_FROM_MECHANISM_TO_REQUIREMENTS_SCIENTIFIC_PLAUSIBILITY_v0.7_RESEARCH_NOTE.md), or the [Canonical Requirements](./00_CANONICAL_REQUIREMENTS_CHALLENGES_SUFFICIENCY_HYPOTHESES_KPIS.md). It creates no S15, T5, H7 or new KPI family.

## 1. Purpose

00M asks whether four bounded operations used by Ecosystem Awareness / Ecosystem Positioning have defensible mathematical representations:

1. recognize A/B/C/D consistently relative to a declared process and frame;
2. relate scopes of different actors through justified partial correspondences;
3. compose task-relevant qualified summaries while preserving material distinctions;
4. requalify with direction when the grounds or context of a position change.

00N asks the next question: whether useful profiles of those operations could contribute to the functions required by EA. The canonical requirements remain the governing specification and falsification basis.

The intended reading chain is therefore:

`00M mechanism → 00N functional-plausibility bridge → S1–S14 / T1–T4 / H1–H6 / KPI-falsification`.

This is a directional trace, not an equivalence. A mathematically plausible operation does not by itself satisfy a requirement.

## 2. Compact traceability

| 00M operation | EA function exposed by 00N | Primary canonical requirement routes | Sufficiency conditions | Hypotheses most directly exercised | Important non-claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Recognize A/B/C/D consistently** | Preserve what a producer established, what remains assessable, what requires exploration and what lies beyond effective evaluation for the declared frame. | **S5, S14**; supports S3/S10 when applicability changes. | **T1, T2**; T4 where the classification must remain usable in time. | **H1, H2**; H5 when freshness/change alters the role. | Classification does not prove source truth, completeness of C/D, authority or action safety. |
| **M2 — Relate different actors' scopes** | Decide whether qualified declarations can legitimately be interpreted against the same receiving question without silently substituting one domain for another. | **S9, S11, S14**; supports S6 where bounded disclosure is used. | **T2, T4**. | **H2, H3, H4**. | Shared terminology, location, technology or MSCA does not itself establish semantic correspondence. |
| **M3 — Compose qualified summaries** | Preserve scope, provenance, dependence, validity, unresolved state and non-fungible distinctions needed by the receiving review. | **S6, S9, S11, S14**; supports S12/S13 where history and correction must survive. | **T2, T4**. | **H3, H4**; H2 for local-to-global inflation. | Aggregation does not create independence, missing evidence, authority, precedence or a global truth state. |

## 3. Requirement-family reading

The mapping above is intentionally many-to-many. The four 00M operations are mechanism components, while S1–S14 are observable specification obligations.

- **S3 / S10** primarily need M4, but M1 is required to preserve the old and new basis and M2/M3 may be required when the change crosses participants.
- **S5 / S14** primarily need M1 and M4, while M3 matters when unresolved state is inherited or composed.
- **S9 / S11** primarily need M2 and M3. M1 prevents a locally qualified result from being silently promoted into a different domain.
- **S6** depends on M3 only where a bounded summary genuinely preserves what the receiving question needs; small payload size alone is not evidence of privacy or sufficiency.
- **S12 / S13** use M3/M4 to keep corrections, current determination and historical provenance distinct.
- **S1, S2, S4, S7 and S8** supply or constrain state that EA may qualify. The mechanism does not replace their legitimate semantic owners.

## 4. Sufficiency reading

00N already states the strongest direct support as bounded parts of **T1** and **T2**. This trace keeps that limit.

- **T1:** M1 + M2 can expose a material break only when the relevant distinction is observable under the declared evidence conditions.
- **T2:** M2 + M3 must preserve the material qualifiers and receiving ownership needed to interpret the result.
- **T3:** M4 may identify the need or candidate direction for response, but authorized action remains a separate obligation.
- **T4:** M3 + M4 make bounded, selective requalification conceivable; total burden, response margin and useful stopping remain empirical obligations.

## 5. Hypothesis reading

- **H1** tests whether M1 preserves insufficient determination rather than forcing binary closure.
- **H2** tests whether M1/M2 preserve residual scope instead of inflating a local result into global confidence.
- **H3** tests whether M2/M3 avoid hidden residual and false confidence from lossy composition and correlated evidence.
- **H4** tests whether M3 preserves enough qualified context without requiring full internal-state disclosure.
- **H5** tests whether M4 detects loss of applicability under freshness and dependency churn.
- **H6** tests whether M4, together with the window-selection function, improves the risk/resource trade-off relative to fixed observation strategies.

## 6. What this trace does not establish

This working proposal does not show that:

- every requirement is fulfilled by the four operations;
- every real-world scope mapping is computable or correct;
- the 00M abstractions are sufficient for every EA profile;
- the interface implementation preserves the required information;
- requalification is always timely or economically beneficial;
- authorized action follows from an epistemic result.

Those remain specification, implementation and validation questions under the existing corpus.
