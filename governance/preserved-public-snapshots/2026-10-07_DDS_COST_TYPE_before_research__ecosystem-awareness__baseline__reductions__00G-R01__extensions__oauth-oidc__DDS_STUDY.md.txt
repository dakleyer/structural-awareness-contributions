## Executive finding

Authentic identity and legitimate resource permission are separate requirements. In this local profile a correctly scoped access token plus current application checks can produce one admitted digital operation. An admitted identity-only M is useful for its stated task but does not solve resource delivery. Later token use and source/issuer meaning require explicit contracts.

The [extension](./EXTENSION.md) supplies the base, Stage1 map/transfer limits and Stage2 mechanism/composition review. This report consumes one DDS and makes no new service promise.

## Cost/Risk/Effectiveness and business limits

**Cost:** actual signature-verification, application-policy and operational SQL counts. Two ephemeral key pairs and eleven synthetic tokens are provisioned; setup is not individually priced/timed. Enrollment, issuer operations, rotation, revocation distribution, retries, transport, clock administration, source governance, human consent and maintenance are unscored. Counts are not currency or R01's cost law; omitted work is not free.

**Risk:** selected wrong/unauthorized/expired/duplicate effect predicates. No qualified P occurs in this finite battery; this does not establish zero deployment risk. Source authenticity/custody and unsupported paths remain residual dependencies.

**Effectiveness:** local admitted write/identity return versus Ø, with intended purpose preserved. Real user/login completion, native API success, overall availability and representative population E are unmeasured.

**Business Value:** conditional identity provenance plus scoped legitimate resource admission and duplicate suppression. It cannot establish issuer truth, usable human capacity, universal delegation or financial ROI. Grant revocation can properly prevent a write while still losing business completion; safety and delivery are distinct.
